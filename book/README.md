# Book: Part VI ("The study") only

Only the part of *From Fields to Quirks* that the publication plan
(`publication_plan/publication_plan.tex`) relies on is tracked here.

- `partVI_the_study.pdf` holds chapters 25-29 and the bibliography, cut from the full build of 2026-11-07
  (pp. 235-295 and 319-330). Page, table and equation numbers match the full book, so "Table 29.2" in the plan refers to it.
  Links into the other chapters are dead.
- `chapters/ch25-ch29_*.tex` are the LaTeX sources. They do not compile on their own.
- `checks/ch25-ch29_*` are the sourced numbers (`*_numbers.csv`) and the check scripts (`*_checks.py`).
- `review/UNRESOLVED.md` lists the known paper errors that the plan says to fix.

The rest of the book workspace (chapters 0-24, figures, reviews, build outputs) is not in the repository.
