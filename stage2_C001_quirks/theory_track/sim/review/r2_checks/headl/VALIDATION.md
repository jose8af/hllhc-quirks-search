What the table is (VERIFIED, probe t15, two MG configurations vs the analytic (132.507/127.9)² = 1.0733):
- (b)/Gμ = 1.069-1.077 is the pure α rescaling. It checks that the LO used in σ_norm (probe t16, variant (b)) is the
  α(M_Z) = 1/127.9, s_W² = 0.2222 convention of TH App. A, i.e. that our LO × K_WG reproduces the TH table (§1.2).
- A consistent α(M_Z) scheme (a) adds −0.3…+2.5 % through s_W² in the γ-Z interference, most at 200 GeV.

What it is not:
- σ_norm = K_WG × LO(α(M_Z)) with K_WG = σ_WG / LO_WG(α(M_Z)) (TH §2.3). The α² of the LO cancels in this product, so σ_norm
  inherits the EW scheme of the WG Resummino tables (NLO EW conventions UNVERIFIED), not that of our LO.
- The WG reference is the right-handed selectron, the same (1,1)₋₁ hypercharge-only state as E, so the s_W² dependence of
  variant (a) cancels in the K transfer as well.
- The +6.9…+9.8 % spread between LO schemes is therefore **not** the uncertainty of σ_norm. The first draft's statement
  that it "justifies TH §2.4's ±7 %" was a mis-citation: TH §2.4 and §5 recommend **±5 %** (ESTIMATE) for "scalar → fermion K
  transfer and EW input scheme" together; 7 % is TH's α² ratio, not the nuisance.

Nuisance to carry on σ_norm: TH's ±5 % (K transfer + scheme, ESTIMATE), plus the residual scheme uncertainty of the WG tables
themselves (UNVERIFIED). Shapes are generated in Gμ; this is irrelevant for shapes, because the coupling ratio g_Z/g_A is fixed.

- kT-MLM matches a parton to a jet when min(pT²)·ΔR² < qCut², so a hard shower emission close in angle to a soft ME jet

  weights are 4-11× those of the TRG rows (3.7-6.6× at the benchmarks, 6-11× on the fine grid before the 300 GeV top-up;
  column ⟨w_INC⟩/⟨w_TRG⟩ in SAMPLES.md). The first draft's "0.4-2 %" counted events, not σ.
- **Row order and weights (S2-R1, A1).** The first draft wrote all TRG rows first and the INC complement at the end, so the
  documented "take the first N" pattern silently returned the raw ME-cut sample for N up to 7.5k-17k tail events. The rows
  are now shuffled with a recorded seed (`shuffle_seed`, `row_src`), and the loader always returns the per-event weight
  (`info['w']`) and offers a deterministic hit-or-miss unweighting (`iter_events(..., unweight=True)`, efficiency 16-28 % on
  TRGS). Regression check in `val_loader.py` (§6): the INC share of w and the weighted median of ℓ_max in the first
  500/2000/5000 events agree with the full file (|pull| < 3) at all four tested points.
- The loader alias `trig` points to TRGS. `trig_mecut` keeps the raw TRG.
- The generation cut itself closes (criterion: max(2 %, 2σ)). **PASS.** The criterion "migration ≤ 1 %" **FAILED**;
  stitching is the remedy, not lowering the cut (an 80 GeV cut would still miss ≈ 3-5 %).

## 7. Sanity checks (V6) (VERIFIED unless stated)
- **Final-state quirks.** Exactly 2 status-1 quirks in every converted event (the converter asserts it). Their history
  codes are 23 → 44/52; there are no decay products.
- **Production vertex.** At the origin.
- **Momentum balance.** |Σ pT(all final-state particles including ν and quirks)| has a median of 5 × 10⁻⁹ GeV.
- **QED FSR off the quirks (plan D12: "keep, physical"): NOT in the samples (diagnosed in S2-R1, B-9 / C-7).**
  - Observation: m(QQ̄) after the shower equals the LHE m(QQ̄) for every event (M_hard − M_shower: median 0, fraction with
    ΔM > 0.1 GeV ≤ 1.5 × 10⁻⁴; critical review R13).
  - Cause: in Pythia 8.313, `TimeShower:QEDshowerByOther` (default on) "allow[s] charged **resonances** to radiate photons"
