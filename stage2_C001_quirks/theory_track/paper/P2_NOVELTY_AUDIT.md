# P2 novelty audit and comparison with published central-detector quirk reach (C001 theory track)

Investigator, 2026-10-16. Trigger: paper/review/REVIEW_PAPER_R2.md finding A-3. Status: COMPLETE.

**Bottom line.** (1) The intro misdescribes the prior work: both studies simulate a ⟨µ⟩ = 50 PU overlay. (2) There is no
genuine tension: at B = 0 our detector-level ε gives a reach of the same order as Knapen et al. (≈ 0.6 TeV at
300 fb⁻¹, ≈ 1 TeV at 3 ab⁻¹, ESTIMATE), so the gap is our simulation-statistics-limited background bounds (≈ 10⁵–10⁷
events against a break-even ≈ 10²–10³). (3) **A new Category-A error** (§2.0): N_exp omits the p_T(QQ̄) > 200 GeV tail
fraction f (0.05–0.17), so every published μ₉₅/N_exp is 6–21× too optimistic (best 37.6 → ≈ 7.8 × 10², ESTIMATE).

## 1. Prior central-detector quirk-track studies (what each one actually did)

### 1.1 Knapen, Lou, Papucci, Setford, arXiv:1708.02243, PRD 96 (2017) 115015 [`Knapen:2017kly`]

All items VERIFIED in the PDF.
- **Model.**
  - Vector-like fermion quirks in (1,1)_1 (EW, Drell–Yan) and (3,1)_{2/3} (coloured) (p. 2).
  - Fig. 6 is labelled "(1,1)1, N_Q = 2" (p. 9). This is the same SM representation and N_IC as our E, with the opposite
    sign convention for Y.
  - No scalar.
- **Λ range.** Fig. 6 spans Λ ≈ 10 eV – 30 keV. The abstract claims "discovery potential for string tension between
  100 eV and 30 keV" (p. 1).
- **Detector.** Simplified ATLAS inner detector:
  - B = 2 T, uniform; barrel pixel + SCT only, |η| ≲ 1.8, 8 layers;
  - the hit position is the pixel centre, the SCT resolution is 2× intrinsic, and neighbouring-pixel hits are merged;
  - z is smeared by 45 mm;
  - the quirk does not interact with material (p. 4, §III.A; p. 3 for neglecting material torque).
- **Pile-up.** "we assume an average of ⟨µ⟩ = 50 pile-up interactions … We model pile-up by randomly selecting minimum bias
  events from a sample of 125×10³ events generated by Pythia8, processed by the simplified detector described in [34]".
  - That detector includes the beam pipe, services, endcaps, bremsstrahlung and ionisation loss.
  - Hadronic secondaries are **not** modelled; the authors argue this is "roughly offset by our conservative choice for
    ⟨µ⟩" (p. 6, §III.D).
  - Track-reconstruction efficiency for PU tracks comes from public ATLAS plots. The unassociated hits are the background.
- **Other backgrounds.**
  - Photon conversions in the beam pipe, from Z(νν)+γ+j generated with MadGraph: ∼1 pb fiducial, ∼10 fb after the
    conversion probability. These are overlaid with PU (p. 6).
  - **Z(νν)+jets is not considered.** Li et al. point this out (1911.02223 footnote 1, p. 1; p. 9).
- **Trigger and selection.**
  - Trigger: E_T^miss from ISR, with p_T(QQ̄) > 200 GeV (p. 4).
  - Plane finder: seed on layers 8 and 7, Δs < 0.05 cm, Δw < 1 cm, then iterative outside-in hit collection (pp. 5–6).
  - Signal region: (1) ≥ 1 plane; (2) all but one layer with 2 hits, the remaining layer ≥ 1; (3) Δw < 1.0 cm and
    Δs < 0.01 cm (p. 7).
- **Background estimate.**
  - "∼10⁻³ of all background events contain a plane with at least one hit in 4 out of 8 layers … drops to 6 × 10⁻⁵ … at
    least one hit in each layer" (p. 7).
  - "In order to compensate for the lack of simulation statistics, the pileup backgrounds are derived by taking the
    distribution from events that are allowed to have 1-hit per layer, weighted by the overall efficiency of passing the
    more stringent requirement" (p. 7). This is a factorised extrapolation, like our R04→deep-rung factorisation.
  - "Even though Fig 5 suggests a few background events after the final selection cut of Δs < 0.01 cm, we suspect that they
    can easily be removed through either a Δφ requirement between E_T^miss and n₃, and/or by examining dE/dx" (p. 7).
  - **The reach is computed "assuming negligible irreducible backgrounds"** (p. 8). Fig. 6 shows "Contours of having 3 (5)
    events … The 3 events bound corresponds to 2-σ exclusion given no background. Discovery is defined by 5 events"
    (p. 9, caption).
  - The number of simulated PU events after the full selection is not given.
- **Efficiency.** "the reconstruction efficiency for these cuts can be as high as ∼ 73 %" (p. 7). Table I (p. 8):
  - EW m_Q = 800 GeV: ε_trig = 0.10, ε_fid = 0.28, ε_reco = 0.11 / 0.41 / 0.65 / 0.72 / 0.74 at Λ = 1 / 2 / 3 / 4 / 5 keV.
  - So ε_tot = 0.10 × 0.28 × ε_reco ≤ **0.021** (ESTIMATE, product of VERIFIED factors).
  - The ≈ 70–73 % is ε_reco only. It is not the total efficiency.
- **Claimed reach.** "With the 300 fb⁻¹ dataset … colored (electroweak charged) quirks as heavy as 1600 (650) GeV may be
  discovered" (abstract, p. 1). Here "discovered" means ≥ 5 signal events with B = 0. √s is not stated explicitly (Run-2
  context).

### 1.2 J. Li, T. Li, J. Pei, W. Zhang, arXiv:1911.02223, PRD 102 (2020) 056006 [`Li:2019wce`]

Authors VERIFIED on INSPIRE and in the PDF. The four authors are Jinmian Li, Tianjun Li, Junle Pei, Wenxing Zhang. "Ran"
is **not** an author; Longjie Ran is on 2108.06748.
- **Model.**
  - Main results: D̃^c, Ẽ^c (scalars) and D^c, E^c (fermions), with E^c = (N_IC, 1, 1, −2) and **N_IC = 3** (p. 2, Eqs.
    II.1–4). Their Y convention gives Q = −1.
  - Appendix A redoes E^c and D^c with N_IC = 2 for the comparison with Knapen et al. (p. 10).
- **√s.** 13 TeV (p. 2, Fig. 2; p. 8, Table II).
- **Λ range.**
  - "This work will focus on the case with Λ ∼ [100 eV – 10 keV]" (p. 1).
  - The selection is optimised at Λ ≈ 1 keV, and the cut-flow quirk is D̃^c at "Λ = 1 keV" (p. 5 footnote 2; p. 6).
  - The quoted reach is effectively at Λ ∼ 1 keV.
- **Detector.**
  - CMS-like barrel: 13 barrel layers, 26 hits per pair (p. 5).
  - Hit resolution 10 µm (p. 4). The tracker dE/dx has 10 % Gaussian smearing.
  - SM and PU hits come from intersecting helices with the layers ("the same method as … Z+jets", p. 6). There are no
    material interactions, no secondaries and no conversions from PU.
- **Pile-up.** "both signal and background events are overlaid by ⟨µ⟩ = 50 pileup events in order to draw a conservative
  conclusion" (p. 6). Pythia 8 minimum bias, A3 tune, 45 mm z spread.
- **Backgrounds.**
  - "the Z(→νν)+jets process overlaid by pileup events is the dominant background" (abstract).
  - Also Z(νν)e⁺e⁻+jet. W(ℓν)+jet is argued away (p. 5).
  - Background count: Table II (p. 8), at 13 TeV in fb: Z+jets 3600 → 84.5 (E_T^miss ≥ 500 GeV) → 72.5 (Δp ≥ 0.8) →
    0.51 (S_p ≥ 50 MeV/cm) → **0.12 fb** (N_left ≥ 10); Zeej ∼ 0.
  - So B ≈ 12 events at 100 fb⁻¹ (ESTIMATE = 0.12 fb × 100 fb⁻¹).
  - This is a non-zero simulated background. The number of simulated Z+jets events is not stated.
- **Selection.** E_T^miss ≥ 500 GeV, Δp ≥ 0.8, S_p (distance-weighted dE/dx) ≥ 50 MeV/cm and N_left ≥ 10 hits within
  30 µm of the plane (p. 8). There is also a hit pre-filter: dE/dx > 3 MeV/cm and helix removal (pp. 6–7).
- **Claimed reach.**
  - "∼ 100 fb⁻¹ … color neutral fermion (scalar) quirks with masses up to 450 (150) GeV" (abstract; p. 9, Fig. 8). These
    are 95 % CL exclusions with N_IC = 3.
  - Appendix A (p. 10, Fig. 9), N_IC = 2, 300 fb⁻¹: their method gives E^c ≈ 400 GeV ("the corresponding bounds … 2.1 TeV
    and 400 GeV", p. 9).
  - Knapen's method reproduced without Z(νν)+jets gives "below 500 GeV". With Z(νν)+jets the Knapen selection leaves
    "∼10⁻² fb, which cannot be neglected" (p. 9). That is ≈ 3 events at 300 fb⁻¹ (ESTIMATE).
- **Lesson relevant for us.**
  - Li et al. already showed that Knapen's B = 0 assumption fails once an irreducible MET+PU background is simulated.
  - They also report, for Knapen's selection on Z(νν)+jets+PU, a plane-finding probability "∼10⁻⁴", dominated by "∼10⁻³"
    for five coplanar hits in three outer layers, and "∼10⁻²" for "all but one layer must contain two hits" (p. 9).

## 2. Why our P2 result differs from the published reach

### 2.0 A normalisation error found during this audit (must be fixed before any comparison) — Category A

**Finding (VERIFIED from code and data).**
- The Stage-3 P2 samples used for A and ε contain **only events with p_T(QQ̄) > 200 GeV**. The npz `meta_json.selection`
  reads `"sample": "trig (TRGS)", "ptmin": 200.0, "eta_max": 4.0`. In the data, min p_T(QQ̄) = 200.0–201.2 GeV for
  F200/1.5, F500/2 and F1000/3 keV.
- `acts/stage4/analysis/products.py::acceptance_point` computes A = ε_fid × ε_trig × ε_presel|trig with these events as
  the denominator. A is therefore **conditional on p_T(QQ̄) > 200 GeV**.
- `acts/stage4/results/make_stage4_summary.py` l. 159 computes `n_exp = SN[...] * L_FB * A`, with σ_norm the **inclusive**
  cross section (`sim/results/xsec.json → normalisation_E.rows.*.sigma_norm_nic2_fb`).
- The tail fraction f(p_T(QQ̄) > 200) is missing. It is f = 0.0485 / 0.1044 / 0.1462 / 0.1711 at E200/500/1000/1500
  (`xsec.json → trigger_yields_E.rows.*.f_pt200_INC`, VERIFIED), and f = 0.1119 for Ẽ500 (ESTIMATE, computed here from
  `data/sim/npz/S-INC_m500.npz`, w > 0 rows).
- Stage 2 defines the yield as "N = sigma_norm(N_IC=2) x f x 3 ab^-1" (`xsec.json → trigger_yields_E.note`).
- **The paper itself is internally inconsistent.**
  - Table `tab:xsec` gives N_200(|η| < 2.5) = 2.71 × 10⁴ events for E200.
  - Table `tab:acc` gives N_exp = 3.2–3.3 × 10⁵ after trigger and preselection.
  - That is 12× more events after the cuts than before them.
- Corrected N_exp(E200, 1.5 keV) = 3.31 × 10⁵ × 0.0485 = 1.61 × 10⁴. This equals N_200,η<2.5 × ε_trig × ε_presel|trig
  = 27 137 × 0.90 × 0.66 ≈ 1.6 × 10⁴ (ESTIMATE, consistent).

**Consequence (ESTIMATE; μ₉₅ unchanged, only N_exp rescaled by f).**

| point | μ₉₅/N_exp as published | corrected (÷ f) |
|---|---|---|
| E200 1.5 keV (best) | 37.6 | **≈ 7.8 × 10²** |
| E200 2 keV | 39.1 | ≈ 8.1 × 10² |
| E200 1 keV | 109 | ≈ 2.3 × 10³ |
| E200 3 / 5 keV | 133 / 140 | ≈ 2.7 / 2.9 × 10³ |

- With the full-chain systematic (× 1/0.87) the best ratio becomes ≈ 9 × 10².
- σ₉₅ = μ₉₅/(L·A) must become μ₉₅/(L·A·f): 8.2 × 10³ fb → ≈ 1.7 × 10⁵ fb at E200 1.5 keV.
- The §5 "Even with B = 0" statement changes:
  - E1500 gives at most 0.30 signal events, not 1.7.
  - E1000 reaches S ≥ 3 only at Λ = 2 and 5 keV (3.2, 3.4), not at Λ ≥ 0.6 keV.
  - Ẽ500 reaches S ≥ 3 only at Λ ≥ 2 keV.
- **The qualitative result (no WP, nothing excluded) is unchanged and becomes stronger.** Every headline ratio and every
  S, N_exp and σ₉₅ number in STAGE4_RESULTS §0/§4.4/§5, STAGE4_SUMMARY.json, the abstract, §1, §8, §10, §13 and
  Fig. `D7`/limits plots is too optimistic by 1/f = 5.8–21.
- **Fix (no re-simulation):**
  - multiply `n_exp` by f(p_T > 200) in `make_stage4_summary.py`, or fold f into A in `products.py`;
  - re-run the summary, self-check, Stage-5 figures and paper numbers;
  - fetch f for Ẽ from a Stage-2 table, not from my ad-hoc computation.
- This is outside the A-3 scope but it is a Category-A error. The orchestrator must route it (fixer + reviewer). All
  numbers below are given **both** as run and corrected.

### 2.1 The decisive difference is the background treatment: they assume or estimate B ≲ O(10), we bound B ≈ 10⁵–10⁷

**What our ε gives with their background assumption (ESTIMATE).**
- Inputs: Poisson CLs, expected (median-n) limit; s₉₅ = 3.00 events for B = 0 and for B = 0.1, 4.11 for B = 1, 8.1 for
  B = 10. The signal S = N_exp(corrected) × ε(r_fb) uses the VERIFIED fallback ε of STAGE4_SUMMARY.json and the best Λ
  per mass.
- **3 ab⁻¹, B = 0, corrected f.**
  - Best-Λ signal counts S = 877 / 87 / 3.4 / 0.30 at E200 / E500 / E1000 / E1500, and 6.8 at Ẽ500.
  - Hence σ₉₅/σ_th = 0.0034 / 0.034 / 0.88 / 10, and 0.44 at Ẽ500.
  - The B = 0 exclusion reach is m_E ≈ **1.0 TeV** (3-event contour, log-interpolated), and Ẽ500 would be excluded.
  - (As published, i.e. without f: 1.7 × 10⁻⁴ / 0.0036 / 0.13 / 1.7.)
- **300 fb⁻¹, B = 0 (Knapen's set-up), corrected f.**
  - 5-event "discovery" up to m_E ≈ **560–590 GeV**; 3-event exclusion up to ≈ **630–665 GeV** (log/linear
    interpolation between E500 and E1000).
  - Knapen et al. claim "650 GeV may be discovered" (5 events, B = 0, 300 fb⁻¹, probably 13 TeV).
  - So **at B = 0 our detector-level PU-200 efficiency reproduces their reach to ≈ 10–15 % in mass.**
  - Our ε_tot = f·A·ε ≈ 0.005 at E500 2–5 keV, against their ≤ 0.021 at 800 GeV and 4 keV. That is ≈ 4× lower: barrel
    |η(n₃)| < 1 against |η| ≲ 1.8, plus deep cuts. Our 14 TeV, K-factor-normalised cross section partly compensates.
- **100 fb⁻¹, B = 0.** The 3-event reach is m_E ≈ 495 GeV, for N_IC = 2 at 14 TeV. Li et al. find 400 GeV at 300 fb⁻¹
  (N_IC = 2) and 450 GeV at 100 fb⁻¹ (N_IC = 3) with B ≈ 12 events. These are of the same order.
- **Conclusion:** our signal efficiency is not the reason for the gap. The gap is entirely the background.

**Break-even background (ESTIMATE).** This is the largest total expected B (single bin) at which our expected limit
still excludes, with the corrected S:

| point | S (corrected) | B_max, no B uncertainty | B_max, σ_ln = 1 |
|---|---|---|---|
| E200 1.5 keV | 723 | 1.35 × 10⁵ | **8.3 × 10²** |
| E500 2 keV | 87 | 1.9 × 10³ | 95 |
| E1000 5 keV | 3.4 | 0.69 | 0.64 |

Our background at the E200 fallback selection FB2, as-run VERIFIED:
- median ≈ 5.9 × 10⁵ (SR-G), B₈₄ = 8.9 × 10⁶ (SR-G) and 2.6 × 10⁶ (SR-T);
- that is 3–4 decades above the break-even B.
- Li et al.'s simulated background, scaled to 3 ab⁻¹ (0.12 fb × 3000 fb⁻¹ ≈ 360 events, ESTIMATE, a different selection
  and √s), would sit **below** our break-even B at E200.

**Where our B comes from, and why (a) dominates.**
- **(a) Background method — dominant, ≥ 3–4 decades.**
  - From R08 onward every deep-rung term is a zero-count bound, entered as 1.14/M with log-normal σ_ln. This covers
    C′ DIF 1.7 × 10⁵, C′ PV pair 1.4 × 10⁵, SM Z(νν) 1.6 × 10⁴ at R09, and the PU term (VERIFIED, STAGE4_RESULTS §1.3).
  - The effective bound per term is ≈ 1.14·c/N_sample with N_sample = 500 (C′) or 2 000 (SM). It is a property of the
    sample size, not a measured fake rate (INV_STATS_BKG §5).
  - Reaching 0.11 needs 10⁸–2.8 × 10⁹ events per term (VERIFIED §2).
  - On top of this come:
    - the pre-registered compounded closure factor × 2.409 on every PU column ≥ R05 (VERIFIED §3);
    - the trend + density σ_ln ≈ 1.7 (up to 8.1 total at R23);
    - B₈₄ (84th percentile) rather than the median as the WP criterion.
  - Knapen et al. instead extrapolate PU from looser planes, argue the residual events away and set B = 0. Li et al. use
    a simulated Z+jets+PU estimate (0.12 fb), whose statistical treatment is not stated (zero-count handling unknown).
  - Neither study would pass our pre-registered WP rule (B₈₄ ≤ 0.11 at 3 ab⁻¹): Li's 360 events (ESTIMATE) is 3.5
    decades above it.
  - **The deep-rung bounds are upper limits.** The true deep-rung background in our set-up may be orders of magnitude
    lower. The simulation cannot tell.
- **(b) PU 200 vs ⟨µ⟩ = 50 — real, but subdominant at the deep rungs.**
  - Measured (non-bound) PU fakes fall from 1.1 × 10¹⁰ (R04) to 1.4 × 10⁵ (R07, the last rung with ≥ 10 counts), VERIFIED.
  - A candidate built from n_int specific PU interactions scales as ρ^{n_int}. Going from PU 200 to PU 50 would lower
    such terms by 4^{n_int}, i.e. 16–4 × 10³ for n_int = 2–6 (ESTIMATE; the n_int distribution was not extracted for
    this audit).
  - Beyond R07 the B is dominated by single-interaction C′ and SM bounds, which do not scale with ρ. Even the PU term
    alone is a bound there: B₈₄ = 2.3 × 10³ at R09.
  - So PU 200 explains why loose selections fail. It does not explain the deep-rung floor.
  - Per triggered SM event, our last measured PU fake probability is ≈ 3 × 10⁻³ at R07 (ESTIMATE = 1.4 × 10⁵ /
    N_trig 4.08 × 10⁷). Knapen et al. quote 6 × 10⁻⁵ for "a plane that contains at least one hit in each layer" (PU 50).
    The selections are not equivalent, so no ratio is claimed.
- **(c) Barrel only / selection — costs a factor ≈ 4 in ε, not decades.**
  - |η(n₃)| < 1 and the frozen fallback columns give ε(r_fb) = 0–0.08 (VERIFIED).
  - Knapen's ε_reco ≤ 0.74 applies within |η| ≲ 1.8 after ε_fid = 0.28. Li uses E_T^miss ≥ 500 GeV and dE/dx.
  - Our selections were frozen before signal ε (pre-registration). They were not optimised per point, whereas Li's cuts
    are optimised at Λ ≈ 1 keV.
  - Our background model also includes topologies absent from both studies: PV pairs, decays in flight, loopers, nuclear
    interactions, plus 8 SM processes with flagged proxies (VERIFIED §1.3). These dominate from R07 on.
- **(d) Λ region — not a cause.**
  - Our grid, 0.45–5 keV, overlaps Knapen's 100 eV – 30 keV and Li's ∼1 keV. Our best points are at 1.5–5 keV, where
    Knapen's ε_reco is 0.41–0.74.
  - Our upper detector-level edge (4.7–6.6 keV without PU, VERIFIED §0) is lower than Knapen's (ε_reco 0.58 at 10 keV,
    coloured). That matters only above 5 keV.
- **(e) Mass/σ normalisation — partly.**
  - The f error of §2.0 makes our published ratios 6–21× too optimistic. Fixing it widens, not narrows, the gap to the
    published reach.
  - The other differences go the other way: N_IC = 2 (Knapen, Li-App. A) vs 3 (Li main), √s 13 vs 14 TeV, our K-factor
    (1.11–1.35) vs LO, 100/300 fb⁻¹ vs 3 ab⁻¹. They are O(1) and, at B = 0, favour our reach.

**Verdict: no genuine tension, and the results are compatible.**
- At B = 0, or at any B ≲ 10²–10³ events, our detector-level efficiencies give a reach of the same order as Knapen et al.
  (≈ 0.6 TeV at 300 fb⁻¹; ≈ 1 TeV at 3 ab⁻¹).
- The gap to "nothing excluded" comes from our background **upper bounds**: ≈ 10⁵–10⁷ events from zero counts in 500–2 000
  simulated events per process, × 2.41 closure, B₈₄ criterion.
- It does not come from a measured background that contradicts theirs.
- Our result does not show their projections are wrong. It shows that a pre-registered, simulation-only estimate at
  PU 200 cannot confirm the background-free assumption. Confirming it needs 10⁸–10⁹ events per process or data-driven
  methods.
- One genuine physics point is supported by our study and by Li et al.: B = 0 is an assumption, not a result. Li et al.
  show Knapen's selection leaves ∼10⁻² fb of Z(νν)+jets+PU. We add PU-200 combinatorics and single-interaction material
  topologies that neither study simulated.
