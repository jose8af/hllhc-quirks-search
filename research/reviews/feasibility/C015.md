# Gate 4 feasibility review: C015, long-lived and flavour-violating ALPs from top decays

Reviewer: HL-LHC feasibility (g4f2). Input read: `candidates/gate4_targets.json` (entry C015) and the primary sources listed below. No other reviews were read.

## Target being evaluated
This is the long-lived t -> q a decay (q = u, c) with a light ALP (m_a about 1 to 35 GeV; the 2202.09371 benchmarks are 2 and 10 GeV). The ALP decays in the HCAL or the muon system (c tau about 0.01 to 10 m) in lepton-triggered ttbar events. The target also covers the leptonic or photonic displaced ALP decays (mu mu, gamma gamma) and the prompt t -> q a, a -> gamma gamma / mu mu. The proposal says that much of this could be done by adding a top-decay interpretation to the existing ATLAS CalRatio W/Z+LLP and CMS muon-detector-shower (MDS) analyses.

## 1. Production rate and yields
- Signal formula: sigma_sig = sigma_tt x BR(t->Wb) x BR(t->aq). The inputs are sigma_tt ~ 830 pb (13 TeV) and BR(t->Wb) ~ 0.96 [2202.09371, Sec. 4.1, Eq. 4.1]. Note that Eq. 4.1 has no factor 2 for the two tops, so it is conservative by about 2.
- sigma_tt(13 TeV, NNLO+NNLL, Top++2.0) = 833.9 pb [2404.06126, Sec. 3.2, around Table 1]. I found no explicit 14 TeV total ttbar cross section in the sources I read; 1902.04070 Sec. 6.1.1 gives only cumulative distributions. Following 2306.08686 (Sec. 4), I use the 13 TeV value as a conservative floor. The 14 TeV value is higher by an amount that is UNVERIFIED here (expected O(15-20%)).
- **ESTIMATE (yields before selection, 3 ab^-1):** N_tt x BR(Wb) >= 833.9 pb x 3x10^6 pb^-1 x 0.96 = 2.4x10^9.
  - That gives 2.4x10^4 signal events for BR(t->aq) = 10^-5 and 2.4x10^3 for BR = 10^-6.
- **Acceptance:** in 2202.09371 Table 1 (m_a = 2 GeV, c tau = 0.1 m), 14.4% of signal passes 3-6 jets plus the CalRatio log10(Ehad/Eem) > 1.2 plus the track cut. The value is 7.75% for m_a = 10 GeV. The paper says "up to ~15%" (Sec. 4.3).
  - This is a Delphes/parametrised study: the Ehad/Eem response is taken from an ATLAS figure, and there is no pileup and no real tracking.
- **ESTIMATE with a lepton trigger:** require a leptonic W from the other top. I use a fraction f_l ~ 0.3 for e/mu including leptonic tau. This uses standard W leptonic BRs, not checked against PDG in this review, so it is UNVERIFIED.
  - BR = 10^-5 gives about 2.4x10^4 x 0.14 x 0.3 ~ 1.0x10^3 events in the HCAL category before any further identification inefficiency.
  - BR = 10^-6 gives about 100 events; BR = 10^-7 gives about 10.

## 2. Backgrounds and what scales with luminosity
- **HCAL (CalRatio-like) category.** In 2202.09371 Table 1 the tt background after cuts is 6.39x10^4 events at 350 fb^-1. That is an efficiency of 2.2x10^-4, estimated from only 10^5 Delphes tt events.
  - **ESTIMATE:** scaled to 3 ab^-1 this is about 5.5x10^5 events. A 2-sigma S/sqrt(B) reach without the lepton requirement is then BR ~ 4x10^-6. The analysis is clearly **background-limited**, so sensitivity improves only as sqrt(L): a factor of about 2.9 from 350 fb^-1 to 3 ab^-1.
  - The paper itself states "we do not include systematic effects" (Sec. 4.3).
  - Real CalRatio backgrounds also include QCD multijet, beam-induced background (BIB) and cosmics. The ATLAS CalRatio search uses dedicated BIB and cosmic datasets [2407.09183, Sec. on triggers/datasets, around the text "BIB-removal algorithm" and "cosmic ray dataset"]. The Delphes tt estimate therefore does not represent the real background, in either direction.
  - **Main background:** the dominant background in lepton-channel LLP searches is tt and W+jets [2403.15332, background section: "The dominant sources of background are tt and W+jets, Z+jets, and multijet..."]. For a top-decay signal, irreducible tt is the background, and it scales with L.
- **Muon-system category.** 2202.09371 assumes this is background-free (Sec. 4.1) and shows only "10-event" discovery lines. With those it claims BR down to ~10^-7 for 350 fb^-1 and ~10^-8 at the HL-LHC with 4 ab^-1 (Sec. 4.3, App. A/Fig. 7).
  - This is the only part of the target where luminosity scales roughly linearly.
  - Real MDS searches are not background-free. Punch-through jets and muon bremsstrahlung require jet and muon vetoes, and the CMS search is optimised with a data-driven ABCD method [2402.01898]. The truth is therefore between linear and sqrt(L). The 10^-8 figure is an upper bound on reach, not a projection.
- **Prompt t -> q a with a -> mu mu / gamma gamma.** The background is tt plus a non-resonant pair, and the search would be a mass bump at low m_a.
  - For a -> mu mu at m_a of a few GeV, quarkonia and Drell-Yan or heavy-flavour dimuons are relevant.
  - The background is resonant-bump-limited, so reach scales as sqrt(L).

## 3. Trigger
- **Run 2 / Run 3 practice.**
  - The ATLAS ID-DV search triggered tt-type events with single and dilepton triggers in a 1-lepton region (lepton pT > 27 GeV, E_T^miss > 30 GeV). It explicitly targets "tt, t -> ac/au" [2403.15332, event selection paragraph].
  - The ATLAS CalRatio W/Z channels likewise use single or dilepton triggers [2407.09183, trigger section, "In the remaining channels, single or dilepton triggers ... were employed"].
  - The Run-2 CMS MDS search triggered on online pT^miss > 120 GeV, required offline pT^miss > 200 GeV, and had a signal acceptance of order 1% [2402.01898, Sec. 6]. That would be very inefficient for t -> q a.
  - In Run 3, CMS added L1 MDS (CSC/DT hit-multiplicity) triggers and HCAL-timing/depth displaced and delayed jet L1 triggers [2601.17544, Secs. 6.3.1, 6.4.5, 6.4.6, 9].
- **Conclusion on trigger.** For this target the trigger is not the bottleneck. One top gives a single-lepton trigger, so no LLP-specific trigger is required.
  - HL-LHC single-lepton L1/HLT thresholds will be higher than in Run 2, and I did not retrieve the values: the Phase-2 L1 thresholds in CMS-TDR-021 (CERN-LHCC-2020-004) are UNVERIFIED here.
  - Dedicated LLP triggers would recover the all-hadronic tt mode (about 45% of tt, UNVERIFIED BR value). The CMS Phase-2 L1 will include tracking and ML algorithms [2601.17544, Sec. 8, citing CERN-LHCC-2020-004].
- **Leptonic ALP decays.** A boosted light ALP gives a collimated displaced dimuon. In Run 3 this is covered inclusively by CMS dimuon scouting and new displaced-dimuon triggers [2112.13769; 2402.14491]. In ttbar the prompt lepton again makes scouting unnecessary. Top reconstruction is not possible on scouting data [2306.08686, Sec. 4.3 "Inclusive di-muon searches"].

## 4. Pileup (<PU> 140-200)
- The CalRatio discriminant relies on a "no tracks with pT > 1 GeV within 0.2 x 0.2" requirement around the jet [2202.09371, Sec. 4.2]. The authors already loosened this to "< 2 tracks with pT > 2 GeV" because pileup tracks can point at the ALP direction (Sec. 4.2).
  - At PU 200 the density of random tracks in a 0.2 x 0.2 cone grows several-fold, so the track-veto efficiency and fake rate will degrade. The size of this effect is UNVERIFIED and no study was found.
  - Vertex/time association of tracks is the relevant mitigation (see Section 5).
- Pileup also adds calorimeter energy, which makes the Ehad/Eem ratio for low-pT, light (2 GeV) ALP jets noisier. The muon-system category is the least sensitive to pileup.
- For the ID-DV and dimuon channels, PU raises the combinatorial-vertex background. Displaced-vertex requirements such as |d0| > 3 mm and m_DV/DeltaR_max > 4 GeV [2403.15332, DV selection] suppress it.

## 5. Phase-2 detector upgrades
- **Help:**
  - CMS Phase-2 timing of about 10 ps-order resolution across the MTD and HGCAL, L1 tracking, and ML at L1 [2601.17544, Sec. 8]. MTD timing allows track-vertex time association, which restores the trackless-jet veto under PU.
  - HGCAL's high granularity gives longitudinal shower information for displaced showers in the endcap.
  - The CSC electronics upgrades for the HL-LHC sustain the MDS trigger [2601.17544, around line "preparation for the HL-LHC, the bulk of the CSC electronics upgrades"].
  - ATLAS ITk extends tracking coverage; HGTD (forward timing) is relevant only for forward ALPs. The ITk/HGTD performance for this signature is UNVERIFIED and there is no specific study.
- **Hurt or uncertain:**
  - The CMS barrel HCAL is not replaced; it keeps its Phase-1 depth segmentation and timing [2601.17544, Sec. 6.3.1].
  - Higher L1 lepton thresholds slightly reduce the tt acceptance.
  - Whether the ATLAS CalRatio and muon-RoI-cluster triggers survive in the Phase-2 TDAQ menu is UNVERIFIED. This target does not need them because of the lepton trigger.

## 6. Systematics
- The background must be data-driven (ABCD, as in 2403.15332 and 2402.01898). The uncertainty is therefore dominated by control-region statistics and non-closure, which shrink with L.
- Signal-side uncertainties include displaced-object reconstruction efficiency, the Ehad/Eem modelling of very light (2 GeV) hadronising ALPs, and ALP hadronisation and decay-width theory. The non-perturbative ALP decays near 1-3 GeV are a known theory uncertainty, but I have no quantitative source in hand (UNVERIFIED).
- Signal-side systematics only rescale the BR limit. They do not limit discovery in the low-background muon-system category.

## 7. Current limits and projections
- **ATLAS ID-DV, 140 fb^-1:** excludes BR(t -> aq) > 0.1% for 1.6 < c tau < 130 mm and 40 < m_a < 55 GeV. It describes this as the first probe of long-lived t -> aq [2403.15332, abstract and summary]. Masses below 40 GeV were not interpreted for the top model; signal samples were at m_a = 40, 55 GeV [2403.15332, simulation section].
- **Recasts and pheno:**
  - Single-top/top+jet recasts give BR ~ 10^-3 (prompt) and 10^-4 (long c tau) [2202.09371, Sec. 4.3, Fig. 6].
  - The ATLAS DV+jets recast gives 3 ab^-1 sensitivities [2404.06126, Sec. 3]. It assumes the same background as at 139 fb^-1 (S95 = 3.8/3.0 events) for 3000 fb^-1, which is an optimistic assumption.
- **Projections:** I found no official ATLAS/CMS HL-LHC projection for t -> q a with a long-lived ALP. Pheno projections:
  - 2202.09371 App. A: 14 TeV, 4 ab^-1, background-free, BR ~ 10^-8.
  - 2404.06126 (3 ab^-1).
  - 2306.08686 (tt + displaced dimuon, flavour-diagonal; 3 ab^-1, conservatively using 13 TeV cross sections).
- **Closest Run-2 experimental analyses to reinterpret:**
  - ATLAS CalRatio + W/Z, 140 fb^-1 [2407.09183].
  - CMS MDS [2402.01898; Run-3 two-MDS 13.6 TeV, INSPIRE 3181328].
  - CMS dimuon resonance in W/Z/tt association [2402.11098]: prompt, multilepton.

## 8. Distinguishability
- The signature is distinctive: a reconstructed hadronic or leptonic top plus a prompt light jet plus one displaced/trackless object. The invariant mass of (displaced object + light jet) close to m_t is an additional handle (2202.09371 Sec. 4.2 notes this but does not use it).
- Light (2 GeV) ALPs give a single narrow trackless jet. They are hard to distinguish from neutral-hadron-rich jets (the tt background with Eem ~ 0, Sec. 4.2 / Fig. 4), which is where pileup-robust track vetoes matter.
- For m_a near the 40 GeV boundary, ATLAS ID-DV already covers part of the space.

## 9. Verdict
- **Biggest feasibility obstacle.** The real (not Delphes) background and signal efficiency of a trackless/low-EM-fraction jet at PU 200 for very light, highly collimated ALPs (m_a ~ 2-10 GeV). In the HCAL category the search is background-limited, so the claimed reach of 10^-7 to 10^-8 rests on a "background-free" assumption that no experimental CalRatio or MDS analysis has demonstrated. A secondary obstacle is that existing searches have not been interpreted below m_a = 40 GeV for the top model, and none target the m_a ~ 1-10 GeV regime with top-decay kinematics.
- **HL-LHC-specific advantage.** About 2.4x10^9 tt events (ESTIMATE, 3 ab^-1) with an unbiased single-lepton trigger from the other top. In the low-background muon-system category, statistics scale almost linearly. Phase-2 timing (MTD/HGCAL), L1 tracking and continued MDS triggers mitigate pileup for the trackless-jet veto.
- **Rating: 4/5.** The target is realistically testable, mostly as reinterpretation or added signal regions of existing lepton-triggered LLP analyses. A wholly new trigger or detector capability is not required. Points are deducted because the light-mass HCAL channel is background-limited and its reach is uncertain. **Confidence: medium.**
