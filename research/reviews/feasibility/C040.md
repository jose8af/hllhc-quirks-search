# C040: Compressed direct stau pairs (dm 5-60 GeV) with ISR/VBF + soft tau_h — HL-LHC feasibility (Gate 4)

**Rating: 3/5 (feasible as a collaboration projection and Phase-2 analysis; physics gain likely modest). Confidence: medium.**

## Target
The target is a collaboration-level HL-LHC projection, and later a Phase-2 analysis, for direct stau-pair production with dm(stau, chi0_1) of about 5-60 GeV. It would use ISR or VBF recoil together with soft tau_h.

## Status check (primary sources)
- **ATLAS Run 2+3 (140 + 53 fb-1).** The ISR channel alone excludes m(stau) up to 115 GeV at dm = 30 GeV. The combined channels give no exclusion for direct staus, because of local deviations of 2.1-2.4 sigma [2609.37215, abstract and Sec 12].
- The ATLAS paper states that compressed tau final states "remain largely unexplored" because of soft-tau reconstruction, trigger bandwidth and backgrounds [2609.37215, Sec 1].
- **CMS soft tau_h + ISR (77.2 fb-1).** This search is interpreted only for chargino/neutralino production with an intermediate stau [1910.01185].
- **HL-LHC Yellow Report.** The ATLAS and CMS stau projections are sensitive only for dm > ~100 GeV [1812.07831, Sec 2.3.1 and 2.3.3]. The report also states that compressed sensitivity "might be partially recovered exploiting the presence of a high pT ISR jet ... identification of tau objects at low pT will be crucial" [Sec 2.3.3].
- The YR statement confirms that no compressed-corridor projection exists. The status **searched-limited-regime + LHC-not-projected-to-HL** is confirmed.
- A natural-SUSY pheno study finds no 5 sigma reach at 3 ab-1. It projects exclusion of m(stau) 200-450 (400) GeV at m(chi0_1) ~ 100 GeV with 0% (25%) systematics, losing sensitivity below ~200 GeV because the taus become too soft [2403.18991, Sec 5.4].

## Cross section and yield
The cross sections below are aNNLO+NNLL values at 13.6 TeV from the LHC SUSY XS WG twiki (SUSYCrossSections13x6TeVslepslep, read via WebFetch, re-verify):

| m (GeV) | stau1 (fb) | L selectron (fb) | R selectron (fb) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 100 | 124 | 300.2 | 106.9 |
| 150 | 30.85 | 70.77 | 26.02 |
| 200 | 10.92 | 24.51 | 9.145 |
| 300 | 2.296 | 5.053 | 1.915 |

There is no 14 TeV slepton page on the twiki.

- ESTIMATE: an L+R-degenerate stau at 150 GeV has about 97 fb, giving about 3e5 pairs at 3 ab-1.
- Requiring a hard ISR jet (pT > 100-200 GeV) for the MET trigger keeps an UNVERIFIED percent-level fraction. This is consistent with the 25.5 fb (pT,j > 100 GeV) versus much larger inclusive cross section seen in analogous electroweak triplet pair production [2603.15882, Table 3; different model, indicative only].
- The selection also needs 15 < pT(tau_h) < ~40 GeV. 1-prong ID efficiency is 60-75% (Tight/Medium) [2609.37215, Sec 4].
- The expected final signal is O(10^2) events against O(10^3-10^4) background (ESTIMATE, unverified).

## Scaling with luminosity
- Both the CMS and ATLAS compressed searches report that the **statistical uncertainty dominates** in most SR bins [1910.01185; 2609.37215, Sec 10]. HL-LHC therefore gives a real ~15x data increase over 193 fb-1.
- In the background-dominated regime the cross-section reach improves by about sqrt(15) ~ 3.9, until fake-tau systematics take over (ESTIMATE).
- The cross section drops about 4x from 100 to 150 GeV and about 2.8x from 150 to 200 GeV [twiki table above]. A 3-4x sensitivity gain therefore moves the dm ~ 30 GeV exclusion from ~115 GeV to roughly 150-200 GeV (ESTIMATE).
- The gain is less at dm < ~15-20 GeV, where the visible tau falls below the ~15 GeV tau_h threshold.

## Backgrounds and systematics
- Jet->tau_h fakes dominate every SR. They are modelled with data-driven fake factors [2609.37215, Sec 5, 9].
- Fake-tau closure uncertainties are 4-29% depending on tau pT [1910.01185].
- The YR projections assumed a 15% jet->tau fake uncertainty and 2.5% tau ID [1812.07831, Sec 2.3.2].
- Irreducible backgrounds are Z->tautau+jets, W->tau nu+jets, ttbar and diboson.
- At 3 ab-1 the fake-rate systematic for 15-30 GeV taus at PU 200 will cap the sensitivity.

## Trigger
- **Run 2/3.** The ATLAS ISR channels use MET triggers with thresholds rising from 70 to 115 GeV and an offline MET > 200 GeV plateau [2609.37215, Sec 3 and Sec 7]. CMS uses offline MET > 230 GeV [1910.01185].
- **Phase-2.** The CMS L1 uses tracker tracks and particle-flow/PUPPI objects, including MET and tau candidates [CMS-TDR-021, INSPIRE 1819968]. Actual Phase-2 L1 MET and di-tau thresholds are UNVERIFIED.
- The ISR+MET route stays viable. A pure soft di-tau_h L1 seed at 15-20 GeV is unlikely to fit the rate (UNVERIFIED).
- VBF triggering (forward dijet + MET) is possible. HGCAL and HGTD improve forward pileup-jet rejection, and the CMS VBF compressed searches [1905.13059] provide a template.

## Pileup and detector
- ATLAS already observes a larger fake-tau contribution in Run 3 because of higher pileup [2609.37215, Sec 8]. This worsens at PU 200.
- Mitigation comes from ITk, the CMS Phase-2 tracker, timing (MTD/HGTD) for isolation and vertex association, and PUPPI MET.

## Distinguishability
- The signature (one or two soft tau_h, ISR jet, MET, low m_T) is shared with compressed charginos/neutralinos decaying via intermediate staus, and with Higgsino-like spectra.
- Shape information (tau pT, m_T, m_T2) gives limited model discrimination.
- For dm < m_tau the stau becomes long-lived. That regime is covered by displaced-tau/HSCP searches, e.g. CMS 2601.17576, and is out of scope here.

## Summary
- **Biggest obstacle:** the jet->tau_h fake background, and its systematic uncertainty, for 15-30 GeV taus under PU 140-200, combined with the small direct-stau cross section after the ISR/VBF requirement.
- **HL-specific advantage:** current sensitivity is statistics-limited, so the ~15x luminosity gain is genuine, and Phase-2 L1 PF/track objects should help keep an ISR+MET trigger efficient.
- **Verdict:** a projection is realistic and missing. The likely outcome is an exclusion reach of a few hundred GeV at dm ~ 20-60 GeV, with little reach below dm ~ 15 GeV.
