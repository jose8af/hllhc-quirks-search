# C019: Doubly charged scalars (displaced, compressed, tau) — HL-LHC feasibility (Gate 4)

**Rating: 3/5 for sub-target (i) displaced fermiophobic H++; (ii) about 1/5; (iii) about 3/5 but incremental. Confidence: medium.**

## Sub-target (i): fermiophobic H++ -> W+W+, c*tau 0.1-100 mm, displaced-vertex search

### Cross section and yield
- The 14 TeV LO Drell-Yan H++H-- cross section is 470.95 fb at 120 GeV, 14.79 fb at 300 GeV, 1.60 fb at 500 GeV and 0.15 fb at 800 GeV [2608.27251, Table 1]. NLO K-factors are available in [1912.08975] (not used here).
- At 3000 fb-1 this gives 4800 produced events at 500 GeV, 3150 after the full DV selection, and about 300 at 800 GeV [2608.27251, Table 4]. These yields assume a high-efficiency displaced-object trigger [2608.27251, Sec 4.2].
- The paper projects 95% CL limits on sigma of about 0.01 fb (B=3) for 200-1050 GeV, with c*tau = 10-100 mm sensitive across the full mass range [2608.27251, Sec 5, Fig 10-11]. The limits for 300-800 GeV are interpolated, and 200 GeV and 1.05 TeV are extrapolated.

### Background
- The study finds zero MC background after mDV >= 20 GeV [2608.27251, Table 5]. However, it generated 2e8 QCD events against 2.15e15 expected [Table 1], so each MC event carries a weight of about 1e7 (ESTIMATE).
- The authors acknowledge that instrumental fakes, overlapping vertices and random track crossings are not modelled, and they bracket the background with B = 3 or 100 [Sec 5].
- The real background is therefore the instrumental high-mDV tail under PU 140-200, which must be estimated from data.
- Existing hadronic DV searches show this tail can be controlled by track multiplicity, vertex mass and multivariate methods [1811.07991, 2012.01581]. They are tuned for neutral di-jet LLPs and do not apply directly [2608.27251, Sec 3.2.2].

### Trigger
- **The trigger is the biggest real issue.** HT triggers are inefficient for low mH++ [2608.27251, Fig 6a]. The paper recommends dedicated L1/HLT triggers on displaced jets or leptons [Sec 4.2].
- CMS Phase-2 L1 receives outer-tracker tracks [CMS-TDR-021, INSPIRE 1819968]. An extended (displaced) L1 tracking plus a BDT vertex finder reconstructs vertices at 0.02-20 cm in simulation only, without hardware limits [PoS LHCP2024 274, doi:10.22323/1.478.0274].
- Run-3 ATLAS introduced displaced-lepton triggers [2410.16835, abstract]. The di-displaced-lepton selection in the pheno study (pT > 20 GeV, 0.5 < |d0| < 100 mm) has modest efficiency for W W decays [2608.27251, Fig 6b].
- No equivalent hardware track trigger exists for ATLAS at L0 (UNVERIFIED; check the ATLAS Phase-II TDAQ TDR).

### Pileup and detector
- A DV does not need primary-vertex association. The fake-vertex rate does grow with track density, which is the main unknown.
- Before decay, a charge-2 parent travels 1-10 cm, so it crosses only the innermost pixel layers. dE/dx or "charge-2 tracklet" tagging is suggested as an extra handle [2608.27251, Sec 4.2]. Its Phase-2 pixel performance is UNVERIFIED.
- MTD and HGTD timing could add time-of-flight information for slow heavy parents (qualitative).
- The DV acceptance depends on the material map. The pheno study uses the CMS map out to 150 mm [2608.27251, Sec 5, ref 2511.08212]; Phase-2 tracker geometry changes this.

### Coverage and distinguishability
- The ATLAS displaced-dilepton vertex search requires oppositely charged leptons [2601.05664, abstract].
- CMS displaced leptons [2110.04809] do not require a common vertex. The pheno authors argue their sensitivity to this signal is weak because of BR(W -> l) and the small electroweak cross section, about 12 fb versus 190 fb for the benchmark at 300 GeV [2608.27251, Sec 3.2.2]. That is the authors' estimate, not a recast.
- A high-mass, charge-asymmetric DV, or two such DVs per event, would be distinctive.

### Scaling with luminosity
- If the background stays near zero, the reach in sigma scales as about 1/L, which matters because sigma is 0.15-1.6 fb at 500-800 GeV.
- If B is about 100, the gain from 300 to 3000 fb-1 is closer to sqrt(L).

## Sub-target (ii): compressed triplet (dM 1-30 GeV), soft same-sign leptons + ISR

**Effectively not feasible as a discovery channel (about 1/5).**
- After all cuts, S = 0.204 fb against B = 6.93 fb (tautau+jets, ttbar, WW, tW, Zll, Wll) for M = 200 GeV, dM = 10 GeV [2603.15882, Table 3].
- At 3 ab-1 that is S about 610 and B about 20800, so S/B is about 3% (ESTIMATE).
- The paper's significance Z = sqrt(L) sigma_S / sqrt(sigma_S + sigma_B) [Eq. 19] has no systematics. It gives 2 sigma reach to M about 220-230 GeV and 5 sigma below about 190 GeV [Sec 4, Fig 5].
- Adding a background uncertainty of 1%, 2% or 5% gives Z of about 2.4, 1.4 or 0.6 (ESTIMATE, Z = S/sqrt(B + (fB)^2)).
- Nonprompt and fake soft leptons, which dominate real soft-lepton searches, are not simulated.
- The earlier same-sign low-m_ll study [2305.06889] uses a dilepton trigger that [2603.15882, Sec 1] calls unrealistic.
- More luminosity does not help here: the channel is systematics-limited.

## Sub-target (iii): hadronic-tau H++ final states

- ATLAS full Run 2 analyses only e/mu final states, though tau decays are counted in the BR. It reaches an observed 1080 GeV (LRSM, equal BR to ee, emu, mumu, etau, mutau, tautau) [2211.07505, abstract and Sec 9].
- A pheno study of the tau_h + 3 lepton channel with tau -> pi nu reaches 655 GeV (NH) and 695 GeV (IH) at 5 sigma with 3 ab-1 [1812.07831, Sec 5.1; 1802.00945].
- The CMS H++ result with tau_h uses only 12.9 fb-1 (CMS-PAS-HIG-16-036, INSPIRE 1510936).
- The CMS HL projection covers ee/mumu only, with a 1400 GeV expected exclusion [CMS-PAS-FTR-22-006, INSPIRE 2084511].
- A tau_h channel is standard and triggerable via the accompanying light leptons. Its added value is limited to tau-dominated BR scenarios, and it is background-limited by jet -> tau_h fakes.
- Overall it is feasible but incremental.

## Summary
- **Single biggest obstacle (i):** an efficient L1/HLT path for low-HT displaced W W decays, together with a data-driven control of the fake high-mass DV tail at PU 200. Neither is demonstrated; the pheno assumes an ideal trigger and has statistically meaningless background MC.
- **HL-specific advantage:** CMS Phase-2 L1 tracking with displaced-track extension, plus near-background-free scaling for a cross section that falls to the 0.1 fb level.
