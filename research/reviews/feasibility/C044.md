# C044 Gate 4 HL-LHC feasibility: VLQ exotic decays (TT -> 6t; X5/3 -> t phi+ -> t W gamma)

**Rating: 4/5 (6t channel ~4, SSL+gamma ~3) | Confidence: medium**

## 1. Targets evaluated
1. Dedicated pair-production search for T T -> (t S0)(t S0) -> 6t, plus the mixed channels tS0 + tZ/th. The analysis would be high-HT, multi-lepton (2SS/3L/3SS) and multi-b.
2. X5/3 X5/3 -> t phi+ ... with phi+ -> W+ gamma: same-sign dileptons (SSL) plus a hard photon.

## 2. Status check (primary sources)
- The recasting atlas [2609.00127, Sec. 4, Fig. 4a] says TT -> tS0 tS0 -> 6t has "no direct search available yet". Its recast excludes the channel up to **1.65 TeV** at an effective BR of 100%, and only up to **1.30 TeV** for Br(T->tS0)=50% and Br(tZ)=Br(th)=25%. The same paper notes that X X -> tS+ tS- is the only exotic top-philic channel searched so far.
- CMS-PAS-B2G-24-005 [INSPIRE 3157006, abstract] covers X5/3 in the single-lepton channel with 138 fb-1. It excludes 1630 GeV (obs., tW) and 1710 GeV (obs., tH+ -> ttb, m_H+ = 1 TeV). It does not cover phi+ -> W gamma.
- ATLAS T -> tS, S -> gamma gamma [2607.28381, Sec. 1 and Sec. 7] requires a resonant diphoton and excludes m_T < 1620 GeV in the composite benchmark. The charged phi+ -> W gamma (single photon, SSL) topology is not targeted.
- The proposed target is confirmed as *searched-limited-regime / indirectly-covered*.

## 4. Backgrounds, trigger, pileup, detector
- **6t:** ttW+jets, SM tttt, ttZ, ttH, and non-prompt or charge-flip leptons from ttbar. ATLAS assigns 133% / 208% uncertainty to ttW+jets at 7 / >=8 jets [ATL-PHYS-PUB-2025-017, Sec. 2]. Discriminants: HT > 2 TeV, >=5 b-tags, >=3 fat jets, top tags, and 3SS leptons, a signature almost absent in the SM [1812.11286 Sec. 4].
- **SSL+gamma:** ttW-gamma and tt-gamma with a non-prompt lepton, plus jet->gamma and e->gamma fakes. These can only be estimated from data.
- **Trigger:** not a limitation. The events have high-pT leptons and HT > 1-2 TeV, and the photon has pT > 200 GeV.
- **Pileup (140-200):** degrades isolation (leptons and photons), b-tag mistag rates and jet counting, which are the key handles. Timing layers (CMS MTD, ATLAS HGTD) and the Phase-2 trackers (CMS-TDR-014, ATLAS ITk pixel TDR) mitigate this. No signature-specific quantitative projection was found (UNVERIFIED magnitude).

## 5. Systematics and scaling
- The ATLAS HL projection for tttt [ATL-PHYS-PUB-2025-017, Sec. 3.1] shows that the inclusive tttt measurement is systematics-dominated above ~250 fb-1. For high-mass resonances, however, "the gain in sensitivity due to the increased luminosity ... is still sizable" (Sec. 4).
- Above m_T ~ 1.6 TeV the 6t and SSL+gamma regions are **near background-free**. The excluded cross section then scales ~1/L, not 1/sqrt(L), so 3 ab-1 gives roughly 10x more events than 300 fb-1 of Run 3.
- Systematics matter mainly for the 1.0-1.5 TeV hybrid channels (4t-like, ttW-limited).

## 6. HL-LHC versus Run-3 plus extrapolation
- A naive luminosity rescale in 1907.05894 (Sec. 3.1) moves the SSL X5/3 reach from 1.19 to 1.56 TeV (35.9 fb-1 -> 3 ab-1). This shows the typical gain of a few hundred GeV.
- 1812.11286 claims 6t discovery up to ~2.5 TeV at 3 ab-1 (abstract, Fig. 4). This is optimistic: the study is stat-only Delphes with limited background generation.
- The HL-LHC adds real reach in the ~1.8-2.3 TeV band. A dedicated Run-3 search would already cover much of 1.6-1.9 TeV.

## 7. Verdict
- **HL-specific advantage:** a background-free regime, where the luminosity converts directly into mass reach. The SSL+gamma channel needs O(10) signal events, and only the HL-LHC can provide them for m > 1.6 TeV.
- **Main obstacle:** the QCD pair-production ceiling (~0.13 fb at 2 TeV) combined with branching-ratio dilution in realistic models (an effective BR of 25% leaves ~1 event at 2 TeV), on top of Run-2 limits already at 1.6-1.7 TeV. For SSL+gamma, the fake-photon background is unquantified.
- **Distinguishability:** good. 3SS leptons, >=5 b-tags and HT reconstruction separate 6t from tttt. The photon pT spectrum and the SSL Delta-phi / m_ll separate X5/3 decay modes [1907.05894 Sec. 4.1, 4.3].

## Caveats
- The 1.5 TeV cross section is an inference, not a quoted number.
- The recast bounds use LO MadGraph normalized to NNLO+NNLL [2609.00127 Sec. 3].
- No official ATLAS/CMS HL projection exists for either channel.
