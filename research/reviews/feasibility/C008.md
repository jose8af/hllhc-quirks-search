# C008 - Photon-enriched SVJ and photon-ISR SVJ: HL-LHC feasibility (Gate 4)

**Targets evaluated:**
- **(i)** Photon-enriched SVJs (SVJγ) from an s-channel Z′: non-isolated photons inside the jets and high neutral-EM-fraction (NEMF) jets that fail standard JetID. Basis: arXiv:2407.08276.
- **(ii)** Low-mass (about 200-700 GeV) Z′→SVJ SVJ tagged with an ISR photon. Basis: arXiv:2409.04741.

**Rating: 3/5 overall. (i) is about 3-4/5, (ii) is about 2/5. Confidence: medium.**

## 1. Cross sections and yields
- **(i)** Benchmark: Z′ couplings g_q = 0.25 and g_χ = 0.4 (2407.08276 sec. 3), with r_inv = 0.3 and BR_γ = 0.3.
  - The paper claims 5σ discovery or exclusion "up to 5 TeV" already with 138 fb⁻¹ at 13 TeV using a DNN tagger (abstract; sec. 6, Fig. 5).
  - The existing CMS inclusive SVJ search "has no sensitivity to SVJγ signals" (sec. 6). That search excludes 1.5-4.0 TeV inclusive and up to 5.1 TeV with its BDT for standard SVJs (arXiv:2112.11125, abstract).
  - The cut-based selection keeps about 0.4-0.6% of the signal at m_Z′ = 3 TeV (2407.08276 Table 2). The DNN working point rejects about 99.6% of background jets and keeps about 95-97% of signal jets (sec. 5).
  - Absolute σ values were read only from a plot, so they are UNVERIFIED.
- **(ii)** Photon pT > 150 GeV and B(Z′→χχ) = 47% (2409.04741 sec. 2.1).
  - For the photon channel the paper gives only significance curves, computed "given perfect background modeling" (sec. 5). Expected limits are computed only for the jet-ISR channel (Fig. 17).
  - No yield or limit exists for (ii), so it is UNVERIFIED.

## 2. Backgrounds and distinguishability
- **(i)** Simulated backgrounds are QCD, γ+jets, tt̄ and EW V+jets (2407.08276 sec. 4).
  - The JetID NEMF < 0.9 cut exists to reject spurious jets (sec. 5, quoting the CMS JetID). Removing it reopens instrumental backgrounds: ECAL noise/spikes, beam-induced background and photon-dominated fake jets. These are not simulated in the pheno.
  - The γ+jets background in which the "jet" is a single photon is separable through isolation and substructure (Fig. 3b; sec. 5).
  - Distinguishability is good in simulation: AUC about 0.99 (Fig. 4).
- **(ii)** Backgrounds are γ+jets plus multijet with a fake photon and fake MET. The authors state that fakes and mismeasurement are not modelled ("Jets misidentified as photons will complicate the event selection…", sec. 5).
  - Overlap: ATLAS γjj/jjj low-mass resonance searches cover 200-650 GeV for visible dijets (arXiv:2403.08547, abstract and sec. 4). The CMS boosted γ+qq̄ search (arXiv:1905.10331) covers boosted topologies.
  - At low r_inv these searches partly constrain the same Z′ (indirectly-covered). The open region is moderate-to-high r_inv, where the m_jj peak is degraded.

## 3. Trigger
- **(i)** The m_T > 1.5 TeV selection puts events on the plateau of the Run-2 HT/jet triggers (2407.08276 sec. 5). There is no HL-LHC trigger issue.
- **(ii)** Single-photon trigger at about 140 GeV in Run 2 (2409.04741 sec. 4.2, citing ATLAS). ATLAS γjj uses offline pT > 150 GeV for full efficiency (2403.08547 sec. 4).
  - Phase-2 single-photon L1/HLT thresholds were not read from the TDRs (UNVERIFIED). If they rise, the low-mass reach shrinks.

## 4. Pileup and Phase-2 detectors
- Neither pheno study includes pileup (both use Delphes; 2407.08276 sec. 4, 2409.04741 sec. 2).
- Neutral energy (photons, π⁰) inside jets cannot be removed by charged-hadron subtraction. So at ⟨PU⟩ 140-200 the NEMF, photon isolation and r_T/r_inv estimators will smear.
- (ii) uses jets with pT > 25 GeV out to |η| < 4.5 (2409.04741 Table 2). That is strongly exposed to PU jets, so MTD/HGTD timing-based PU-jet rejection matters.
- CMS HGCAL granularity could help photon-in-jet identification in the endcaps, but changes the JetID/NEMF definitions. Signal (i) is central (|Δη| < 1.5). This is qualitative; no study was found.

## 5. Systematics
- No standard candle for photon-rich jets. Calibrating the tagger and NEMF modelling needs data control samples (e.g. γ+jet, π⁰-rich jets). The energy scale of EM-rich jets is also uncertain.
- (ii) at low mass: γ+jets statistics will be enormous at 3000 fb⁻¹, so the smooth-fit and spurious-signal background systematics dominate. The analysis is background-systematics-limited, so its reach grows slower than √L.
- Neither pheno includes systematics.

## 6. Projections
None. No HL-LHC projection of any SVJ search was found:
- 1812.07831 has no SVJ section.
- INSPIRE queries for "semivisible"/"semi-visible" plus "High-Luminosity" or "HL-LHC" gave 0 hits.

## 7. Verdict
- **Biggest obstacle:**
  - For (i), removing the NEMF cut admits instrumental high-EM-fraction fakes, and photon-rich jets have no calibration.
  - For (ii), there is no realistic background model (γ+jets and fake-photon multijet with fake MET) at low mass under PU200, and not even a Run-2-level limit has been computed.
- **HL-specific advantage:**
  - (i) is already testable with Run-2/3 data. HL-LHC adds mass and coupling reach in a background-limited m_T bump hunt (gain of roughly √L); nothing is intrinsically HL-specific.
  - (ii) gains statistics for an ISR-suppressed channel, but it is systematics-limited. HGCAL and timing are possible aids.
- The target is better pitched as a Run-3 analysis with an HL-LHC extrapolation than as an HL-LHC-only target.
