# Gate 4 Feasibility review: C005

**Candidate:** Emerging jets / dark showers, Higgs portal (GeV dark pions)
**Target I evaluated:** A CMS search for Higgs-portal (ggF/VBF/VH) emerging jets or dark showers. The dark mesons have masses of about 2-20 GeV, decay hadronically, and have cτ of about 1-100 mm, so they decay inside the tracker. The search would count multiple displaced vertices and use Phase-2 displaced L1/HLT tracking.
**Reviewer:** Gate-4 feasibility (g4f1). Primary sources only. All arXiv IDs were checked with `tools/inspire.py get`.

## Verdict (short)
- **Rating: 3/5. Feasible, with conditions.**
- **Lepton-tagged VH (W/Z → e, μ) works with triggers that exist today.** It gives O(10^2) selected signal events for BR(H→dark) = 10^-3 at 3000 fb^-1 (ESTIMATE below). When at least two displaced vertices are required, it runs close to the background-free regime, so its reach grows roughly linearly with luminosity.
- **The ggF/VBF part depends on two things.** It needs (a) a displaced-object L1 trigger that is not yet in the Phase-2 baseline, and (b) control of low-mass displaced-vertex backgrounds at PU 200. Neither has been demonstrated for 2-5 GeV hadronic dark mesons.
- **The existing HL-LHC projection (2512.24814) is optimistic.** It used offline-like trigger thresholds, did not simulate pileup, modelled the background as LO Pythia bb̄ only, and included no systematics.

## 1. Production cross section and yields
SM Higgs cross sections at 14 TeV are from 1902.00134 (HL/HE-LHC Higgs Yellow Report, WG2):

| Mode | σ(14 TeV) | Source and location |
|---|---|---|
| ggF (N3LO) | 54.72 pb | 1902.00134, Table 2 |
| VBF | 4.260 pb | 1902.00134, Table 4 |
| WH | 1.498 pb | 1902.00134, Table 5 |
| ZH | 0.981 pb | 1902.00134, Table 10 |
| ttH | 0.6128 pb | 1902.00134, Table 16 |
| ℓ+νH / ℓ−νH (per lepton flavour) | 0.104 / 0.0666 pb | 1902.00134, Tables 8 and 9 |
| ℓℓH (per lepton flavour) | 0.0331 pb | 1902.00134, Table 13 |

**ESTIMATE.** Inputs are the table above; the assumption is that the YR quotes "l" per single lepton flavour.
- **All Higgs bosons:** σ_tot ≈ 62.1 pb. That gives N_H ≈ 1.9×10^8 at 3000 fb^-1 and 2.5×10^8 at 4000 fb^-1.
  - For BR(H→q_D q̄_D) = 10^-3 this is about 1.9×10^5 signal Higgs bosons before selection.
  - For BR = 10^-4 it is about 1.9×10^4.
- **Lepton-tagged VH (e+μ):** σ ≈ 2×(0.104+0.0666) + 2×0.0331 = 0.407 pb, or about 1.2×10^6 Higgs bosons at 3000 fb^-1.
  - For BR = 10^-3 this is about 1.2×10^3 events before selection.
  - Taking the maximum displaced-jet acceptance of O(10%) for lepton-associated modes reported in 2512.24814 (Sec. V, Fig. 13, "peak efficiencies of order O(10%)" at cτ ~ 2-50 mm) gives about 120 events at 3000 fb^-1.
- **ggF with today's high-HT emerging-jet trigger:** the HT > 900 GeV cut keeps only 3.5×10^-3 of ggF signal (2307.04847, App., discussion of Fig. 10, 13 TeV). This is why the existing CMS EJ search (2403.01556, Sec. 4: HT > 900 GeV / jet pT > 450 GeV triggers) has essentially no Higgs-portal sensitivity.
- **ggF/all modes with the Run-3 CMS displaced-jet triggers:** these have an overall efficiency of ≈0.4-1.0% for H→SS, S→bb/dd (2409.10806, Sec. 4). Applied to all Higgs bosons with BR = 10^-3, that would give about 750-1900 triggered events at 3000 fb^-1 (ESTIMATE). Dark showers with many soft 2-20 GeV mesons have softer tracks and less HT than two-body H→SS, so this efficiency is UNVERIFIED for the dark-shower benchmark.

## 2. Current limits (what is already excluded)
- **Indirect bound on undetected Higgs decays:** B_u < 0.12 observed (0.21 expected) at 95% CL (ATLAS, 2207.00092, Sec. "Higgs boson coupling…", lines around Fig. 6).
- **HL-LHC projection for B_BSM:** the expected 1σ uncertainty is 0.025 for the ATLAS+CMS combination in scenario S2 (1902.00134, Sec. 2.x, κ-framework paragraph).
- **CMS muon-detector showers (2402.01898, abstract and conclusions):** "first LHC limits on models of dark showers produced via H decay", reaching BR(H→dark quarks) as low as 2×10^-3. This is the long-lifetime (≳ m) regime.
- **CMS Run-3 displaced jets (2409.10806, Sec. 8):** B(H→SS) > 1% is excluded for cτ0 ≈ 1.3-380 mm with S→dd at mS > 40 GeV. Interpretations are H→SS and twin-Higgs/folded-SUSY only; there is no dark-shower interpretation.
- **ATLAS ZH(ℓℓ) with ≥2 inner-detector displaced vertices (2107.06092, abstract and Sec. 8):** BR > 10% is excluded for cτ ≈ 4-100 mm.
  - Background is 1.30 ± 0.08 (stat) ± 0.27 (syst) events and 0 events were observed. This means the analysis is in the background-free regime.
  - Each DV must satisfy m/ΔR_max > 3 GeV (Sec. 4.1). This is not tuned for 2-5 GeV dark mesons.
- **CMS ZH LLP displaced jets (2110.13218, abstract):** B(H→SS) < 6% for cτ = 10-100 mm at m = 40-55 GeV, and < 20% at about 15 GeV.
- **ATLAS EJ searches (2505.02429 at 13.6 TeV, 2510.12347 at 13 TeV, abstracts):** these target Z′ s-channel or t-channel Φ mediators, not Higgs production.
- **ATLAS Higgs-portal dark-jet search:** an ATLAS proceeding (INSPIRE recid 3201494, DOI 10.1393/ncc/i2026-26131-3) presents a "Search for Dark Jets from a Higgs portal". It shows the Higgs-portal dark-jet idea is being pursued in ATLAS. I could not verify its status (result or ongoing analysis) or its kinematic region from the abstract.
- **Recast of the CMS EJ search to the Higgs portal (2307.04847):** projected to 3000 fb^-1 in a statistics-only approximation, it pins down BR ≈ 1%, compared with the 2.5-4% indirect HL-LHC expectation (abstract; Sec. 4, Fig. 8). The authors caution that the statistical dominance "might not be fully justified" (Sec. 4).
- **Pheno HL-LHC projection (2512.24814, abstract; Sec. V, Fig. 14):** BR(h→dark showers) constrained to O(10^-5-10^-2) over cτ = 1 mm-100 m. In the tracker the limit reaches "as low as O(10^-4)" at cτ ~ 10-100 mm.

## 3. Critical assessment of the 2512.24814 projection (the main input for the target)
1. **Trigger thresholds.** Its Category I "dijet" trigger is pT > 70/40 GeV, the "single jet" is 180 GeV and HT is 900 GeV (2512.24814, Table II).
   - The Phase-2 CMS L1 jet seeds for PU 140 are single jet > 173 GeV, dijet > 136 GeV, quad jet > 72 GeV and HT > 350 GeV. 2003.03943 (Sec. 2.1) quotes these from the CMS Phase-2 L1 TDR, CERN-LHCC-2020-004 (INSPIRE 1819968). That TDR could not be downloaded from CDS here, so these numbers are secondary-sourced.
   - For PU 200 "these might be increased" (2003.03943, Sec. 2.1).
   - So the dijet 70/40 GeV hadronic category is not a realistic unprescaled L1/HLT path. The lepton-triggered Category II (single μ 22 GeV, single e 36 GeV) is closer to reality.
2. **No pileup simulation.** Section IV says "pile-up was not simulated in this study". The muon-system part relies on MTD "to mitigate" pileup (footnote, Sec. IV B).
3. **Background modelling.** Background is LO Pythia "HardQCD" bb̄ only (Sec. IV A, Eq. 22). There is no material-interaction, K_S/Λ, fake-vertex or PU-track combinatorial background. Background acceptances are ~10^-5 (Cat. I) and ~10^-7 (Cat. II) (Sec. V).
4. **No systematics.** Significance uses the Asimov-type formula with no background uncertainty (Eq. 23).
5. **Conclusion.** The O(10^-4) limits are best read as a statistics-and-trigger ceiling, not a realistic expectation.

## 4. Backgrounds (dominant, in the tracker regime 1-100 mm, m_meson 2-20 GeV)
- **Heavy-flavour hadrons.** b and c hadrons have cτ ~ 0.1-0.5 mm (UNVERIFIED, standard PDG order of magnitude). They make low-mass, few-track displaced vertices. This is the dominant physics background in 2512.24814 (Sec. IV A).
- **K_S0 and Λ decays, photon conversions, and hadronic interactions in material.** These sit in the same mass range as 2-5 GeV dark mesons. The ATLAS DV analyses reject them with mass cuts (m/ΔR_max > 3 GeV in 2107.06092, Sec. 4.1) and material vetoes, which also remove the low-mass signal.
- **Random track crossings.** These grow combinatorially with the number of displaced/mis-measured tracks, so they get worse at PU 200 (qualitative; magnitude UNVERIFIED).
- **Instrumental backgrounds in VH(ℓ).** Z/W+jets with heavy flavour and tt̄. In Run 2 these were reduced to O(1) event with ≥2 high-mass DVs (2107.06092, Sec. 8).
- **Regime.** Requiring ≥2 multi-track DVs plus a lepton tag is close to background-free, and sensitivity scales ~L. One-DV or ggF-inclusive selections are background-limited, and sensitivity scales ~√L or is limited by systematics (2409.10806 has 3.34 ± 1.28 predicted background events at 34.7 fb^-1 for the H→SS displaced-jet SR, Sec. 8).

## 5. Trigger
- **Run 3 (CMS).** HLT displaced-jet triggers run at HT > 430 GeV with ≥2 inclusive-tagged jets, or L1 HT > 240 GeV + μ(6 GeV) with ≥2 displaced-tagged jets. Efficiency is ≈0.4-1% for H→SS (2409.10806, Sec. 4).
- **Phase-2 L1 track trigger.** It reconstructs tracks with pT > 2 GeV (2003.03943, Sec. 1; 2203.09503, Sec. 3 trigger discussion).
  - Prompt "tight" L1 tracks "die out beyond x⊥ of a couple mm"; looser tracks extend to "several cm" (1705.04321, Fig. 4/5 text).
  - A CMS feasibility study of an extended displaced L1 tracking plus vertexing (BDT) reconstructs vertices at L_xy 0.02-20 cm. It is "simulation only … does not account for hardware limitations" (INSPIRE 2908980, abstract). So it is not baseline.
  - The Phase-2 multijet/HT L1 seeds require a common z-vertex built from tracks with L_xy < 1 cm (2003.03943, Sec. 2.1). Displaced jets can fail this requirement, which is a possible inefficiency for the signal.
- **Trigger-independent channel.** VH(ℓ) runs on single/double lepton triggers and is the robust path.
- **ggF.** Needs an L1 displaced-track jet/vertex seed (as in 1705.04321, 2003.03943) or HLT scouting with displaced tracking. The latter is UNVERIFIED for the Phase-2 HLT.

## 6. Pileup effects
- **What PU raises.** At ⟨PU⟩ 140-200, PU raises the rate of random displaced-track crossings and dilutes the prompt-track veto variables (PU_dz, α3D) used in EJ tagging (2512.24814, Sec. IV A, which uses PU_dz but does not simulate PU).
- **Expected impact.** The EJ variables are relatively robust because they use track z-association. A ≥2-DV requirement scales worse than linearly with PU. The impact is qualitative and no CMS Phase-2 number is available (UNVERIFIED).

## 7. Phase-2 detector upgrades
- **Help:**
  - CMS Phase-2 tracker with L1 tracking (pT > 2 GeV; 2003.03943).
  - MTD timing of about 30 ps per MIP (2003.03943, Sec. 1). It associates tracks to vertices and can reject PU tracks; regional MTD use at L1 is "may still be possible" only (same source).
  - Finer granularity, which helps vertexing.
  - ATLAS ITk/HGTD are the analogous upgrades (not studied here).
- **Hurt or limit:**
  - Higher L1 thresholds at PU 200.
  - Prompt-only baseline L1 tracking.
  - Displaced-track HLT reconstruction cost at 750 kHz input (750 kHz is the Phase-2 L1 output from the TDR; UNVERIFIED, not read from the primary source).

## 8. Systematics (expected)
- **Signal:** displaced-track and vertex reconstruction efficiency (dominant in 2107.06092, Sec. 7), JES 5-10%, pileup modelling 1-8% and GNN/tagger modelling 4-14% (2409.10806, Table 2).
- **Dark-shower modelling:** dark-sector hadronization uncertainty is not part of the experimental systematics but dominates the interpretation (2203.09503).
- **Background:** data-driven ABCD; 21% in 2107.06092 (Sec. 6), and ~38% relative in 2409.10806 (3.34 ± 1.28, Sec. 8).

## 9. Single biggest obstacle
**Triggering on ggF/VBF Higgs → dark showers with soft (a few GeV per meson), displaced, low-HT final states at PU 200.**
- Run-3 displaced-jet triggers keep only ≈0.4-1% of H→SS (2409.10806).
- The L1 displaced-track seed that would change this exists only as a simulation-level feasibility study (INSPIRE 2908980; 1705.04321).
- Without it, the program reduces to VH(ℓ). That channel has a cross section ~0.4 pb, which limits reach to roughly BR ~ 10^-3 (ESTIMATE, Sec. 1), not the 10^-4 claimed in 2512.24814.
- **Second obstacle:** for m_meson ≲ 5 GeV, the K_S/Λ/material/heavy-flavour low-mass DV backgrounds make background-free counting unproven.

## 10. HL-LHC-specific advantage
**A real Phase-2 advantage.**
- The L1 track trigger (pT > 2 GeV at 40 MHz) gives the first hardware-level access to track displacement and multiplicity, which is exactly what this signature needs (1705.04321; 2003.03943; INSPIRE 2908980).
- MTD timing helps reject PU in vertex association (2003.03943, Sec. 1).
- 20-30× more Higgs bosons than Run 2 matter in the background-free VH(ℓ) ≥2-DV channel, where reach scales ~L.
- More luminosity helps little in background-limited single-DV ggF selections.

## 11. Is the signature distinguishable?
- **Yes for the 10-20 GeV mesons, with ≥2 multi-track DVs in a lepton-tagged or displaced-L1-seeded event.** Run-2 ZH analyses already show that ≥2 DVs are near background-free (2107.06092).
- **Marginal for 2-5 GeV gluon-decaying mesons.** Their DVs have 2-4 tracks and masses comparable to K_S/B hadrons.
- **Model-dependent separation from H→SS (two-body).** It relies on DV multiplicity and track multiplicity, which depend on the dark-shower parameters (2512.24814, Fig. 4).
