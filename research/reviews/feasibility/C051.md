# Gate 4 feasibility review: C051
**High-mass DY angular coefficients (Lam-Tung A0-A2, T-odd A5-A7) and A_FB / sin2theta_W running**

Reviewer: HL-LHC feasibility, Gate 4 (independent; inputs: `candidates/gate4_targets.json` and primary sources only).
Date: 2026-10-05

## 0. Target being evaluated (from gate4_targets.json)
1. A full (phi-dependent) unfolded A0-A7 measurement, especially A0-A2, double-differential in m_ll (> ~170-200 GeV) and pT_ll.
2. A5-A7 above the Z peak.
3. An experimental extraction of the MSbar sin2theta_W(mu) running from high-mass NC DY. (The high-mass A_FB itself is already measured, [S1].)

## 1. Existing measurements (what the baseline really is)
- **CMS high-mass A_FB and A0, 138 fb-1, 13 TeV** [S1]: template fit in 7 mass bins from 170 GeV to >1000 GeV. A_FB and A0 are pT- and y-integrated per mass bin, and the result is not unfolded (S1, Sec. 3, lines "the measurement is not unfolded to parton-level mass bins"). Table 2 gives A_FB in the >1000 GeV bin as 0.638 +- 0.052 (stat) +- 0.011 (syst). Table 3 gives A0 in the >1000 GeV bin as -0.219 +- 0.107 (stat) +- 0.050 (syst), and 0.086 +- 0.008 +- 0.022 in 170-200 GeV.
- **Full A0-A7 measurements exist only around the Z peak**:
  - CMS, 140 fb-1, 81-101 GeV, double-differential in pT and y [S2, abstract].
  - ATLAS 8 TeV Z-peak measurement [S3, abstract]. It reports a significant deviation of A0-A2 from O(alpha_s^2) predictions and evidence for nonzero A5,6,7.

  The Z-peak SM Lam-Tung violation therefore already disagrees with fixed-order theory. That is a theory baseline problem for any high-mass A0-A2 BSM interpretation.
- **sin2theta running.** No collaboration result was found in the INSPIRE searches listed in Sec. 9. The phenomenology projection is [S4].

## 2. Cross section and yields
No absolute high-mass DY cross section was read from a source. The yields below are derived from published statistical uncertainties.
- **ESTIMATE (yield, m_ll > 1 TeV, 13 TeV, 138 fb-1)**: about 200-300 selected ee+mumu events.
  - Inputs: delta A_FB(stat) = 0.052 in the >1000 GeV bin [S1, Table 2], with A_FB of about 0.6.
  - Method: the counting relation delta A of about sqrt((1-A^2)/N) gives N_eff of about 0.64/0.052^2, which is about 240. Template fitting is about 20% better than counting [S1, Sec. 3], and dilution makes it worse, so this is an order-of-magnitude figure only.
- **ESTIMATE (HL-LHC, 3000 fb-1)**: luminosity scaling x21.7 gives about 5000 events above 1 TeV, before any 13 to 14 TeV cross-section gain (UNVERIFIED, not computed). Statistical errors scale by 1/sqrt(21.7), which is 0.21.
- **High pT_ll subset**: [S5] (Petriello and Simsek; 14 TeV, 3 ab-1, pT_ll > 100 GeV) chose bins "so that the relative statistical uncertainties in each bin are smaller than 10%" [S5, Sec. IV.A, Table II]. That implies at least 100 events per bin, with the last bin being m_ll in [2000, 2600] GeV, pT in [100, 7000] GeV. The number of usable (m_ll, pT_ll) cells above 1.5 TeV is therefore only about 1-4.

## 3. Projected statistical reach (ESTIMATES from scaling S1, 13 TeV cross section, same analysis)
| Quantity, bin | Run 2 stat / syst [S1] | HL stat (x0.21) | Regime at HL |
|---|---|---|---|
| A_FB, >1000 GeV | 0.052 / 0.011 | about 0.011 | stat about equal to syst |
| A0, 170-200 GeV | 0.008 / 0.022 | about 0.002 | systematics-limited (already in Run 2) |
| A0, 510-700 GeV | 0.036 / 0.034 | about 0.008 | systematics-limited |
| A0, >1000 GeV | 0.107 / 0.050 | about 0.023 | systematics-limited unless syst improves |

Reading of the table:
- More luminosity helps A0-A2 only above about 500 GeV, and even there the gain flattens once the statistical error falls to the Run-2 systematic floor.
- A5-A7 stay statistics-limited. The SM values are of order 1e-3 at high pT at the Z peak [S3, Table 1, e.g. A7 = 0.0014 in the 132-173 GeV pT_Z bin at NNLO]. The stat precision is of order 0.05-0.1 per 100-event bin (ESTIMATE from the moment variance, about sqrt(<f^2>/N) with <f^2> of about 0.2-0.3). An SM measurement is therefore impossible above the Z peak, and only large BSM effects are testable.

## 4. Published HL-LHC projections
- **A6/A7 and dim-8 CP-odd operators** [S5]: 14 TeV, 3 ab-1, statistics-only fit.
  - Non-marginalized 1D fits reach effective scales up to about 9 TeV.
  - The fully marginalized 7D fit reaches only about 1-2 TeV and shows "strong degeneracies" [S5, Sec. IV.A, abstract].
  - Caveat: systematics, detector resolution and unfolding are not included.
- **Lam-Tung A0-A2 with dipole operators** [S6]: HL-LHC pseudo-experiments at 14 TeV and 3000 fb-1, statistical error only, rescaled from ATLAS 8 TeV. This study is **Z-peak only** (80-100 GeV) [S6, numerical section, case II].
- **Light-quark dipoles** [S7]:
  - Existing LEP/SLC and Z+jet pT constraints already exclude the parameter space that could explain the observed A0-A2 excess [S7, abstract and "Violation of Lam-Tung relation" section].
  - HL-LHC pT_ll in [600, 3000] GeV with 5% syst improves the bounds by about x4.5 [S7, HL-LHC paragraph].
  - The pT spectrum itself is therefore a strong competitor to the angular-coefficient route for dim-6 dipoles.
- **sin2theta_W MSbar running** [S4]: 13.6 TeV, 3000 fb-1, triple-differential (m, |y|, cos theta_CS F/B).
  - Expected precision at HL-LHC is 0.7% at 133 GeV, 1.9% at 1000 GeV and 3.5% at 3250 GeV.
  - The SM values over the same range are 0.23323 at 133 GeV, 0.24350 at 1000 GeV and 0.24954 at 3250 GeV [S4, Table 2].
  - So the SM running from 133 GeV to 1 TeV (about 4.4%) is roughly 2 sigma per high-mass bin. Several bins combined give stronger evidence (ESTIMATE; no combined significance is quoted).
  - The largest uncertainty is the PDFs, and it varies by up to 50% between PDF sets in the last bin [S4, Sec. 4, Table 3].
  - Lepton systematics are assumed to fall by x4 relative to ATLAS 8 TeV, and luminosity is 1% [S4, Sec. 3].
  - The sensitivity comes mostly from the absolute cross section, not A_FB: the log-derivative is about 0.9 for sigma versus about 0.3 for A_FB at 1 TeV [S4, Sec. 2]. The "running" measurement is therefore really a high-mass absolute-cross-section measurement, exposed to luminosity, PDF and missing-higher-order (Sudakov EW) systematics.

## 5. Backgrounds
- At high mass the dominant background is dileptonic ttbar, then tW, diboson, tau tau and photon-induced events [S1, Sec. 5].
- These are controlled with an e-mu control region and a b-jet veto [S1, Sec. 5].
- Misidentified leptons (MisID) are estimated from data.
- The photon-induced fraction is 1.8% at 170-200 GeV and 4.1% above 1 TeV [S1, Table 5].
- The signal-to-background ratio is high. Backgrounds are a systematic issue, not a sensitivity killer.

## 6. Trigger
- Run 2 used single-muon and single-electron triggers [S1, Sec. 5].
- Leptons at m_ll > 170 GeV are far above any Phase-2 single-lepton L1/HLT threshold (qualitative; Phase-2 threshold values UNVERIFIED, not read).
- The trigger is not an obstacle. Efficiency systematics are negligible in Run 2: muon trigger 0.1e-3 and electron trigger 0.4e-3 on A_FB [S1, Table 1].

## 7. Pileup and detector upgrades
- **Pileup** was already a sizeable A_FB systematic in Run 2, at 2.8e-3, about 5% of syst [S1, Table 1]. It enters through isolation and the MET and b-veto used for ttbar suppression.
  - At <PU> 140-200 it affects isolation efficiency and the ttbar veto.
  - The angular variables are built from the two leptons, so cos theta_CS, phi_CS and pT_ll are themselves robust.
  - Phi-dependent coefficients at high pT_ll may need the recoil, but the pT_ll measurement does not.
- **Tracker extension (CMS Phase-2 tracker, ATLAS ITk)**:
  - The YR WG1 study for sin2theta_eff at 14 TeV finds that extending muon acceptance from |eta| < 2.4 to |eta| < 2.8 cuts the statistical uncertainty by about 30% and the PDF uncertainty by about 20% [S8, Sec. 4.4.5, Table 26 and text].
  - That study is at the Z peak. At high mass, forward leptons reduce dilution in cos theta, which helps A_FB, A4 and sin2theta. This is an HL-specific gain, but its size at high mass was NOT quantified in any source read.
- **Momentum resolution and charge ID at TeV** matter for the sign of cos theta. Run-2 muon resolution is better than 7% up to 1 TeV in the barrel [S1, Sec. 4]. An improved Phase-2 tracker would help (UNVERIFIED magnitude).
- **Timing detectors** (MTD/HGTD) and **HGCAL** have no direct impact, beyond pileup mitigation for isolation and for forward electrons (HGCAL).

## 8. Systematics (what limits each sub-target)
- **(1) A0-A2 at high mass.** A0 was systematics-dominated over most of the mass range already in Run 2 (A0 syst 0.022-0.05 [S1, Table 3]). The SM prediction of A0-A2 itself is in tension at the Z peak [S3]. Theory uncertainty on the SM Lam-Tung violation at high mass and pT will be the limiting factor for any BSM claim. A fully unfolded phi-dependent measurement also adds resolution and unfolding systematics that S1 avoided with its template method.
- **(2) A5-A7.** Statistics-dominated (S5 assumes stat only). Systematic offsets from detector-induced phi asymmetries (charge-dependent alignment) are not quantified (UNVERIFIED).
- **(3) sin2theta running.** PDFs (dominant) [S4], luminosity, EW Sudakov higher orders, and lepton efficiency at high pT.

## 9. Feasibility verdict
- **Experimentally distinguishable?** Yes. This is a clean dilepton final state with trivial triggering, and the existing CMS template machinery [S1] and Z-peak A0-A7 machinery [S2, S3] demonstrate the techniques.
- **What scales with luminosity:**
  - A5-A7 and the high-mass, high-pT A0-A2 cells are statistics-limited, so they gain roughly as sqrt(L).
  - The inclusive or low-mass A0 and the PDF-limited sin2theta(mu) points are systematics-limited, so they gain mostly from PDF and theory progress and from the forward acceptance.
- **Biggest obstacle.**
  - For BSM sensitivity in A0-A2 and A5-A7: very few events where the EFT effects grow (m_ll > 1.5 TeV and pT_ll > 100 GeV gives only about 1-4 bins of about 100 events at 3 ab-1 [S5, Table II]), combined with SM theory uncertainty on the Lam-Tung violation and strong EFT degeneracies [S5].
  - For running: PDF uncertainty [S4].
  - Dim-6 dipole interpretations face strong existing bounds from other observables [S7].
- **HL-specific advantage.** About 22x the Run-2 luminosity in the stat-limited high-mass, high-pT region, plus extended tracker acceptance (|eta| up to 2.8 and beyond), which reduces dilution and PDF uncertainty [S8]. The sin2theta running sub-target becomes meaningful only with HL statistics (1.9% at 1 TeV, versus 2.3% for Run 3 [S4, Table 2]).
- **Rating: 3/5.**
  - Sub-target (3) is realistically testable at about 2 sigma per bin, PDF-limited.
  - Sub-target (1) is a feasible measurement, but its BSM power is marginal: it is systematics- and theory-limited, and partly superseded by pT spectra.
  - Sub-target (2) is feasible only as a null test with limits on large dim-8 coefficients.
- **Confidence: medium.** No collaboration-level HL-LHC projection for high-mass Ai exists, and the yields are scaled estimates.
