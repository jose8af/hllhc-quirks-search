## Target evaluated
"CMS Higgs-portal (ggF/VBF/VH) emerging-jet/dark-shower search with hadronically decaying dark mesons of ~2-20 GeV at short proper decay lengths (~1-100 mm, in the tracker), using multi-displaced-vertex counting and Phase-2 displaced L1/HLT tracking."

## HL-LHC / Phase-2 documents
- CMS-PAS-FTR-18-018 (INSPIRE 1704286), "First Level Track Jet Trigger for Displaced Jets at HL-LHC": identifies a blind spot for ctau ~1 cm in H->phi phi->4j and shows L1-track extension to few-cm impact parameter gives large trigger gains. Benchmark is H->2 LLP, not dark showers.
- CMS Line Segment Tracking for displaced tracks at HL-LHC, arXiv:2609.26695 (extends displaced-track acceptance in r by >x4 vs ~8 cm). Technical, no physics projection for dark showers.
- Pheno HL-LHC projection: arXiv:2512.24814 (Higgs-portal gluon-rich dark jets, m_eta_d ~3 GeV, all six Higgs production modes, 14 TeV trigger menu, tracker EJ ctau 1 mm-1 m and CMS muon system 50 mm-100 m; projected BR(h->dark shower) O(1e-5-1e-2)). Also arXiv:2112.05690 ("Emerging jets displaced into the future"), arXiv:2103.08620 ("Triggering on emerging jets"), arXiv:2307.04847.
- Recasts: arXiv:2505.03058 (HV sensitivity of CMS muon-endcap search), arXiv:2512.24814 (recast of CMS EJ and MDS).
- No official CMS or ATLAS HL-LHC projection specific to Higgs-portal dark showers / emerging jets was found.

## What remains uncovered
1. **Hadronic dark-meson Higgs-portal dark showers with ctau ~1-100 mm decaying inside the tracker**: no official CMS or ATLAS result interprets tracker-based displaced-jet/DV searches in a dark-shower (many-LLP) model. CMS MDS (2402.01898) is the only official hadronic Higgs-portal dark-shower limit and is muon-system based (ggF only); its lower ctau edge in the plots is 1 cm.
2. **m_pi < 16 GeV with VH (ZH/WH) lepton trigger and multi-DV counting**: ATLAS 2107.06092 is the closest (ZH, >=2 DVs, ctau 4-100 mm) but only for 16-55 GeV, 2-body b-bbar LLPs with massive DVs.
3. **VBF / VH production of Higgs-portal dark showers**: not used in any official dark-shower result (CMS MDS dark-shower samples are ggF only; muonic CMS searches are ggF-dominated - production modes beyond ggF not verified for 2511.11888/2606.25840, UNVERIFIED).
4. **Phase-2 L1-track-based trigger projection for dark showers**: only H->2 LLP benchmark in FTR-18-018 and pheno projections.

## Adversarial caveats
- H->SS displaced-jet limits (CMS 2409.10806, ATLAS 2107.06092) already probe the same detector region and lifetime; a dark shower with m_pi 10-20 GeV decaying to b-bbar is partially captured, and a recast (not done by the collaborations) could close part of the "gap". The novelty is the interpretation/selection optimisation for many soft DVs and m_pi 2-10 GeV, not the detector region.
- The "ATLAS already preparing one" premise is supported only by a Master-thesis-based proceeding (prospect, Delphes). Not evidence of an approved ATLAS analysis; equally not evidence of absence of internal CMS work.
- Upcoming Run-3 CMS displaced-jet full-dataset results could add dark-shower interpretations; nothing public found (not proof of absence).
- Pheno projection 2512.24814 already provides the HL-LHC sensitivity estimate; residual novelty is experimental implementation.
- HEPData numbers for the 2402.01898 dark-shower limits at ctau = 1-10 cm not read (UNVERIFIED).

## Status
`constrained-but-region-open` (sub-labels: `searched-limited-regime` for the muon-system and H->2LLP searches; `LHC-not-projected-to-HL` for official projections). Target judged **partly open**: the ctau ~1-100 mm, m_pi ~2-16 GeV, hadronic, VBF/VH-tagged multi-DV dark-shower region lacks an official result; ggF H->dark-shower is covered only at long ctau (muon system), and H->2 hadronic LLP at 1 mm-1 m is covered with a different signal topology.

Confidence that the region is open: medium.
