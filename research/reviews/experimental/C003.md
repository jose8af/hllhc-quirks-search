# C003: Soft unclustered energy patterns (SUEP), experimental status review (Gate 4)

Reviewer: skeptical experimentalist. Date: 2026-10-06. Sources: INSPIRE (tools/inspire.py) and full-text PDFs (tools/arxiv_pdf.py). Full query list in C003.json (37 query strings, including forward citations of arXiv:1612.00850).

## Target evaluated
The target is displaced, hadronically decaying, isotropic SUEP with c*tau of roughly mm to m, from ggF- or VH-produced mediators. The second part is an HL-LHC (Phase-2 L1-track or scouting) projection of the prompt programme. Pair-produced or non-resonant SUEP is a side item.

## HL-LHC / Phase-2
- arXiv:2211.05720 (and the Snowmass version arXiv:2203.07314) is an HL-LHC track-trigger study using SUEP as a benchmark. It is truth-level. Sec. 2.2 says: "For simplicity, this study focuses on a scenario in which each dark meson decays promptly to a pair of light quarks". The benchmark has mS 125-1000 GeV and m=T=1 GeV (Table 1: pT>0.5/1/2 GeV, nTrack>100/150/200). The same section states: "Decay products may be also be displaced... ", which is an explicit unfollowed lead.
- I found no CMS/ATLAS Phase-2 physics projection (FTR/PUB) for SUEP. The CMS Phase-2 L1 TDR (INSPIRE 1819968) cites arXiv:1612.00850, but I did not check what it says about SUEP (UNVERIFIED). The CMS L1 Data Scouting Phase-2 documents (INSPIRE 3110965, 3065059) contain no SUEP projection that I could verify.

## Open region (assessment)
- **Prompt SUEP is well covered** in Run 2: ggF from 125 GeV to 2 TeV (offline and scouting), WH/ZH at 125 GeV, and a muon-rich channel at ATLAS.
- **Displaced hadronic SUEP is partly open.** Every SUEP search builds its multiplicity from PV-associated tracks and generates prompt signals. A SUEP whose dark mesons decay at mm to tens of cm loses those tracks. The displaced neighbours do not close this hole:
  - The CMS displaced-dimuon HV search is muon-rich.
  - Emerging-jet searches require energetic jets and probe mediators of roughly 600 GeV or more.
  - Muon-detector-shower searches cover the metre scale.

  No search or recast quantifies sensitivity to isotropic displaced soft-track multiplicity at mS of about 125 GeV to 1 TeV.
- **HL-LHC: LHC-not-projected-to-HL.** No official projection exists, only a prompt truth-level trigger study.
- **Pair-produced / non-resonant SUEP:** I found no experimental search.

Status: `searched-limited-regime`, with a partly open displaced sub-region and `LHC-not-projected-to-HL` for HL-LHC. Confidence that the target is open: medium.

## Caveats
- Some displaced-SUEP parameter space may already be picked up by CMS displaced-jet (arXiv:2409.10806, Run 3), MDS or ATLAS DV/CalRatio searches. I found no recast. Not finding one does not prove it is absent.
- Run-3 SUEP analyses may be in preparation (UNVERIFIED). Run-3 HLT displaced tracking could change the trigger picture.
- Signal modelling of displaced SUEP (thermal generator with lifetimes) is not benchmarked in any verified paper.
- An ATLAS hadronic prompt SUEP search was not found. That is a separate gap, not the proposed target.
