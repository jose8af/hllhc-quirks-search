# C015: Long-lived and flavour-violating ALPs from top/Higgs decays. Experimental status review (Gate 4)

Reviewer role: skeptical experimentalist. Sources are primary: INSPIRE through tools/inspire.py and PDFs through tools/arxiv_pdf.py. Review date: 2026-10-05.

## Target under test
Long-lived t -> q a with light ALPs (m_a ~ 1-35 GeV; the 2202.09371 benchmarks are 2 and 10 GeV). The ALP decays in the HCAL or the muon system (c tau ~ 0.01-10 m) in lepton-triggered ttbar events. The target also covers displaced mu mu / gamma gamma from top decays and prompt t -> q a with a -> gamma gamma / mu mu.

Recasts and phenomenology (not experimental results):
- arXiv:2404.06126 recasts the ATLAS DV+jets search arXiv:2301.13866 for t -> a q at m_a = 25, 40, 65 and 90 GeV, at 139 fb-1 and 3 ab-1 (Fig. 1). It states that the search "required the LLP's mass to be larger than 10 GeV" (Sec. 1). It also recasts CMS single top+jets and ATLAS single-top FCNC for prompt and invisible ALPs (App. B). It notes that the ATLAS 2403.15332 bound is weaker than its DV+jets recast by more than an order of magnitude in the coupling (Sec. 1).
- arXiv:2202.09371 (seminal) recasts single-top/top+jet searches (Sec. 3.2) and proposes the ttbar + HCAL (CalRatio-like log10(E_had/E_em) > 1.2, track veto) and muon-system search, with m_a = 2 and 10 GeV benchmarks at HL-LHC (Tab. 1, Sec. 4). It finds Br(t->aq) < 1e-4 reachable (abstract).
- arXiv:2306.08686 is an HL-LHC phenomenology projection for ttbar + displaced dimuon from top-associated ALP production (ttbar a via c_tt). It is production, not t -> q a decay.
- arXiv:2307.11154 is HL-LHC phenomenology for rare top decays to light singlets, including LLP remarks.

HL-LHC/Phase-2 projection documents: I found **no** ATLAS/CMS projection specific to t -> q + LLP. The HL-LHC BSM Yellow Report (arXiv:1812.07831) has no match for exotic-top-to-LLP in a text grep. Generic Phase-2 LLP tools exist (CMS L1 displaced-vertex track trigger, INSPIRE 2908980; ATLAS Phase-II MS dimuon GNN, INSPIRE 3185609). These are not signal projections.

LHCb/LEP: none relevant found for t -> q a (top production at LHCb is low; LEP had no tops).

## What remains open
1. **Light ALPs (m_a ~ 1-35 GeV) from t -> q a, decaying in the HCAL or muon system, in lepton-triggered ttbar events.** No ATLAS or CMS result interprets t -> q a below 40 GeV, and none exists for HCAL or MS decays. The CMS MS-shower search uses a MET trigger and the H->ss signal. The ATLAS CalRatio+W search has a lepton-triggered selection that a ttbar (t->qa) event could in principle pass, but it has no top-decay interpretation and treats ttbar as background. This sub-region is **open as an interpretation**. Whether the existing selections are already sensitive to it is UNVERIFIED (no recast below 10 GeV found). The 2404.06126 recast covers only m_a >= 25 GeV in the plotted benchmarks.
2. **Displaced mu mu / gamma gamma from t -> q a.** No top-decay interpretation was found in CMS displaced-dimuon, scouting or ATLAS dark-photon searches. Caveat: in the 2202.09371 quark-coupled ALP model, a -> mu mu and gamma gamma are loop-induced and subdominant (Sec. 2, Fig. 1), so these channels need extra lepton/photon couplings to matter. Generic dimuon-scouting limits might be reinterpretable (UNVERIFIED).
3. **Prompt t -> q a, a -> gamma gamma / mu mu (light a).** No ATLAS/CMS search found. The closest are E5 (X->bb, 20-160 GeV) and E6 (ttbar phi production, 15-350 GeV, multilepton SRs that could catch t->q phi(mu mu) + W->l, but are not interpreted). This is open, with moderate confidence.
4. **Higgs-portal part**: well covered (E1, E3, E9, E10, and CMS/ATLAS H->LLP summaries). It should be dropped from the target.

## Status label
`searched-limited-regime`: t -> a q is searched only at m_a = 40 and 55 GeV with ID DVs. The light-mass, calorimeter/MS-decay region is `constrained-but-region-open`. HL-LHC sensitivity exists only in phenomenology (2202.09371, 2404.06126, 2306.08686), so the label `LHC-not-projected-to-HL` also applies to the experiment side.

## Assessment of the proposed target
**Partly open.** The light-mass (1-35 GeV), HCAL/MS, lepton-triggered ttbar region has no experimental interpretation. Displaced and prompt gamma gamma / mu mu from top decay also show no experimental interpretation. I agree with the target's own caveat: much of this could come from adding a t -> q a signal to existing ATLAS CalRatio+W/Z (2407.09183) and 1-lepton DV (2403.15332) selections, or CMS MDS. A new trigger stream is not obviously required. CMS MDS uses a MET trigger, so a lepton-triggered CMS MDS selection would be the more genuinely new piece. The opportunity is therefore mainly "new signal interpretation + HL projection" and only partly a new analysis.

## Key caveats
- ttbar background in the 1-lepton CalRatio/MDS channels is large, and existing selections may have low acceptance for soft (few-GeV) ALPs from top decay (ALP pT ~ m_t/2 boost). This is UNVERIFIED.
- Absence of a top-decay interpretation is not proof of absence of sensitivity. ATLAS/CMS may have internal or ongoing reinterpretations. Run-3 papers (e.g. CMS MDS pair INSPIRE 3181328) were checked only at abstract level.
- Meson-decay (B -> K a, K -> pi a) bounds constrain flavour-violating couplings involving light quarks at low m_a. The relation to t-c-a couplings is model-dependent and was not verified here.
