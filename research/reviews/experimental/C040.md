# C040 - Stau coannihilation corridor / light sleptons / Z'-mediated sleptons
Gate 4 experimental-status review (skeptical experimentalist). Date: 2026-10-06.
Scope: target (a), a collaboration-level HL-LHC/Phase-2 projection or analysis for compressed direct stau pairs.

## Verdict
**Status:** searched-limited-regime + LHC-not-projected-to-HL. The target is **partly open**.
**confidence_open:** medium.

The facts quoted in the proposed target check out against the primary sources:
- ATLAS 2609.37215: the ISR channel alone excludes up to 115 GeV at Delta m = 30 GeV; the combination sets no exclusion.
- CMS 1910.01185: 77.2 fb-1, interpreted mainly for chargino/neutralino decays via stau.
- The HL-LHC YR stau projections are sensitive only for Delta m > 100 GeV.

The open item is the collaboration projection and Phase-2 analysis. It is not open as an analysis concept, because ATLAS has just run exactly this strategy on Run 2+3 data.

## Assessment
- **No collaboration HL-LHC projection for compressed direct stau was found.**
  - The 2018 YR explicitly leaves the compressed region as a suggestion.
  - The ESPPU-2026 highlights document (2504.00672) contains no stau projection in its text.
- **Prompt coverage is weak for Delta m about 10-60 GeV.**
  - Run 2 + partial Run 3 has no combined exclusion.
  - The ISR-only exclusion is limited to m <= 115 GeV at Delta m = 30 GeV.
- **Delta m below 10 GeV (outside the ATLAS grid) is not covered by prompt searches.** This was not checked against long-lived/disappearing-track stau coverage in detail.

Adversarial points:
1. ATLAS 2609.37215 is the template. An HL extrapolation is incremental, and the full Run-3 analyses (ATLAS, and CMS per the review 2510.17971: "analyses are already in preparation ... direct slepton and stau production ... especially in compressed mass regions") may precede any projection.
2. The physics value depends on Phase-2 L1/HLT low-pT tau_h performance. A CMS Phase-2 ML L1 tau trigger exists (INSPIRE 2751742), but its quantitative low-pT efficiency was not checked (UNVERIFIED).
3. Novelty is experimental-projection only. Pheno projections already exist (1704.04669, 2308.05723, 2403.18991, per the seed; their IDs were matched by WebSearch titles, not read here).

Z' -> slepton (other sub-ideas): no ATLAS/CMS search was found. Only phenomenology exists (2608.18321, 1206.3513). This was out of scope for target (a).
