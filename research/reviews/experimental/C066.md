# C066: Experimental status review (Gate 4)

**Target under test:** resonant lepton-PDF single-LQ production with charm-tagged jets (e/mu + c-jet). Also in scope: vector-LQ and e-mu-LFV interpretations of the lepton+jet resonance, and a collaboration-level HL-LHC projection.

**Verdict:** PARTLY OPEN, but the remaining gap is narrow. Confidence that the target is open: **low**.
Status labels: `one-observable-not-another` (no c-tag), `indirectly-covered` (charm couplings via reinterpretation), `LHC-not-projected-to-HL` (lepton-induced channel).

## What is covered
1. **ATLAS, arXiv:2507.03650 (JHEP 12 (2025) 180)**
   - Data: Run 2 at 140 fb^-1 (13 TeV) plus Run 3 2022-23 at 55 fb^-1 (13.6 TeV).
   - Channels: e+light, mu+light, e+b and mu+b. Each channel has a 1L SR and a 2L SR (same flavour), binned in m(lj) (Table 2, Table 3).
   - Trigger and selection: single-lepton triggers; lepton and jet pT > 130 GeV.
   - Jet flavour: b-tagging uses the GN2 85% working point. "Light-jet" means not b-tagged. **There is no c-tagging.**
   - Benchmark: S~1 with a single coupling y_de, y_smu, y_be or y_bmu.
   - Limits: up to 3.4 TeV at y_de = 1, and 4.3, 3.1 and 2.8 TeV for y_smu, y_be and y_bmu = 3.5 (abstract; Figs 14-17).
   - Sec. 1 states: "Alternative models featuring couplings to u/c-quark flavours show detector-level signatures kinematically indistinguishable with the studied scenarios, permitting phenomenological re-interpretation through coupling parameter rotation."
   - Sec. 9 mentions future HL-LHC analyses only in general terms.
2. **CMS, arXiv:2608.28377 (CMS-EXO-24-005; PAS at INSPIRE 2967962)**
   - Data: 138 fb^-1 at 13 TeV. Muon channel only (events with electrons are vetoed).
   - Categories: 1mu or 2mu, crossed with BDT loose/tight and 0b/1b (DeepJet), giving 8 categories (Table 2). Fit is to M(mu j).
   - Excluded: M 1.5-3.6 TeV for lambda 0.2-0.6 in LQ(u mu); M > 1.8 TeV for lambda > 1 in LQ(b mu) (Fig. 7, Sec. 10).
   - Sec. 1 states: "Scenarios with couplings to strange or charm quarks would yield intermediate sensitivities, bracketed by these two benchmarks." No explicit charm limit is given.
3. **CMS, arXiv:2308.06143 (PRL 132 (2024) 061801):** tau-quark-induced LQ in tau + jet + MET, with btag/no-btag categories. This covers the tau sector, so tau versions of the idea are already covered.
4. **ATLAS, arXiv:2604.19495:** generic e+jet and mu+jet high-mass spectrum with 164 fb^-1 of Run 3 at 13.6 TeV, interpreted as quantum black holes. I read only the abstract. It could be recast, but it has no LQ interpretation.
5. **Review, arXiv:2505.08738 (Neutelings, ATLAS+CMS proceedings, May 2025):** Tables 1-4 show no single-LQ e/mu + j/c entries. Sec. 4 lists "electron or muon accompanied by a light- or charm-flavored jets" as an opportunity.
   - This statement **predates** arXiv:2507.03650 and arXiv:2608.28377, so the light-jet part is now superseded.
6. **Theses:**
   - INSPIRE 3183811 (Buchin 2026, ATLAS e+light-jet, Run 2 + Run 3)
   - INSPIRE 3153108 (Lux 2025, ATLAS mu+light-jet)
   - INSPIRE 3132656 (proceedings)

   I checked these at abstract level only. No charm channel was seen.

## What remains open (adversarial assessment)
- **c-tagged category (the proposed target).** Experimentally it is not done. However:
  - Both collaborations say charm couplings are already covered by reinterpretation (ATLAS) or bracketing (CMS).
  - At the GN2 85% WP, true c-jets fall into both the light and the b categories. The size of the c-mistag rate is UNVERIFIED.
  - The c-PDF suppresses the signal. arXiv:2005.06475 (conclusions) says couplings to s or c "are more difficult to constrain."
  - So the added value of a c-tag is a possible S/B gain against the W/Z+light background. That gain has not been demonstrated by anyone (UNVERIFIED). A pheno study would have to show a meaningful gain before this can count as an analysis gap.
- **CMS electron channel and CMS Run 3:** not found. These would be routine extensions, and an update may be in preparation (not found ≠ absent).
- **Vector LQ, e-mu LFV (e-induced production with mu+j decay), and joint coupling scans:** no experimental interpretation was found. The 1L SRs of the existing searches already accept these topologies, so this is a reinterpretation-level gap.
- **HL-LHC:** no ATLAS/CMS PUB, FTR or Yellow-Report projection of lepton-induced LQ was found. The only projection found is the pheno one in arXiv:2005.06475, Figs 3-4, which includes q = u, d, s, c.
  - Correction to the target text: "5.7 (4.2) TeV" is the mass up to which the HL bounds would surpass the Q_W constraints for LQ-e-u (LQ-e-d). It is not a mass reach.
