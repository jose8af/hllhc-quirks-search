# C008: Enriched semi-visible jets (photons, ISR), experimental status review (Gate 4)

Reviewer: skeptical experimentalist. Date: 2026-10-06. Sources: INSPIRE (tools/inspire.py) and full-text PDFs (tools/arxiv_pdf.py). The full query list is in C008.json (>15 formulations: semivisible/semi-visible, dark quarks, boosted, ISR photon, neutral EM fraction, HL-LHC, plus forward citations of 2407.08276 and 2409.04741).

## Target evaluated
- (i) Photon-enriched SVJs (SVJγ: non-isolated photons, high neutral-EM-fraction jets) from s-channel Z'.
- (ii) Low-mass (~200-700 GeV) Z' -> SVJ SVJ tagged by an ISR photon.

The lepton- and tau-enriched SVJ variants are assumed to be taken. That is checked below.

## Pheno sources for the target
- **arXiv:2407.08276** (Cazzaniga, Russo, Sitti, de Cosa, 2024) proposes SVJγ through a Z' plus a light pseudoscalar (ALP) portal. Abstract: the jets "evade the phase space probed by current LHC searches exploiting jets or photons due to the expected high jet neutral electromagnetic fraction and photons candidates non-isolation". Sec. 6 and Fig. 5 state that "the inclusive CMS SVJ search [18] has no sensitivity to SVJγ signals". The paper proposes dropping the NEMF jet-ID cut and adding a DNN tagger. It has 4 citations and no experimental follow-up found.
- **arXiv:2409.04741** (2024) covers photon-ISR and jet-ISR SVJ. Sec. 1 says that ISR dijet searches reach mediator masses "from 700 down to 200 GeV [28,29]", citing CMS arXiv:1911.03761 and ATLAS arXiv:2403.08547. It also says: "The ISR production channel has not yet been explored in the context of dark QCD searches". The benchmark has dark hadron mass 20 GeV, photon pT>150 GeV and CMS-like simulation. The forward citation arXiv:2604.20456 (2026, pheno) does rinv regression in photon+SVJ events and is not experimental.

## Open region (assessment)
- **(i) SVJγ: `proposed-not-searched`.** No dedicated search exists. Both CMS s-channel SVJ searches explicitly veto events with a high photon-fraction leading jet (f_γ>0.7, pT>1 TeV) and use standard jet ID. Lepton/τ-enriched SVJ is now covered (CMS arXiv:2608.05323), so the photon variant is the remaining enriched channel.
- **(ii) Photon-ISR low-mass SVJ: `proposed-not-searched`, with partial indirect coverage.**
  - No SVJ search reaches below about 1.5-2 TeV (CMS mT>1.5 TeV, ATLAS grid ≥2 TeV, CMS reinterpretation axes ≥ ~1.5 TeV).
  - Visible dijet+γ searches (ATLAS 200-650 GeV) would constrain only the small-rinv limit, and no SVJ recast was found.
  - The jet-ISR variant is in preparation at CMS (thesis), which is a competing/neighbouring effort.
- **HL-LHC: `LHC-not-projected-to-HL`.** I found no CMS/ATLAS SVJ projection document.

Confidence that the target is open: medium.

## Caveats
- The CMS jet-ISR boosted analysis could be extended to photon-ISR. Run-3 SVJ plans are unknown.
- The SVJγ authors are CMS members. The 2026 ETH thesis (INSPIRE 3131301) cites 2407.08276 and might contain a photon-SVJ study. Its content is UNVERIFIED.
- The ATLAS anomaly-detection region (2505.01634) might retain partial SVJγ acceptance, depending on jet cleaning. UNVERIFIED.
- SVJγ needs an extra ALP portal. Constraints on that portal were not reviewed. The photon-ISR channel pays an α_em rate penalty. Sensitivity claims are pheno-level.
