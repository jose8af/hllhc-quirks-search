## HL-LHC / projection documents
- arXiv:1902.04070 (YR WG1, Sec. 4.4.5): sin2theta_eff projections from Z-region A_FB only (ATLAS, CMS, LHCb). No high-mass angular-coefficient or running-sin2theta projection found in that document (grep of the full text).
- CMS proposal for weak mixing angle at HL-LHC (INSPIRE recid 1643433, 2017; CMS PAS FTR-17-001 per YR ref [478], UNVERIFIED number): Z-region.
- arXiv:2302.10782 (theory/pheno): Run-3 (13.6 TeV) and HL-LHC 3 ab-1 projections of sin2theta_W^MSbar(mu) from high-mass NC DY; systematic assumptions extrapolated from ATLAS 8 TeV high-mass DY (Sec. 3).
- arXiv:2511.19617 (pheno): A6, A7 at HL-LHC 3 ab-1, mass bins [170,350] and [350,1000] GeV, pT_ll>100 GeV, statistical only (Sec. on numerics, ~l.527-534 of text).
- arXiv:2608.04437 (pheno, 2026): CP-violating dipole interference in A5-A7 using existing ATLAS+CMS (Z-peak) data.
- arXiv:2512.05018 (pheno): A0-A2 to isolate chirality-breaking operators; Sec. 3 "Experimental status" table states only one A_i measurement per experiment exists (but lists CMS 2015 as A0..A7; CMS 1504.03512 is actually A0..A4 per its abstract).

## Open region (precise)
1. Phi-dependent, unfolded A0, A1, A2, A3 (and A0-A2) **above the Z peak**, double-differential in m_ll (>~150-170 GeV) x pT_ll; no collaboration measurement found. The CMS 2202.12327 A0 is a single pT/y-integrated template parameter per mass bin with average pT 38-88 GeV, so it does not test Lam-Tung at high pT.
2. A5, A6, A7 above the Z peak (any mass bin >101 GeV): none found.
3. Gap 150-170 GeV in CMS 13 TeV A_FB/A4 coverage (2408.07622 stops at 150, 2202.12327 starts at 170) — minor.
4. Experimental extraction of sin2theta_W^MSbar(mu) running: none found at collaboration level.

## Adversarial caveats
- Sub-target (3a) A_FB at high mass is measured; any proposal framing it as unmeasured fails.
- The SMEFT targets in (1),(2) are O(1/Lambda^4) (non-interfering / dim-8) effects; projected reach is statistics-limited and the pheno studies (2511.19617) use stat-only uncertainties. Experimental value hinges on QCD O(alpha_s^3) Lam-Tung modelling, which is already discrepant at the Z peak (ATLAS 1606.00689).
- 2003.11615's "novel angular dependence" concerns l=3 spherical harmonics beyond the standard A0-A7 basis — distinct from (1)/(2); an A0-A7 measurement would not capture it.
- A running-sin2theta extraction from CMS 2202.12327 public data (HEPData) by theorists is possible; not checked whether such a recast exists beyond 2302.10782 citers listed (none found among citers).
- CMS 2202.12327 Tab. 3 >1 TeV A0 is ~2 sigma low vs aMC@NLO (my arithmetic from table) — a motivation, not evidence.
- Absence in INSPIRE/web is not proof that no CMS/ATLAS analysis is in preparation (internal analyses are invisible).
