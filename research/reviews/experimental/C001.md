# C001 - Quirks / quirkonium: experimental status review (Gate 4, skeptical experimentalist)

**Proposed analysis target under test:** a dedicated ATLAS/CMS central-detector search for colour-neutral, EW-charged quirks using coplanar non-helical hits. It targets mQ above the FASER reach in the mesoscopic regime Lambda ~ 0.1-100 keV, and it would need custom Phase-2 tracking and HLT. Secondary targets: (a) a quirkonium dijet/diboson resonance plus a low-pT track excess (2506.11192); (b) a Wgamma/WZ resonance plus a displaced glueball vertex (2504.02940 Sec. 4).

**Status label:** `searched-limited-regime` overall. The primary sub-target is `proposed-not-searched` at ATLAS/CMS. The only LHC-experiment quirk search is forward (FASER) and stops at about 180 GeV.
**Confidence the primary target is open:** high for "no ATLAS/CMS dedicated search exists"; medium for "the region is not covered indirectly" (see caveats).

**Other experiments.** A LHCb VELO-based quirk proposal exists (arXiv:2601.09023, pheno, 2026): planar back-to-back hit patterns, projections for 10 fb-1 (2026) and 300 fb-1 (Upgrade II). No LHCb collaboration search was found. MoEDAL is not found in quirk searches. The CMS 0T-data proposal (1703.00912) has not been followed by any CMS search I could find.

**Collaboration-level statements.** The CMS dark-sector review arXiv:2405.13778 mentions quirks only as model context (4 hits, all theory). The 2026 theory review arXiv:2606.30760 (Sec. on quirks, p. 39) says: "Quirks were targeted in one Tevatron search [495], but there are no dedicated LHC searches." It cites the FASER result only as preprint context. The LLP white paper 1903.04497 (Sec. 3.5.2) says: "there are no current public searches by the LHC collaborations" for intermediate string lengths.

## 2. Phenomenology defining the proposed target (not experimental coverage)
- Knapen, Lou, Papucci, Setford, arXiv:1708.02243: coplanar-hit search in the ATLAS/CMS inner tracker at <mu> = 50 pile-up. With 300 fb-1 it gives discovery potential for **Lambda between 100 eV and 30 keV**, with coloured (EW-charged) quirks up to 1600 (650) GeV. It needs a MET > 200 GeV trigger (or a proposed HLT track trigger). It assumes |eta| < ~1.8 fiducial coverage. (Abstract; Sec. II.B trigger; Sec. II.C plane-finding.) **No HL-LHC (200 PU) version found.**
- Sha et al., arXiv:2410.00269 (hep-ex, PRD 114 (2026) 015005): ML (GNN) reconstruction of quirk tracks. Proof of concept on a generic 8/25-layer detector, benchmark mQ = 500 GeV, Lambda = 500 eV. Not a search and not ATLAS/CMS simulation. Condren and Whiteson, arXiv:2509.08878: model-agnostic non-helical tracking, also a proof of principle.
- 2506.11192 (quirk SUEP): microscopic strings, 140 fb-1 at 13 TeV, **no pile-up added** (text near line "No pile-up events were added"). Its HL-LHC viability is untested.
- 2504.02940: claims that a large region of EW-doublet squirk parameter space "will not be probed by the currently implemented search strategies" even after HL-LHC (Sec. 1). The proposed resonance + displaced glueball method is for HL-LHC (Sec. 4).

## 3. Open region (adversarial assessment)
- **Genuinely open at ATLAS/CMS:** colour-neutral EW-charged quirks with mQ above ~180 GeV (the FASER reach at Lambda = 10 keV) for Lambda ~ 0.3-30 keV. Here the oscillation amplitude is about um-cm, so tracks are non-helical and fail HSCP momentum/quality cuts. The Farina-Low recast gives **no** bound for (1,1)_-1 at Lambda = 1 keV, and only 150 GeV at 100 eV. No ATLAS/CMS search for non-helical/coplanar hits, either published or as a PAS/CONF, was found. The only LHC-experiment quirk result is FASER (forward, low-pT Drell-Yan pairs, mass reach < ~200 GeV).
- **Partly covered:** Lambda < ~300 eV (HSCP recast, colour-neutral up to 150-650 GeV depending on Lambda). Lambda > ~30 keV to 1 MeV (D0 107 GeV, FASER up to 100 keV; monojet recast for coloured quirks). Low-Lambda stopped quirks (CMS OoT recast). So the stated band "0.1-100 keV" is too generous at both ends. The defensible open band is roughly 0.3-30 keV for colour-neutral states with mQ > ~180 GeV, plus coloured quirks at Lambda >~ 1 keV above the recast bounds (500 GeV at 1 keV, (3,1)_2/3 fermion).
- **Secondary targets:** the Wgamma/WZ + displaced-vertex combination has no dedicated search. Inclusive Wgamma resonance limits from 300 GeV exist (CMS 2406.05737). Low-mass (100-300 GeV) Wgamma resonances lie below the CMS search range (0.3 TeV), so that region is not covered by it. The quirk-SUEP low-pT-track-anomaly method has no experimental implementation and no pile-up study.
- **HL-LHC projections:** no ATLAS/CMS Phase-2 projection for quirks was found. The HL/HE-LHC YR WG3 (arXiv:1812.07831) has **0 mentions of "quirk"** (full-text grep). The only HL-era projections are pheno ones: LHCb Upgrade II (2601.09023), FASER2/FPF (2404.13814, 2108.06748, 2510.26260), and HL-LHC claims in 2504.02940. The status is `LHC-not-projected-to-HL` for the central-detector part.

## 4. Key caveats
1. Feasibility is the real obstacle, not novelty. The target needs non-standard tracking at 200 PU and a trigger, either MET >~ 200 GeV (low efficiency for DY quirk pairs with little recoil) or a hardware track trigger. Knapen et al. studied only 50 PU. The Phase-2 L1 track trigger assumes helical tracks.
2. An SLAC ATLAS Confluence page titled "Quirks" exists (seen via web search; content not readable). An internal ATLAS effort may therefore exist or have existed. **UNVERIFIED**; "not found" does not mean absent. The ATLAS-affiliated ML tracking work (2410.00269) also hints at experimental interest.
3. FASER is analysing the remaining Run-3 data (2022-24 is about half). Its reach in mQ is limited by forward acceptance, so it is complementary, not competing, above ~200 GeV.
4. The exact shapes of the gray recast regions in FASER Fig. 5 were read from the axis labels and legend text only, not digitised. Mass values for those regions other than the Table II numbers in 1703.00912 are UNVERIFIED.
5. LEP limits on charged EW states (~100 GeV) are relevant but were not checked here (UNVERIFIED).
