# C019 - Doubly charged scalars: compressed, displaced, tau/W cascades
Gate 4 experimental-status review (skeptical experimentalist). Date: 2026-10-06.

## Verdict
**Status:** partly open. The labels differ by sub-target:
- (i) Displaced fermiophobic H++ (ctau about 0.1-100 mm): **indirectly-covered / not reinterpreted**. No collaboration search targets a same-sign or charged-parent displaced vertex. However, some displaced-lepton searches apply no charge requirement, so they have partial acceptance.
- (ii) Compressed triplet with soft same-sign leptons: **proposed-not-searched** (phenomenology only).
- (iii) Hadronic-tau H++: **searched-limited-regime**. The only CMS tau_h result uses 12.9 fb-1 (PAS), and ATLAS full Run 2 uses e/mu only.

**confidence_open:** medium.

The proposed target's claim that "the ATLAS displaced-dilepton-vertex search requires opposite charge" is correct (arXiv:2601.05664, title and abstract). It overlooks CMS 2110.04809, which explicitly makes **no charge requirement**.

## Assessment by sub-target
**(i) Displaced fermiophobic H++**
- The phenomenology paper 2608.27251 (Sec.3.2.2, p.12-15) states that the CMS displaced-vertex and displaced-lepton searches (refs 1811.07991, 2012.01581, 2110.04809, 2410.16835, 2601.05664) "cannot be reliably reinterpreted ... without a dedicated recasting". Its Figs. 3-4 show the gap at ctau ~0.1-100 mm.
- My objection: CMS 2110.04809 has charge-agnostic acceptance for prompt-vertex-less displaced lepton pairs. A H++ -> l+l+ (Yukawa case, m 100-150 GeV) or leptonic W+W+ signal at ctau 1-100 mm could already be partly constrained. This has not been demonstrated either way.
- Even so, a dedicated same-sign / charged-track-attached DV analysis is genuinely absent from collaboration output.
- The hadronic W+W+ displaced decay (multitrack DV with a charged parent track) is the cleanest open sub-region.

**(ii) Compressed triplet (Delta M 1-30 GeV)**
- No collaboration search was found.
- 2305.06889 (intro, citing 2108.10952) asserts that ISR/soft-lepton SUSY searches are insensitive. This was not independently checked; treat it as a pheno claim.
- 2603.15882 gives an ISR + soft-lepton 14 TeV strategy, so the open item is the experimental analysis/projection itself.

**(iii) tau_h**
- CMS tau_h H++ was last shown with 12.9 fb-1.
- A University of Helsinki MSc thesis (INSPIRE 3063898, May 2025, CMS group, supervisor S. Lehti) studies the 4 tau_h channel on 2018 MC. It found no expected exclusion for 2018 alone and projects about 470 GeV (Run 3) and about 730 GeV (HL-LHC) (thesis Sec.8, around line 2161 of the text extraction).
- This suggests a CMS tau channel may be in preparation (UNVERIFIED).

## Key caveats
1. A recast of 2110.04809 and 2410.16835 is needed before claiming the displaced regime is open.
2. Long-lived Yukawa-coupled H++ exists only for m < ~150 GeV (2608.27251 abstract). The fermiophobic case extends to TeV.
3. 2608.03988 (recast) argues that the ATLAS 2211.07505 4-lepton efficiency is unphysically high, so the published limits may be too strong.
4. 'Not found' is not proof of absence. Full-Run-2/Run-3 CMS H++ analyses could be internal.
