# C001: Quirks, theory deep-read (Gate 4)

**Proposed target being evaluated:** an ATLAS/CMS central-detector search for coplanar, non-helical hits from colour-neutral, EW-charged quirks with m_Q above the FASER reach (179 GeV at Lambda = 10 keV), for Lambda ~ 0.1-100 keV. It needs custom Phase-2 tracking and HLT.
**Verdict in one line:** the physics is sound and the signature is distinctive. The idea is phenomenologically **proposed-not-searched** at ATLAS/CMS, but it is not a new proposal (1708.02243, 2410.00269). The open problems are experimental: trigger, 200-PU tracking, and reach above roughly 200 GeV. They are not theoretical. The stated Lambda window is too wide: above ~30 keV the coplanar-hit method does not apply.

## 1. Claim and framework
- New gauge group SU(N_IC) with confinement scale Lambda, and vector-like Q with SM charges. The benchmark used by FASER is (1,1,-1,N_IC=2), i.e. right-handed-lepton-like charges [2607.26195, p.2]. If no IC-charged state is lighter than Lambda, string breaking is suppressed by ~exp(-m_Q^2/Lambda^2). The produced pair then stays joined by a flux tube of tension sigma = Lambda^2 [1708.02243 Sec.I-II; 0805.4642 Sec.3.1].
- Dynamics: a Nambu-Goto string with massive endpoints, S = -m_Q sum int d tau - Lambda^2 int dA + S_ext [1708.02243 Eq.2]. CM-frame oscillation amplitude: d_cm ~ 2 cm (gamma-1)(m_Q/100 GeV)(keV/Lambda)^2 [1708.02243 Eq.1; 2410.00269 Eq.1].
- Regimes [0805.4642 abstract; 1708.02243 Sec.I]:
  - Lambda <~ 100 eV: two separately reconstructable tracks, covered by HSCP-type searches.
  - ~100 eV to 10 keV: d ~ 0.1-10 cm, and no tracks are reconstructed by standard algorithms.
  - >~ 30 keV: the pair is unresolved and looks like a single straight, high-dE/dx track (D0-type).
  - MeV and above: prompt annihilation.
- Production and decay chain: q qbar -> gamma*/Z -> Q Qbar (Drell-Yan) -> oscillating pair that crosses the tracker in ns. A macroscopic-string pair does not annihilate in flight, because ionisation kicks change angular momentum too much [0805.4642 Sec.4]. If the pair stops, it annihilates late (out of time) [1811.08903]. IC glueballs (m ~ 6 Lambda) are the only IC hadrons. At keV scale they are invisible and long-lived [2512.20696 Sec.2].

## 2. Controlling parameters
- **m_Q** (sets the rate).
- **Lambda** (sets the regime).
- **SM representation and spin** (Farina-Low Tab.II: colourless scalars have much weaker limits than colourless fermions).
- **N_IC** (multiplies the cross-section).
- **Pair boost/ISR** (the lab-frame oscillation length differs strongly from Eq.1 event by event [2410.00269 Sec.II]).
- **eps**, the glueball-emission probability per crossing. It is a non-computable nuisance, set to 0.1 [1811.08903 Sec.3; 2607.26195 p.3].

## 3. Robust vs model-dependent
**Robust**
- EW Drell-Yan rate: FASER uses a 2-4% scale/PDF uncertainty [2607.26195 p.5].
- **Coplanarity of the hits.** This follows from the central force alone, provided the external forces are small, and holds for any central force [1708.02243 Sec.II].
- Low velocity and ~2x MIP charge for pairs.
- No in-flight annihilation for macroscopic strings.

**Model-dependent**
- Annihilation position and timing, stopped-quirk rates, and survival over long distances. These depend on eps, and the energy-loss treatment for stopped quirks is "highly uncertain" [1811.08903 App.].
- Trigger: there is no native quirk trigger. The analysis relies on ISR MET; eps_trig ~0.10 for DY at 800 GeV [1708.02243 Tab.I]. A dedicated HLT or a hardware track trigger is only suggested [1708.02243 Sec.III.B].
- Glueball radiation: Knapen et al. argue it is negligible at large separation, since acceleration ~Lambda^2/m_Q << m_glueball. Only ~Lambda of energy per close approach is emitted, which barely affects macroscopic trajectories [1708.02243 Sec.II]. This supports the robustness of the central-tracker signature.
- SUEP-like soft pions and quirkonium dijets (2506.11192) are a **different regime**: QCD-coloured quirks, Lambda' >~ 10 GeV, microscopic strings. That model has an ad hoc parameter E_frac in 0.2-1 and was simulated without pile-up [2506.11192 Sec.II-III].
- W gamma / WZ resonance plus displaced glueball (2504.02940) is also a different regime. It concerns natural squirk doublets under SU(3)', where glueballs of ~10+ GeV decay via Higgs mixing. Its projections are made at 13 TeV with 3 ab^-1 [2504.02940 Sec.4.2].

**Conclusion:** EW-charged quirks with m_Q ~ 0.2-0.7 TeV and Lambda ~ 0.1-30 keV are not excluded by any constraint I checked. The 30-100 keV portion of the target leads to a single HIP-like straight track, which is not a coplanar-hit topology.

## 5. Motivation and uncertainties
- **Motivation (3/5).** Quirks are a generic consequence of a hidden non-Abelian group with only heavy matter. Neutral-naturalness top partners (folded SUSY, quirky little Higgs) are quirks, but with GeV-scale Lambda [1512.05782]. keV-scale Lambda is justified only as "natural" in the log-flat sense, Lambda = mu0 exp(-8 pi^2/b g0^2) [0805.4642 Eq.1.2]. With GUTs plus extra IC fundamentals, Kang-Luty quote MeV, 10 keV and 100 eV examples [0805.4642 Sec.2]. So the mesoscopic window is a signature-driven target, not a naturalness prediction.
- **Uncertainties that limit a search.**
  - Trigger efficiency, which is the dominant limitation.
  - Event-by-event dependence of the trajectory on ISR and opening angle, which needs bespoke simulation (Geant4 quirk extension, as used by FASER).
  - eps, which matters only for stopped/OoT and far-forward signatures.
  - The pile-up hit density at <mu> ~ 200. Knapen et al. studied <mu> = 50, and 2410.00269 did not study pile-up at all [Sec.VIII].
- **Existing proposals to cite rather than re-claim.**
  - Coplanar-hit method: 300 fb^-1, discovery potential for 100 eV-30 keV, EW quirks to 650 GeV [1708.02243 abstract].
  - GNN tracking: N > 3 events per 300 fb^-1 for m_Q <~ 700 GeV and Lambda <~ 5 keV [2410.00269 Sec.VI].
  - LHCb VELO planar-hit search [2601.09023].
  - FASER2 at the FPF during HL-LHC, with forward TeV reach claimed [2404.13814 abstract].
  An HL-LHC ATLAS/CMS study would therefore be a novel **experimental analysis**, specifically a realistic Phase-2 implementation. It is not novel phenomenology.

## 6. Distinctiveness
No SM process or neighbouring BSM state (HSCP, R-hadron, monopole, multi-charge, disappearing track) produces two coplanar, mutually curving, slow, ~2x-ionising tracks. The backgrounds are random pile-up hit coincidences and conversions, which are claimed removable while keeping ~70% reconstruction efficiency [1708.02243 abstract and Sec.III]. Once observed, dE/dx and the hit pattern can measure m_Q and Lambda [1708.02243 App.A].
