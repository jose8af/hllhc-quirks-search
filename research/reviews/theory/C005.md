# C005: Theory deep-read. Higgs-portal emerging jets / dark showers (GeV dark mesons)

Reviewer: Gate-4 theory deep-reader (g4t1). Inputs used: only `candidates/gate4_targets.json` (C005 entry) and the primary papers listed below (full PDFs read via `tools/arxiv_pdf.py`).

## 1. Claim and framework
- **Target under evaluation:** CMS Higgs-portal (ggF/VBF/VH) dark-shower / emerging-jet search, with hadronically decaying dark mesons at m ~ 2-20 GeV and c*tau ~ 1-100 mm (tracker). It would use multi-DV counting and Phase-2 displaced L1/HLT tracking. The CMS MDS search (2402.01898) loses sensitivity in this regime, and the CMS displaced-jet search (2409.10806) is interpreted only for h->SS.
- **Lagrangian (as realised in the projection paper 2512.24814, Sec II):** SU(3)_d with one Dirac dark quark, a singlet "pseudoscalar" phi_p with i y' phi_p q-bar gamma5 q, and a scalar potential with mu_Hp phi_p |H|^2 and lambda_Hp terms. phi_p mixes with h (theta_p). In the IR, q-bar gamma5 q -> Lambda f eta, so eta_d mixes with h2 (theta_Lambda). eta_d then decays through Higgs-like couplings with width Gamma = 2.5e-7 x^2 GeV at m = 3 GeV, where x = sin(theta_Lambda) sin(theta_p) (Table I). Two production chains are considered: h -> h2 h2 -> 4 eta_d (cascade) and h -> q_d q_d-bar -> shower (DS1-3, varying Lambda_d/m_eta and probVector).
- **Generic framework (2203.09503 Sec 2.2.7; 2103.01238 Sec II-III):** the dark shower is produced through h, and the visibly decaying particle (VDP) decays through one of five "decay portals" (gluon, photon, vector, Higgs, dark photon). Once the portal is chosen, its branching ratios are fixed. The hadronisation is parameterised, not computed.
- **Alternative coherent framework (2110.10691):** TeV-scale EW-doublet fermions generate irrelevant Z- and Higgs-portal operators. CP-odd dark pions behave as composite ALPs (Z mixing) and CP-even ones mix with h. Sec 8: "dark pions with 2m_mu <~ m <~ 2m_b have lifetimes varying from a millimeter to 10 meters". The same section adds that "the CP-even dark pions remain out of reach due to their very small mixing with the Higgs".

## 2. Parameters and chain
pp -> h (ggF/VBF/VH/ttH) -> q_d q_d-bar -> dark shower -> {eta (visible, LLP), omega (stable or -> eta eta)} -> SM hadrons at a displaced vertex. The signal is controlled by:
- BR(h -> dark);
- m_eta;
- c*tau (x, f_eta or epsilon);
- xi_omega = m_omega/m_eta and xi_Lambda = Lambda/m_eta (CMS 2402.01898 uses (2.5,2.5), (2.5,1), (1,1));
- probVector;
- N_c, N_f;
- the portal choice.

## 3. Robust vs model-dependent
- **Robust:**
  - The rates come from SM Higgs production times one free BR.
  - The per-portal decay BRs are fixed.
  - Events contain several DVs, and the total visible mass is <= m_h.
  - For Higgs and gluon portals there is a steep lifetime-mass relation. The minimal natural lifetime is c*tau >~ 3 cm (5 GeV/m)^7 for the Higgs portal (2103.01238 Eq. 15) and c*tau >~ 7 cm (m_a/500 GeV)^4 (5 GeV/m)^7 for the gluon portal (Eq. 4). These are "not rigorous exclusions" (Sec II).
- **Model-dependent:**
  - Meson multiplicity and invisible fraction.
  - Track-level EJ variables for ~60 GeV dark jets.
  - Cascade vs shower topology.
  - Hadronic BRs of 2-4 GeV Higgs-like scalars, which are non-perturbative (1809.01876 abstract: earlier estimates "disagree by several orders of magnitude"; the dispersive treatment is used up to 2 GeV and the spectator model above).
  - 2203.09503 Sec 4.1.2: "Currently there is no theoretical insight into how these models or their parameters should be adjusted for different values of N_cD, N_fD, or m_piD/Lambda_D". probVector lies between 0.5 (QCD data) and 0.75 (spin counting).

**Net:** the target region survives, but its natural core is m_eta >~ 5-10 GeV with Higgs/gluon portals. That region is heavy-flavour-rich for the Higgs portal (2103.01238 Sec III D: tau tau and c c-bar always open, b b-bar mostly open). The 2-4 GeV, mm-lifetime gluon-rich benchmark of 2512.24814 lies below the minimal-UV lifetime bound. It is allowed only with tuning or the paper's specific three-scalar mixing construction. The Z-portal / CP-odd dark-pion route (2110.10691) gives mm-10 m naturally for 2m_mu-2m_b. There the dark showers are better initiated by Z (or h) decays, but Higgs mixing as the *decay* route is disfavoured.

## 5. Motivation and limiting theory uncertainties
- **Motivation (3/5):** neutral naturalness and hidden-naturalness motivations (2110.10691 Sec 1). Higgs exotic decays are a generic portal. Lifetimes in the LLP window arise naturally for portal-suppressed decays.
- **Limiting uncertainties:**
  - Dark hadronisation (multiplicity, vector fraction, invisible fraction). With Q = 125 GeV and few-GeV mesons the shower is short, so the Lund modelling is stretched; 2203.09503 notes that "it is not even clear that the Lund model is accurate" in some limits.
  - Low-mass hadronic decay modelling.
  - 2505.03058 abstract: results "depend on the underlying hadronization model ... we urge caution".
- **Recommendation:** an experimental search should present limits per portal on the (m, c*tau) grid of 2103.01238, with alternative hadronisation tunes as a systematic (the Snowmass report suggests "two or more tunes").

## 6. Distinctiveness
- **Against SM:**
  - DV multiplicity >2 per event, with vertex mass > m_B when m_eta >~ 5 GeV.
  - Dark-jet system near m_h (2512.24814 Fig. 6 for DS1/DS2), plus VBF/VH tags.
  - Backgrounds: B/D hadrons (vertex mass < 5 GeV), material interactions, merged pileup. The 3 GeV gg benchmark overlaps the B vertex-mass region, which weakens distinctiveness there.
- **Against BSM neighbours:**
  - h -> SS (2409.10806, 2402.01898) gives two DVs of fixed mass.
  - SUEP (C003) is isotropic and soft.
  - t-channel EJ comes with TeV-scale QCD-produced mediators and hard jets.

## 7. Specific issues found in the projection paper 2512.24814
1. Eq. (9)-(11) as printed: the eta-tilde diagonal mass entry is 0, so the lower eigenvalue of Eq. (11), (m_h2^2 - sqrt(m_h2^4 + 4 y'^2 f^2 Lambda^2))/2, is negative. In practice m_eta and x are treated as free inputs.
2. eta_d is a pseudoscalar bound state but decays through CP-even Higgs mixing. This needs explicit CP violation (the paper notes CP-violating effects), and no EDM check is given.
3. The HL-LHC background model is LO PYTHIA b b-bar only, without pileup (Sec IV A). The MDS projection assumes zero background (Fig. 17). The trigger thresholds in Table II (e.g. dijet 70/40 GeV) are not verified against the Phase-2 menu. The quoted reach of O(1e-4) to O(1e-5) is therefore optimistic (UNVERIFIED).
4. The seed's "largely unconstrained above a few GeV" quote (2203.09503 Sec 2.1) concerns *flavour* bounds on t-channel models, not Higgs-portal showers.

## 8. Lead noted (not pursued by forward citations)
- 2110.10691 Sec 7: "If the dark pions are heavy enough to decay inside the detector, the phenomenology becomes similar to the emerging jets scenario, albeit with EW rather than QCD production of the mediators. Evaluating the impact of the existing CMS search on our signals is beyond the scope of this work". The final state is W/Z/h + 2 dark jets from Drell-Yan Q Q-bar. At 13 TeV, sigma ~ N_d x 1.6 fb at M = 900 GeV (Eq. 7.1-7.2 text).
