# C003: Theory deep-read. SUEP / soft bombs (displaced, isotropic, hadronic variant)

Reviewer: Gate-4 theory deep-reader (g4t1). Inputs used: only `candidates/gate4_targets.json` (C003 entry) and the primary papers below (full PDFs read).

## 1. Claim and framework
- **Target under evaluation:**
  - Displaced, hadronically decaying, isotropic SUEP (dark-meson c*tau ~ mm-m) from ggF/VH-produced mediators: a cloud of soft displaced tracks/vertices that fails EJ jet requirements and is not muon-rich.
  - An HL-LHC (Phase-2 L1-track/scouting) projection of the prompt programme.
  - Pair-produced/non-resonant SUEP as a secondary item.
- **Mechanism (1612.00850 Sec II):**
  - A portal M^(4-D_vis-D_HV) O_vis O_HV (Eq. 6) feeds a hidden sector that is strongly coupled (lambda >> 1) over a wide window down to Lambda.
  - Multiplicity: <n> ~ (Q/Lambda)^(1+O(1/sqrt(lambda))) (Eq. 3, assuming Gribov-Lipatov). AdS arguments imply sphericity at leading order.
  - Hadron momenta are modelled as Maxwell-Boltzmann with T ~ Lambda (Eq. 4); the benchmark is m_phi = 1 GeV, T = 0.5 GeV (Eq. 5).
  - Decay: phi -> A'A' via phi A'A'-tilde, with A' kinetically mixed (epsilon >~ 1e-5 gives c*tau <~ 1 mm for m_A' >~ 250 MeV, Eq. 8 and text). Prompt by construction.
- **Higgs-SUEP:** produced via H^dagger H O_HV. 1612.00850 Sec II.C warns that "the Higgs vacuum expectation value will introduce a tadpole for O_HV, which generically breaks conformal invariance not too far from the weak scale". Fine-tuning or extra model building is needed. The alternative S S^dagger H H^dagger with m_S < m_h/2 leads to soft-bomb **pair** production.

## 2. Parameters and chain
pp -> S or h (ggF/VBF/VH) -> quasi-conformal hidden shower -> N ~ m_S/T dark mesons (thermal) -> SM. Controlling parameters:
- m_S, T_D, m_D, T_D/m_D;
- the decay portal and its coupling (which set c*tau and the final-state composition);
- BR(h -> SUEP);
- the invisible fraction.

Because the dark mesons carry p ~ T ~ m_D, beta*gamma ~ O(1) in the mediator frame, so the lab decay length is about c*tau.

## 3. Robust vs model-dependent
- **Robust:**
  - High multiplicity of soft (sub-GeV to ~GeV) particles whose total energy is bounded by the mediator mass.
  - Multiplicity grows with m_S/T.
  - Isotropy in the mediator frame at leading order in 1/sqrt(lambda).
  - For gluon/Higgs decay portals, GeV dark hadrons are generically long-lived. 2107.12379 Sec 2.3: "the simplest gluon portal scenarios suggest that dark hadrons lighter than ~10 GeV have macroscopic decay lengths". Also 2103.01238 Eq. 4 and 15. **Displacement is therefore the more generic expectation for these portals**, and the prompt hadronic SUEP needs, e.g., a hadrophilic vector portal (2107.12379).
- **Model-dependent:**
  - Sphericity. 2203.09503 Sec 3.1.1: "For an observably spherical SUEP, one may need N_cD ~ 100".
  - The thermal spectrum ("necessarily somewhat ad hoc"; "T_D/Q_c should be varied").
  - Fluctuations at low visible multiplicity.
  - Final-state composition via m_A'.
  - The lifetime, which is not tied to the production rate in the simplified model.
  - Pair/non-resonant topologies, for which no worked benchmark was found.

**Net:** the displaced-SUEP target is theoretically motivated: displacement is the generic outcome for gluon/Higgs portals. But the natural parameter region splits into two pieces:
- m_D ~ 1-3 GeV with long lifetimes (>~ m). These decay mostly beyond the tracker.
- m_D ~ 5-10 GeV with cm lifetimes. These have modest multiplicity from a 125 GeV mediator and overlap the C005 dark-shower regime.

The "mm-m, isotropic, high-multiplicity, hadronic, from Higgs" corner needs either a heavier mediator (larger m_S/T) or a tuned or non-minimal decay portal.

## 5. Motivation and limiting theory uncertainties
- **Motivation (3/5):** strongly coupled hidden valleys, a trigger "nightmare" scenario, and Higgs exotic decays. The theoretical existence of SUEP-producing confining theories is established only for a few SUSY cases with N_f >> N_c (2203.09503 Sec 3.1.1). The SUEP vs 5d-KK-cascade interpretation is disputed.
- **Limiting uncertainties:**
  - No first-principles generator; the thermal ansatz and the T_D/m_D choice set the track-pT spectrum near reconstruction thresholds, and therefore the efficiency.
  - Uncharacterised deviations from sphericity.
  - Poisson asymmetries when few mesons decay visibly.
  - For displaced variants, non-perturbative GeV-hadron decay widths and BRs (1809.01876).
- An experimental search should quote limits on a (m_D, T_D, c*tau) grid and vary the spectrum shape (e.g. thermal vs 5d simplified models, 2009.08981, not read).

## 6. Distinctiveness
- **Against SM:**
  - Many soft tracks with significant impact parameters forming multi-track vertices (vertex mass > m_K).
  - Isotropy in the mediator frame. Displacement suppresses the prompt pileup and underlying-event confusion that plagues prompt SUEP (1612.00850 Sec I).
  - Dangerous SM fakes: K_S/Lambda decays (cm decay lengths), conversions and nuclear interactions in material, heavy-flavour decays, merged pileup vertices.
- **Against BSM neighbours:**
  - Emerging jets are collimated, with a jet requirement.
  - Higgs-portal dark showers (C005) have jet-like DV groups.
  - h -> SS gives two DVs.
  - Quirks give anomalous tracks.
  - The SUEP to dark-shower boundary is continuous, so separation rests on event-shape variables (isotropy, multiplicity), not a sharp observable.
