# C066 - Resonant lepton-quark-fusion leptoquarks (charm-tag target): theory deep-read (Gate 4)

Reviewer: theory deep-reader (g4t4). Source papers were read from the PDF/text (cache/pdf). Numbers come from the sources unless they are marked UNVERIFIED.

## 1. Claim and framework
- **Lepton PDFs.** QED radiation generates charged-lepton PDFs in the proton. With the LUX method, i.e. structure functions from e-p data, they are known at NLO to <5% over most of the mass range (2005.06477 Sec. 6.1).
- **The process.** A minimal scalar LQ with a single coupling, L ⊃ λ_lq LQ*(E^c Q^c), Γ = λ²M/16π (2005.06475 Eqs. 1-2), is produced resonantly in l q -> LQ -> l q:
  - The rate scales as λ² × L_lq(M).
  - The signature is a narrow m(lj) peak with a forward, soft same-flavour lepton from γ -> l l splitting.
- **Proposed target:** e/μ + **charm-tagged** jet, plus vector-LQ and LFV interpretations and an HL-LHC projection. The 3rd-generation sub-ideas are judged dead or reinterpretation-level.

## 2. Assumptions and controlling parameters
- **Parameters:**
  - M_LQ.
  - λ, which sets both the rate and the width.
  - The quark flavour, through the PDF luminosity. The ordering is u > d >> s ~ c > b, and Q_W bounds apply only to u and d.
  - The lepton flavour. The m(lj) resolution is 1.6-2.3% for electrons and 6.7-17% for muons (2005.06475).
  - The LQ spin and chirality.
- **Assumptions:** BR(lq) = 100%, narrow width (valid for λ ≲ 1.5 at 2 TeV per ATLAS 2507.03650 Sec. 5), and λ perturbative (< √(4π)).

## 3. Robust vs model-dependent
**Robust:**
- The m(lj) resonance plus the forward second lepton (CMS 2608.28377 Sec. 1; ATLAS 2507.03650 SR-2L).
- Lepton-PDF uncertainty below 5% except at M/√s ≳ 0.3.
- Signal scale/PDF uncertainty below 10% (2005.06475 fn. 6).
- **Key point for the charm target:** the theory itself says u and c couplings give "kinematically indistinguishable" signatures and can be reinterpreted by flavour rotation (ATLAS 2507.03650 Sec. 1). CMS states that s or c sensitivity is "bracketed" by its uμ and bμ benchmarks (2608.28377 Sec. 1). So existing untagged light-jet results already constrain λ_cl once reinterpreted. Charm tagging adds two things:
  1. flavour identification of the coupling after a discovery;
  2. some extra background rejection against W/Z + light jets. The irreducible W+c (sg -> Wc) background stays.

**Model-dependent:**
- The large-x charm PDF, including possible intrinsic charm. It sets the l-c rate at 2-4 TeV, but its size was not quantified in any source read (UNVERIFIED).
- Vector-LQ (U1) rates, which depend on κ.
- Interference between resonant, pair and t-channel contributions at large λ.

## 4. Consistency and indirect constraints

| Constraint | Source | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| APV / Q_W | 2005.06475 (from 1810.10017); 2507.03650 Sec. 8.3 | λ_eq < 0.17 M/TeV for u and d only. The resonant search wins only below ~2.3 TeV (y_de). **No bound on c or s**, so the charm region survives |
| Pair production | 2507.03650 Sec. 1 | ~1.8 TeV (e) and 1.7 TeV (μ), roughly flavour-blind. The resonant mode wins for y_de ≳ 0.25, y_sμ ≳ 0.7, y_b ≳ 1.0, so charm needs λ ~ O(1) |
| DY tails (t-channel) | 2005.06475 Figs. 3-4; 2507.03650 Sec. 1 | Strong for u and d. For c, suppressed by the sea PDF; the quantitative bound is UNVERIFIED |
| LFV / FCNC | 2608.28377 Sec. 1 | Comparable couplings to two lepton flavours are excluded far beyond the reach, so the e-μ-LFV resonant interpretation is theoretically disfavoured. A single-coupling c-l LQ is flavour-safe at tree level |
| Perturbativity / width | 2507.03650 Secs. 5, 8.3 | s-μ and b-l mass reaches are quoted at y = 3.5, close to √(4π). Charm will also need O(1-3) couplings to go beyond pair-production reach, which is a theoretically marginal, broad-width region |
| g-2 motivation | 2608.28377 Sec. 1 | The updated SM prediction is consistent with experiment, so the μ-q LQ motivation now rests on generic arguments |

**Bottom line.** The charm region is not killed by indirect constraints, because APV does not apply and DY is PDF-suppressed. But it is only reachable beyond pair-production limits for λ ≳ O(1). There, width and perturbativity issues appear, and the theory motivation is generic rather than anomaly-driven.

## 5. Motivation and limiting uncertainties
- **Motivation 2/5:**
  - No current anomaly points specifically to (e/μ)-c couplings. The status of the b->s ll LFU ratios is UNVERIFIED here.
  - The g-2 motivation has faded (2608.28377).
  - R_D(*) points to τ couplings, which belong to the 3rd-generation sub-ideas.
- **Robustness 4/5:** the production mechanism and lepton PDFs are solid.
- **Main uncertainties:**
  - The large-x c/s PDF.
  - Heavy-flavour composition of high-pT W/Z + jets.
  - Broad-width line shape at λ > 1.5.
  - Parton-shower handling of incoming leptons. 2005.06475 used a photon replacement with an O(10%) effect; the experiments now use NLO POWHEG-RES.

## 6. Distinctiveness
- One hard lepton, one hard jet, low MET, a jet veto, and a forward soft same-flavour lepton.
- A c-tag would be the only resonance-level handle that distinguishes the u and c couplings.
- Incremental sensitivity over a reinterpreted light-jet search is likely modest. It is not quantified in any source (UNVERIFIED).

## 7. Notes on the target statement
- "2005.06475 Figs. 3-4: HL reach ~5.7 (4.2) TeV" is imprecise. These masses are where the HL resonant bound would **surpass the Q_W bound** for λ_eu (λ_ed). They are not a mass reach (2005.06475, text after Fig. 3 and Conclusions).
- The "remaining opportunities" sentence in 2505.08738 (Sec. 2 and Conclusions) is from May 2025. It predates ATLAS 2507.03650 and refers to LQ final states generally.
- I agree with downgrading the e-μ-LFV sub-idea on theory grounds (flavour constraints, 2608.28377).
- I did not deeply re-evaluate the 3rd-generation sub-ideas (b τ, mono-top, β_R/κ). CMS 2308.07826 was not read.
- Assessment: the charm target is better classed as **one-observable-not-another / reinterpretation-first**. A useful first step is a theory-level flavour-rotation reinterpretation of ATLAS/CMS light-jet results for λ_cl, before arguing for a dedicated c-tag analysis.
