# C015 — Long-lived, flavour-violating ALPs from top decays: theory review (Gate 4)

Reviewer role: Theory Deep-Reader. I read the full texts of 2202.09371, 2101.07803, the relevant sections of 2110.10698 (Secs. 2.3, 3.7, 3.8.4 and Fig. 23), 2403.15332 and the introduction of 1611.09355. Figure values below were read by eye and are approximate (to within a factor of a few).

## 1. Claim and framework
- **EFT:** L = ½(∂a)² − ½m_a²a² + (∂_μa/f_a)(c_uR)_ij ū_Ri γ^μ u_Rj, where c_uR is a hermitian matrix (2202.09371 Eq. 2.1–2.2). At the UV scale the ALP couples at tree level only to right-handed up-type quarks (the "charming ALP" of 2101.07803). The UV origins are flavoured dark-QCD sectors with scalar mediators, or Froggatt–Nielsen models in which only the RH up quarks carry charge.
- **Production and decay chain:** pp → tt̄, then one top decays as t → a q (q = u, c), with Br set by |(c_uR)_3q|/f_a (Eq. 2.3). Above 2m_c the ALP decays mainly to cc̄ at tree level, with the remainder going to gg. For m_a ~ 1–10 GeV, cτ reaches cm–m (2202.09371 Fig. 1).
- **Proposed search:** A leptonic top provides the trigger. The ALP is tagged in the HCAL with log10(E_had/E_em) > 1.2 and ≤ 2 tracks with pT > 2 GeV, or in the muon spectrometer. The authors quote a 2σ reach of Br(t → aq) < 1e‑4 with 350 fb⁻¹, and ~1e‑7 (Run 3) or ~1e‑8 (HL-LHC) if the search were background-free (Sec. 4.3, App. A). Recasts of single-top and top+jet searches leave intermediate lifetimes open: cτ ≈ 0.006–1 m at m_a = 2 GeV and ≈ 0.02–10 m at m_a = 10 GeV (Sec. 4.3).

## 2. Assumptions and controlling parameters
- **Parameters:** m_a; c_3q/f_a, which sets Br; and the diagonal couplings c_ii/f_a, which set cτ. Here c_cc and c_uu enter at tree level and c_tt through running, e.g. c_cc(m_c) ≃ c_cc(Λ) − 0.13 c_tt(Λ) (2110.10698 Eq. 3.45). Branching ratios depend only on c/f_a, apart from log(Λ) effects.
- **Simplification in 2202.09371:** all diagonal entries are set equal, as are all t–q entries. The paper then trades the coupling ratio c_ii/c_3q for (Br, cτ).
- **D-mixing requirement:** (c_uR)_12 must be essentially zero because of D0–D̄0 mixing (2101.07803 Sec. IV.A).

## 3. Robust vs model-dependent predictions
**Robust:**
- One boosted, narrow LLP accompanied by a light jet, with m(a + j) ≈ m_t, inside a tt̄ event.
- The tt̄ rate is precisely known.
- For m_a > 2m_c the ALP decays mainly to cc̄ (ATLAS uses Br(cc̄) ≈ 75%, Br(gg) ≈ 25%; 2403.15332).
- At a fixed coupling ratio, cτ ∝ 1/Br(t → aq).

**Model-dependent:**
1. **The cτ–Br map** depends on the UV flavour structure.
2. **Visible channels for 1 GeV ≲ m_a ≲ 2m_c:** the two papers disagree here.
   - 2202.09371 Fig. 1 (f_a = 1e6 GeV, quark-hadron duality): hadrons dominate, Br(γγ) ~ 0.3–0.6 and Br(μμ) ~ 1e‑3.
   - 2110.10698 Sec. 3.8.4 (universal c_u at Λ = 4π TeV): "Leptonic decay channels dominate for ALPs with masses 2m_e < m_a < few GeV".
   - Consequence: the displaced μμ/γγ sub-target is uncertain at the order-of-magnitude level, and above 2m_c it is negligible in the minimal model.
3. **Prompt a → γγ:** in this model the photon coupling arises only at one loop (2110.10698 Sec. 3.8.4). A sizeable a → γγ therefore requires an additional c_γγ, which is a different model.

**UV-consistency issue (my derivation, not stated in the papers).**
- In Froggatt–Nielsen models where the RH up quarks carry same-sign charges, c_uR = U†diag(Q)U is positive semidefinite. That implies |c_3q|² ≤ c_33·c_qq.
- In addition, c_tt feeds into c_cc through running.
- So without tuning, c_ii/c_3q ≳ 0.1–1.
- From 2202.09371 Fig. 1 (right), at fixed ratio cτ ∝ 1/Br:
  - Ratio 0.1: cτ ≈ 1–4 cm corresponds to Br ~ 1e‑6 at m_a = 2 GeV and ~1e‑7 at 10 GeV.
  - Ratio 1: the corresponding values are ~1e‑8 and ~1e‑9.
- Reaching Br ~ 1e‑4 with cm–m lifetimes requires ratios of ~1e‑2 (2 GeV) to ~1e‑3 (10 GeV).
- The ATLAS 40–55 GeV benchmark needs an even more extreme hierarchy.
- Dark-QCD completions, with couplings ~ κT^aκ† (an indefinite matrix), can produce off-diagonal ≫ diagonal only through special structure.
- **Conclusion:** the generic expectation for the signal is Br(t → qa) ≲ 1e‑6–1e‑8. That is the reach of a near background-free HL-LHC HCAL/muon-system analysis, not of a conservative one.

## 5. Motivation and theory uncertainties
- **Motivation (3/5):** ALPs with flavour structure are a well-defined EFT, and the top-trigger idea is clever. However, the large-Br, long-lifetime corner is not a generic UV outcome.
- **Main theory uncertainties:**
  - hadronic widths for 1–3 GeV ALPs (duality vs chiral/data-driven approaches), which shift cτ and the visible channel;
  - log(Λ) dependence of the loop-induced couplings;
  - the UV coupling structure.
- **Signal-side uncertainties are small** (tt̄ × Br and the kinematics are well predicted). The experimental reach therefore depends on how well the background can be modelled, not on theory.

## 6. Distinctiveness
- The LLP plus a light jet reconstructs m_t, in a tt̄ event that supplies the trigger.
- This differs from h → aa (two LLPs), W/Z + a, and emerging jets.
- It cannot be distinguished from any other t → qX (LLP) model.
- **Recommendation:** present results model-independently as Br(t → qX) vs (m_X, cτ_X), extending to m_X ~ 1–40 GeV and to the HCAL and muon-system volumes.

## Verdict on the proposed target
- **Theoretically consistent.** For m_a ≳ 5 GeV, flavour and cosmology constraints leave the region open.
- **Model-independent value:** the hadronic HCAL/muon-system channel at low mass is worth adding to the experimental programme as a signal reinterpretation.
- **Two qualifications:**
  - The physically generic parameter region requires very small branching ratios (≲1e‑6).
  - The displaced μμ/γγ sub-target is not robustly predicted by the minimal model.
