# C008: Enriched semi-visible jets (photon-enriched SVJ; ISR-tagged SVJ), theory deep-read (Gate 4)

**Proposed targets being evaluated**
- (i) Photon-enriched SVJ from an s-channel Z' (2407.08276).
- (ii) Low-mass (~200-700 GeV) Z' -> SVJ SVJ tagged with a photon from ISR (2409.04741).

**Verdict in one line**
- (i) is phenomenologically proposed-not-searched. Its distinctive objects are real (high-NEMF jets with non-isolated photons), but it depends on an ad hoc second (ALP plus vector-like lepton) portal, so robustness is low.
- (ii) is **in tension with existing constraints as published**. The visible Z' -> qq branch with g_q = 0.25 is excluded by ATLAS dijet+ISR (g_q < 0.06-0.1 for 200-650 GeV). The viable region needs B(Z'->chi chi) ~ 1 and g_q <~ 0.1, which cuts the rate. Photon ISR is also the weaker ISR handle.

## 1. Claim and framework
- The hidden valley is a dark SU(N_c) with N_f dark quarks. A leptophobic Z' couples to SM quarks (g_q) and dark quarks (g_chi) [2407.08276 Eq.1; 2409.04741 Sec.2.1].
- The dark shower hadronises into stable and unstable dark mesons, with r_inv = <N_stable/N_total> [1503.00009 Eq.2]. Unstable mesons decay back through the same Z'.
- The result is a jet that is only partly visible, with MET aligned to it [1503.00009; 2112.11125].
- (i) Photon variant. A pseudo-scalar ALP couples to dark quarks and to a vector-like lepton. This induces pi_v -> gamma gamma with Gamma_gamma ∝ m_pi^7 Xi_gamma^2, where Xi_gamma = lambda_L lambda_psi/(m_a^2 M_L). The rate competes with the helicity-suppressed Z'-portal decay Gamma_q ∝ m_q^2 Xi_q^2 [2407.08276 Eqs.2-6, Fig.2].
  - Benchmark: Lambda_v = m_pi_v = 20 GeV, m_rho ~ 54 GeV, rho_v -> pi_v pi_v at 100%, r_inv = 0.3, BR_gamma = 0.3-1, g_q = 0.25, g_chi = 0.4, M_Z' = 1.5-5 TeV [2407.08276 Sec.3].
  - The dark-meson photons are non-isolated. They push the jet NEMF above the JetID cut (0.9). The CMS hadronic SVJ selection then keeps only 0.4% instead of 14-20% of signal [2407.08276 Sec.4, Tab.2].
- (ii) ISR variant: pp -> Z' + gamma/jet, then Z' -> chi chi -> 2 SVJ.
  - Parameters: g_q = 0.25, g_chi = 0.5, N_c = N_f = 2, giving B(Z'->chi chi) = 47%. Dark hadron masses are 20 GeV, M_Z' = 0.5-1.25 TeV, and r_inv = 0.1, 0.5, 0.9 [2409.04741 Sec.2.1].
  - MET decomposition (mT2-assisted on-shell, MAOS) gives m_MAOS and a per-jet r_inv estimator.
  - Expected limits are shown for the jet-ISR channel only, with perfect background modelling assumed. The integrated luminosity is not stated in the text I read [2409.04741 Sec.5, Fig.17].

## 2. Controlling parameters
- **Mediator:** M_Z' and g_q, g_chi. These set the rate and B(Z'->chi chi) vs B(Z'->qq).
- **Dark shower:** r_inv, alpha_dark/Lambda_v, m_pi_v, m_rho/m_pi.
- **Photon variant:** BR_gamma, a free knob from (lambda_L, lambda_psi, m_a, M_L).
- **ISR variant:** the ISR pT threshold, which is driven by the trigger (photon > 150 GeV; jet ~500 GeV) [2409.04741 Sec.2.1].

## 3. Robust vs model-dependent
**Robust (kinematics)**
- MET aligned with a jet.
- M_T tracking M_Z'.
- With hard ISR, m_MAOS resolution barely degrades as r_inv rises [2409.04741 Outlook].
- **My own inference:** in the photon variant the photons come from two-body pi_v decays. The in-jet diphoton mass therefore peaks at m_pi_v, independent of dark hadronisation. 2407.08276 does not use this; it relies on a DNN over pT D, f_gamma and tau_4. The opening angle ~2 m_pi/E (~0.2-0.8 for E ~ 50-200 GeV at m_pi = 20 GeV, own estimate) should be resolvable by the ECAL (UNVERIFIED for Phase-2).
- The **visible Z' -> qq decay is unavoidable** in the s-channel model, because the same g_q gives production.

**Model-dependent**
- r_inv: exponentially sensitive to dark-quark mass splittings, and controlled by N_f [1503.00009 p.3].
- BR_gamma: comes from an independent portal.
- Substructure-based tagging: the dark-hadronisation dependence of substructure is a known problem. 2301.07732 proposes Lund-plane observables to mitigate it.
- Prompt vs displaced decays: the photon paper notes that light dark mesons give displaced or emerging signatures instead [2407.08276 Sec.2, Conclusions]. A CMS emerging-jet search excluded mediators up to 1.25 TeV for 1-10 GeV mesons with 5-225 mm decay lengths [quoted in 2112.11125 Sec.1].

## 5. Motivation and uncertainties
- **Motivation (3/5).**
  - Confining dark sectors are generic, and the SVJ relic-abundance mechanism is viable [1809.10152].
  - Photon enrichment is a signature-driven add-on: a second portal (ALP plus VLL) introduced to make dark pions decay to photons. No independent motivation fixes BR_gamma.
  - ISR tagging is a trigger strategy rather than new physics. It matters only if light leptophobic mediators with mostly-dark decays exist.
- **Limiting uncertainties.**
  - Dark-hadronisation modelling, which affects any substructure/DNN tagger.
  - The meaning and distribution of r_inv.
  - Photon fakes and spurious MET, which Delphes does not model [2409.04741 Sec.5].
  - For HL-LHC, photon isolation and NEMF behaviour at ~200 pile-up are unknown (UNVERIFIED).
- **HL-LHC relevance.**
  - (i) is claimed discoverable at the benchmark with Run 2 alone [2407.08276 Fig.5]. HL-LHC therefore extends the reach in g_q x BR_gamma, not in mass.
  - (ii) at HL-LHC targets the small-g_q, invisible-dominated corner left by 2403.08547. Photon ISR has lower rate than jet ISR, and 2409.04741 shows limits only for jet ISR.

## 6. Distinctiveness
- MET-along-jet events differ from both monojet (MET recoiling against jets) and dijet (no MET) topologies [2112.11125 Sec.1].
- The photon variant adds high-NEMF, multi-prong jets with non-isolated photon pairs at a fixed m_gamma gamma. Its closest neighbours are photon-jets from ALP -> gamma gamma (no MET, no hadronic component) and emerging jets.
- The ISR variant gives a localised (m_MAOS, r_inv) 2D feature. The authors report ~10x better expected limits than the 1D approach [2409.04741 Fig.16-17, Outlook].
