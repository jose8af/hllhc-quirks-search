# C051: theory deep-read. High-mass DY angular coefficients (Lam-Tung, T-odd A5-A7) and sin2theta_W running

Gate 4 theory reviewer. Sources read in full text: 2003.11615, 2511.19617, 2412.13014, 2405.04069, 2302.10782, plus excerpts from 2202.12327, 2604.25678, 1708.00008 and 1609.08157.

## 1. The claim and its framework
The candidate bundles four different physics claims. They do not share a motivation.

- **(a) Lam-Tung breaking from dim-6 dipoles.** The light-quark Z dipoles O_uZ and O_dZ are the only dim-6 operators that break A0=A2 at LO in QCD (2412.13014, Sec. 2 and App. C). Their interference with the SM vanishes for massless quarks, so the effect is O(|C|^2/Lambda^4) (2405.04069, abstract; 2412.13014, Sec. 4). The effect grows with energy, so it is larger off-peak (2405.04069, Fig. 5).
- **(b) T-odd A6 and A7 from dim-8 CP-odd operators.** These are the semileptonic four-fermion operators with a gluon field strength, (l gamma l)(q gamma T^A q)G^A_{mu nu} and its variants (2511.19617, Table I). They populate A6 and A7 at tree level, linearly in C/Lambda^4. Their contribution to A5 is negligible. They leave the Lam-Tung relation intact at tree level (Sec. III). Their effect in phi-integrated rates is only a Gamma_Z-suppressed remnant (Sec. IV).
- **(c) l=3 harmonics from dim-8 psi^4 D^2 operators.** These operators (2003.11615, Eqs. 11-12, seven operators) produce l=3 harmonics. This needs B coefficients beyond the A0-A7 basis (Eq. 17). The SM produces them only through EW Sudakov logarithms, and that SM contribution is small (Fig. 1). The proposed target omits this branch. It is arguably the cleanest smoking gun.
- **(d) Running of MSbar sin2theta_W.** The test uses triple-differential NC DY (2302.10782).

## 2. Assumptions and controlling parameters
- **Wilson coefficients.** C_qZ/Lambda^2 enters quadratically. The seven CP-odd dim-8 coefficients enter linearly at 1/Lambda^4. The psi^4 D^2 coefficients enter at 1/Lambda^4.
- **Energy scaling.** The A6/A7 shifts grow steeply with m_ll and pT. At Lambda=1 TeV and C=1, A7 shifts by O(1) for m_ll in 350-1000 GeV and pT near 1 TeV (2511.19617, Figs. 5-6). A shift that size is a symptom of the linearised EFT failing, not a physical prediction.
- **Analysis setup in the A6/A7 projection.** It uses an HL-LHC fit with statistical uncertainties only. Its bins run up to m_ll = 2.6 TeV and pT = 7 TeV (Table II).
- **Assumption behind the running test.** It assumes SM running of alpha_EM and no BSM contact terms (2302.10782, Sec. 4).
- **Production chain.** The final state is qqbar -> g l l or qg -> q l l at pT_ll > 100 GeV, with the angles defined in the Collins-Soper frame.

## 3. Robust and model-dependent predictions
**Robust:**
- In SM QCD, A0=A2 holds through O(alpha_s) and is broken from O(alpha_s^2) (1708.00008).
- A5-A7 start at O(alpha_s^2) (2511.19617, Sec. I) and measure about 1e-3 at the Z peak (CMS 2604.25678, Sec. 6).
- Dim-6 four-fermion operators create no new angular structure (2003.11615).
- The SM sin2theta_MSbar value goes from 0.23323 at 133 GeV to 0.24350 at 1 TeV (2302.10782, Table 2).

**The SM Lam-Tung violation is not small.**
- CMS at 13 TeV measures A0-A2 of about 0.13-0.18 for pT_ll between 80 and 400 GeV at the Z peak (2604.25678, Table A.3).
- Both NNLO-matched MC predictions underestimate the measurement for pT > 35 GeV (Sec. 6).
- A BSM search through A0-A2 therefore sits on an O(0.1) SM effect that is not fully understood.

**Model-dependent:**
- All quoted reaches depend on the model and on the EFT truncation.
- For A6/A7 the reach is about 9 TeV with one operator at a time, falling to about 1-2 TeV when marginalised. The marginalised fit has strong flat directions (2511.19617, Figs. 8, 10, 13-14 and Eqs. 30-37).

## 4. Consistency and indirect constraints
- **Dipoles.** The bounds are |C_u|/Lambda^2 < 1/(2.3 TeV)^2 and |C_d|/Lambda^2 < 1/(1.9 TeV)^2, from the ATLAS 36 fb-1 pT_ll spectrum; LEP/SLC give 1.7/1.6 TeV (2412.13014, Eqs. 24 and 26). With dipoles at these bounds, A0-A2 stays inside the SM uncertainty band (Fig. 3). The C_u value used in 2405.04069 gives effects about 6 times larger and is excluded (App. D). The inclusive high-pT_ll spectrum is the stronger dipole probe, and HL-LHC is projected to improve it by about a factor 4.5 (Sec. 4). **Verdict:** off-peak A0-A2 as a dipole probe is subdominant. Its value is mainly as a diagnostic for the source of an anomaly.
- **CP-odd dim-8 operators.** No EDM or other CP-odd low-energy constraint is discussed in 2511.19617. It is UNVERIFIED whether loop-induced EDMs (electron, neutron) already constrain these first-generation operators.
- **EFT validity.** The 2511.19617 projection quotes marginalised scales of 1-2 TeV while using events with sqrt(s_hat) of several TeV. I found no clipping or validity scan in the paper. Squared CP-even terms are dropped. They do not enter the A6/A7 numerators linearly, but they do change the denominator. **Verdict:** the parameter region the target relies on is only partly inside EFT validity. The 1-D reaches are more credible than the marginalised ones.
- **A_FB already measured.** CMS has measured A_FB and A0 versus mass above 170 GeV (2202.12327, abstract and Sec. 1). The A_FB-as-SMEFT-probe sub-idea is therefore not open.
- **Running degeneracy.** Energy-growing oblique W and Y parameters and four-fermion operators also distort high-mass NC DY (1609.08157). A "running" extraction is therefore degenerate with contact-interaction BSM unless both are fitted together. The SM running from 133 GeV to 1 TeV is about 0.010 in sin2theta (0.23323 to 0.24350). The projected HL-LHC uncertainty at 1 TeV is 0.0047 (2302.10782, Table 2), so the running is resolvable only at the level of a few sigma per bin.

## 5. Motivation and limiting theory uncertainties
**Motivation is moderate (3/5).** The T-odd A6/A7 and l=3 B-coefficient measurements give clean null tests: the SM is near zero, systematics are small, and statistics limit them. The dim-8 CP-odd direction is not otherwise probed, but there is no specific UV model or anomaly driving it. Off-peak A0-A2 is weakly motivated as a BSM probe after 2412.13014, but useful as a QCD test.

**Limiting theory uncertainties:**
- O(alpha_s^3) and beyond plus resummation for A0-A2.
- SM absorptive O(alpha_s^2) A5-A7 off-peak, which is not computed in the sources read (UNVERIFIED size).
- High-x PDFs for sin2theta: 0.8-2.8% at HL-LHC depending on the PDF set (2302.10782, Table 3).
- EFT truncation.

## 6. Distinctiveness
- **A6/A7 and B-harmonics: high.** They are T-odd or l=3 structures that are tiny in the SM.
- **A0-A2: moderate.** It is degenerate with QCD and dominated in sensitivity by spectra.
- **sin2theta running: low.** It is degenerate with contact interactions.

## Verdict
The idea survives as a well-defined measurement target, but should be narrowed:
1. Unfolded A5-A7 above the Z peak, and an extended-basis l=3 fit, versus m_ll and pT_ll at HL-LHC.
2. A0-A2 off-peak only as an SM QCD test.

The SMEFT interpretation of (1) needs an EFT-validity treatment and EDM cross-checks, both UNVERIFIED.
