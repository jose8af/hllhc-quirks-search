# C040: theory deep-read. Compressed stau-neutralino coannihilation corridor (direct stau, Phase-2)

Gate 4 theory reviewer.

**Sources read in full text:** 2601.06358 (Sec. 3.4), 2308.05723, 1704.04669, 2403.18991, 2609.37215 (abstract, Secs. 11-12), 1910.01185 (abstract), 1606.08878 (abstract).

**Sources checked on specific points:** 2505.21476 (conclusion line), 2506.03069 (abstract).

**Not evaluated:** the Z'(B-L) to slepton branch (2608.18321) and the natural-SUSY branch beyond 2403.18991. The proposed target is option (a) only.

## 1. The claim and its framework
- **Model.** The MSSM with a bino-like chi0_1 LSP and a light stau NLSP. When Delta m = m(stau1) - m(chi0_1) is small, stau-chi coannihilation lowers the bino relic density to the observed value.
- **Gap the target addresses.** The LHC has no sensitivity to direct stau production for m_tau <~ Delta m <~ 60 GeV (2601.06358, Sec. 3.4.1 and Sec. 3.4.4). Prompt soft taus, tau trigger thresholds and large backgrounds cause this.
- **Proposed target.** A Phase-2 projection, and later an analysis, of pp -> stau stau (+ISR/VBF jet), stau -> tau chi0_1, using the soft tau_h + jet + MET signature (1606.08878).

## 2. Assumptions and controlling parameters
**Assumptions:**
- Standard thermal freeze-out.
- A bino-dominated LSP.
- BR(stau -> tau chi) = 1.
- Other sparticles decoupled.

**Controlling parameters:**
- **Delta m.** It sets tau softness and, for Delta m < m_tau, the stau lifetime. The stau is about 1 ns just below m_tau and about 100 ns at 1.4 GeV (2601.06358, footnote 2).
- **m(stau).** It sets the cross section.
- **Stau L-R mixing.** It sets the cross section, the tau polarisation and whether a tau sneutrino partner is present.
- **Bino fraction of chi0_1.**

**Production chain:**
- Direct electroweak pair production through Drell-Yan.
- In full models, also chargino/neutralino -> stau cascades. In 1704.04669 those cascades dominate the total SUSY rate (Sec. 4).

## 3. Robust and model-dependent predictions
**Robust:**
- Coannihilation needs small Delta m. 2308.05723 finds Delta m <~ 30 GeV for a stau_L-like NLSP and Delta m <~ 10 GeV for stau_R, with m_(N)LSP <~ 550 GeV (Sec. 5). 1704.04669 finds about 20 GeV in SUGRA (Sec. 3).
- For Delta m > m_tau the decays are prompt. For Delta m < m_tau the stau is long-lived and covered by charged-LLP and disappearing-track searches (2601.06358, Sec. 3.4.2).
- Pure stau_R pair production has no 5 sigma discovery reach at HL-LHC, even for large Delta m. This is the YR result quoted in 2308.05723 Sec. 5.5.1 and 2403.18991 Sec. 5.4.

**Model-dependent:**
- **Delta m between about 30 and 60 GeV is relic-relevant only with bino-higgsino/wino mixing.** In 2308.05723 those mixed-LSP points with m_chi <~ 300 GeV are excluded by LZ. The upper half of the 5-60 GeV target window is therefore experimentally interesting, as a hole in coverage, but not well motivated by coannihilation.
- **The ~550 GeV upper end of the strip relies on the g-2 constraint.** That constraint is now obsolete (see Sec. 4).
- **Discovery mode in full models.** The cascades from charginos and neutralinos, not direct staus, are the discovery mode in 1704.04669 (1510-2650 fb-1 for the best 1tau signal region).

## 4. Consistency and indirect constraints
- **g-2 motivation is gone.** The 2025 Theory Initiative update gives Delta a_mu = 38(63) x 10^-11, "no tension between the SM and experiment" (2505.21476, conclusions). 2308.05723 used the WP20 value (Eq. 1). The 2021 g-2-motivated leads (2104.03839, 2104.03302) lose their premise. The coannihilation argument stands on dark matter alone.
- **Relic density.** It survives, but only in a narrow Delta m strip that shrinks as m_chi grows.
- **Direct detection.** Pure-bino coannihilation points pass LZ and partly sit below the neutrino floor (2308.05723, Fig. 7 discussion). Collider searches are therefore the main probe; this is a point in favour of the idea.
- **LEP and current LHC limits.**
  - LEP excludes stau_R below 65 GeV for Delta m >~ 2 GeV (2601.06358, Sec. 3.4).
  - ATLAS with 140 fb-1 at 13 TeV plus 53 fb-1 at 13.6 TeV excludes m(stau) < 115 GeV at Delta m = 30 GeV with the ISR channel alone. This is for degenerate stau_L+stau_R. The combination gives no exclusion because of 2.1-2.7 sigma local excesses. The best-fit point is m(stau) = 90 GeV, m(chi) = 40 GeV (2609.37215, abstract and Sec. 11).
  - The expected combined reach is only just above 120 GeV.
  - CMS 1910.01185 (77 fb-1) is interpreted only for electroweakino production with an intermediate stau.
- **Verdict.** The relic-motivated region (Delta m ~ 5-30 GeV, m(stau) of order 100 GeV to a few hundred GeV) survives all checked constraints and is untested beyond LEP. With the expected sensitivity, HL-LHC can reach only its low-mass end: plausibly m(stau) ~ 100-200 GeV. This range is UNVERIFIED and reasoned from current expected limits, not from a projection.

## 5. Motivation and limiting theory uncertainties
**Motivation is moderate (3/5).**
- Bino dark matter with stau coannihilation is a standard, predictive corner of the MSSM. It is hard to probe by direct detection.
- The g-2 leg is gone, and nothing points to light staus specifically. The ATLAS excesses are mild and sit at Delta m = 50 GeV, outside the coannihilation strip for a pure bino.

**Theory uncertainties:**
- The cross section depends on stau chirality and mixing. The pure-R versus degenerate-L+R difference is O(2-3), UNVERIFIED.
- The tau polarisation depends on mixing and on neutralino composition. It changes the soft tau_h visible-pT acceptance.
- ISR modelling of the hard-jet recoil.
- The NLO+NLL cross-section uncertainty itself is small (UNVERIFIED value).
- The mapping from Delta m to relic density is exponentially sensitive. The "target" Delta m therefore varies with m_chi and with any small higgsino/wino admixture.

## 6. Distinctiveness
**Low to moderate.** The signature is a soft tau_h + ISR jet + MET, without a resonance or edge. It competes with W(tau nu)+jets, Z(nu nu)+jets and fake taus. Sensitivity relies on Phase-2 trigger and tau-ID improvements and on shape information (tau pT, mT). A discovery would also fix m_chi from Delta m kinematics only weakly.

## Verdict
The physics motivation for the corridor is sound on dark-matter grounds alone. Two parts of the stated rationale should be narrowed:
- Drop g-2.
- Restrict the relic-motivated Delta m to about 5-30 GeV, not up to 60 GeV.

The theoretically clean target is: pure stau_R and degenerate L+R, Delta m in 5-30 GeV, m(stau) in about 90-250 GeV.

The idea is not novel phenomenology: 1606.08878, 1704.04669, 2308.05723 and 2403.18991 already cover it. The gap is a collaboration-level Phase-2 projection or analysis. Direct staus have small rates, so HL-LHC reach for the higher-mass coannihilation strip (up to ~550 GeV) is not expected.
