# C019 — Doubly charged scalars (compressed, displaced, τ/W cascades): theory review (Gate 4)

Reviewer role: Theory Deep-Reader. I read 2608.27251 (Secs. 1–3), 1808.00943 (Sec. 2), 2410.19561 (Secs. I–III and conclusions), 2603.15882 (abstract, Secs. 2–3 and results), 2305.06889 (introduction), 1105.2209 (introduction and Sec. II–III) and hep-ph/0506176 (Sec. 4).

## 1. Claim and framework
**Model.** The type-II seesaw adds a Y = 1 triplet Δ (states H±±, H±, H0, A0).
- Yukawa: f_L L^T C iσ₂ Δ L, with m_ν = √2 f_L v_t (1808.00943 Eq. 2.7–2.8).
- A trilinear μ H^TΔH term induces v_t.
- One quartic sets an equally spaced spectrum, M²_H++ − M²_H+ = M²_H+ − M²_H0 (1808.00943 Eq. 2.5–2.6). Its sign gives a positive or negative hierarchy.
- Variants: the Georgi–Machacek H5±± (custodial fiveplet, fermiophobic at tree level), and the left-right model's H_R±±.

**Decay widths** (2603.15882 Eq. 16):
ll : WW : H+W* ≈ (m_ν/v_t)² : (v_t/v_d)²(M/v_d)² : max(ΔM,0)⁵/(v_d²M³).

The three sub-targets evaluated:
1. **(i) Long-lived H±± with cτ ~ 0.1–100 mm**, searched for with a charged-parent displaced-vertex (DV) analysis.
2. **(ii) Compressed positive hierarchy** (ΔM ~ 1–30 GeV, H0/A0 → νν): soft same-sign leptons + MET + ISR.
3. **(iii) Hadronic-τ final states.**

## 2. Assumptions, parameters, decay chains
**(i) Long-lived H±±.** There are two very different cases.
- **Type-II with neutrino-mass Yukawas:**
  - Γ_ll ∝ m_ν²/v_t² and Γ_WW ∝ v_t², so their product is fixed by m_ν. The total width is smallest near the crossover v_t ~ 0.1–1 MeV (1808.00943 Sec. 2.1, Fig. 1).
  - Hence cτ ~ mm–m only for M ≲ 150–160 GeV, i.e. with off-shell WW (1808.00943 Fig. 1; 2608.27251 Sec. 2, Fig. 1a).
  - The Yukawas involved are |f_L| ~ 1e‑10–1e‑6, with f_L·v_L = 0.027 eV (NH) or 0.048 eV (IH) (1808.00943 Eq. 2.14).
  - Cascades must be negligible: |M_H++ − M_H+| ≲ 1 GeV, or H±± lightest (1808.00943 Sec. 2.1).
- **Fermiophobic case (y_ll = 0):**
  - Γ = Γ_WW ∝ G_F² v_t² M³ (Eq. 2.11), so the lifetime grows monotonically as v_t → 0.
  - H±± is long-lived for v_t ≲ 1e‑4 GeV above the WW threshold, at masses up to ~1 TeV (2608.27251 Fig. 1b, abstract).
  - The triplet then has no role in neutrino masses. The motivation reduces to GM, composite or custodial models (2410.19561 studies GM H5±±).
  - Naturalness: a small v_t is technically natural, since μ → 0 restores a U(1)_Δ. With y_ll = 0 and no other source of lepton-number violation, L with L_Δ = 0 is exact, so y_ll is not regenerated. This is my argument; 2608.27251 Sec. 2 states that higher-order effects induce y_ll, which must depend on extra UV ingredients (UNVERIFIED).

**(ii) Compressed positive hierarchy.**
- Chain: H++ → H+W* → H0/A0 W*W*, with H0/A0 → νν.
- Cascades dominate for ΔM ≳ 10 GeV and 1e‑5 ≲ v_t ≲ 1e‑2 GeV (2603.15882 Sec. 3, Fig. 1). The H0/A0 decays are invisible for v_t ≲ 1e‑4 GeV (2305.06889 introduction).
- The unconstrained region identified by the authors is ΔM ≳ 10 GeV with v_t ~ 1e‑7–1e‑3 GeV (2305.06889 introduction; 2603.15882 Sec. 3).
- The cascades at moderate splittings were first discussed by Akeroyd–Aoki and Akeroyd–Sugiyama. Their numbers: for v_t = 0.1 MeV, H± → H±±W* dominates already for |ΔM| > 2 GeV (1105.2209 Sec. III); for NH/IH, BR(H±± → H±W*) can approach 100% (hep-ph/0506176 Fig. 3).

**(iii) Hadronic-τ final states.**
- In pure type-II, BR(H±± → l_a l_b) ∝ |(m_ν)_ab|²/(1+δ_ab), which is fixed by oscillation data together with the lightest mass m0, the hierarchy and the Majorana phases (1808.00943 Eq. 2.8–2.10; hep-ph/0506176 Sec. 4).
- For NH with m1 ≈ 0, (m_ν)_ee is small and μμ, ττ and μτ dominate. The τ-containing fraction is therefore large (qualitative; numerical BRs UNVERIFIED).

## 3. Robust vs model-dependent
**Robust:**
- The Drell–Yan pair-production rate (gauge-fixed).
- The doubly charged parent: net DV charge ±2, and ~4× MIP dE/dx on the incoming track.
- The anti-correlation of the ll and WW widths in type-II, which caps the long-lived mass window at ≲150–160 GeV.
- The EWPD cap |ΔM| ≲ 40 GeV (2603.15882 Sec. 3), so the W in cascades is always off-shell and the leptons are soft.
- The neutrino-data-driven flavour pattern.

**Model-dependent:**
- The TeV-scale long-lived H±± (fermiophobic only).
- The presence and size of cascades (a free quartic coupling).
- GM-specific constraints: H5^0 → γγ, which is not s_H-suppressed, excludes 70 ≲ m5 ≲ 108 GeV in the low-m5 benchmark independent of the H5±± lifetime, but W and H3 loops can cancel (2410.19561 Sec. II).
- The exact τ share.
- The LR-model H_R±±, whose flavour pattern is unrelated to light-neutrino data.

## 5. Motivation and theory uncertainties
- **Motivation (4/5):**
  - The type-II seesaw is among the best-motivated neutrino-mass models, and (ii) and (iii) sit squarely inside it, consistent with EWPD and LFV.
  - (i) is well motivated only in its narrow 100–150 GeV type-II window. The broad fermiophobic version gives up the neutrino link.
- **Theory uncertainties:**
  - Production normalisation: NLO QCD and the photon-initiated piece, which is Q⁴-enhanced for H±± and depends on the photon PDF (size UNVERIFIED).
  - Off-shell four-body W*W* and cascade widths near thresholds (1808.00943 App. A), which control the cτ mapping below 160 GeV.
  - Radiative splittings among nearly degenerate multiplet components (O(0.1–1 GeV), UNVERIFIED). These could open soft-pion transitions (e.g. GM H5±± → H5± π±) that compete with v_t-suppressed decays and change the long-lived signature. Neither 2608.27251 nor 2410.19561 discusses this.
  - The τ BRs (m0, phases).
  - Modelling of a charged-parent DV with W* products, which existing DV tools do not handle (2608.27251 Sec. 3.2.2).

## 6. Distinctiveness
1. **(i)** A pair-produced charge-2 track ending in a DV whose same-sign products sum to charge ±2. This is unlike neutral-LLP DVs, singly charged disappearing tracks (winos), and opposite-charge displaced dileptons.
2. **(ii)** Two soft same-sign leptons with low m_ll from one parent, plus MET and an ISR jet. Compressed higgsinos give opposite-sign soft dileptons, so the charge requirement is a strong discriminator.
3. **(iii)** The flavour pattern of H±± decays is a direct test of type-II seesaw against oscillation data. No neighbouring BSM model predicts it.

## Verdict on the proposed targets
| Target | Assessment |
|---|---|
| (i) | Theoretically sound, but the TeV-scale reach rests on the fermiophobic assumption. In neutrino-motivated type-II the target is M ≈ 100–150 GeV with mixed ll/W*W* decays (2608.27251 benchmark: 120 GeV, cτ = 5 mm). |
| (ii) | Well motivated and consistent. Its limits come from experiment (soft-lepton thresholds), not theory. |
| (iii) | Theoretically robust in pure type-II. |
