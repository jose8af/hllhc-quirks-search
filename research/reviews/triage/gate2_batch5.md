# Gate-2 batch 5 triage (C053-C065)

## C053 - PASS (proposed-not-searched, conf low-medium)
- Observable: Narrowed to two sub-seeds: (a) gg->Z gamma / gamma gamma high-pT tails (and Z decay angles) for dim-8 gluon-gluon-EW quartic operators; (b) ssWW(jj)/WZjj decay-lepton spin-correlation asymmetries as aQGC/aTGC discriminants. Other sub-seeds (nTGC, triboson dim-8, Drell-Yan W,Y, HEFT VBF HHH) are covered or theory-only.
- Gap: No CMS/ATLAS result in the INSPIRE searches (collab + 'gluon'+'quartic' abstract queries returned nothing; no experimental citers of 2112.06729 among 17 citing papers) interprets gg->Z gamma / gamma gamma tails in gluonic dim-8 operators; and no CMS/ATLAS ssWW aQGC analysis uses decay-angle spin-correlation asymmetries (2606.06436 is theory, 2026).
- Closest: arXiv:2512.08582 (CMS); arXiv:2601.14102 (CMS); INSPIRE:2909586 (ATLAS); arXiv:2603.18630 (ATLAS); arXiv:2310.04350 (ATLAS)
- HL-LHC: High-pT tails of gg->Zgamma / diphoton and boosted ssWW are statistics-limited; 3000/fb extends reach on dim-8 coefficients; asymmetries need large ssWW samples.
- Reason: Umbrella candidate; most sub-seeds are well-covered (nTGC by CMS 2512.08582/2601.14102, ATLAS 2909586; dim-8 aQGC by ATLAS 2603.18630, CMS 2510.00118, 2410.04210). Two narrow sub-gaps remain. Confidence limited: did not read full texts; gluonic operators may be unreachable with unitarity-safe limits (not checked).

## C054 - PASS (one-observable-not-another, conf medium-low)
- Observable: Spin density matrix / concurrence / Bell (CGLMP) observables in H->WW*->lnulnu (and semileptonic) and in WW/WZ/ZZ continuum; also tau pairs.
- Gap: Searches (collab:CMS/ATLAS abstracts 'entanglement','Bell','spin density matrix') found experimental entanglement only for ttbar and ATLAS H->ZZ*; no CMS/ATLAS entanglement/Bell measurement in H->WW* or WW/WZ continuum or tau pairs.
- Closest: arXiv:2603.26463 (ATLAS); INSPIRE:3087935 (CMS); arXiv:2406.03976 / 2311.07288 / 2512.17557 (CMS/ATLAS)
- HL-LHC: Entanglement in H->WW* and diboson needs large samples; statistics-limited; HL-LHC dataset is the realistic route (H->ZZ* was already done with Run 2+3 data).
- Reason: Identifiable observable; precedent measurement (ZZ*) shows feasibility. Caveat: arXiv:2601.09540 (conclusions, per scout quote) finds WZ concurrence bounds cannot demonstrate entanglement in realistic regimes, and SMEFT sensitivity is weak, so physics value is a QI test of SM more than new-physics search. Neutrino reconstruction in WW* is the main challenge.

## C055 - HOLD (searched-limited-regime, conf medium)
- Observable: Beyond-threshold toponium: eta_t->W+W- dilepton signature, excited/P-wave toponium; QI/steering observables of ttbar near threshold.
- Gap: Threshold excess and spin-correlation/QI measurements already exist from both collaborations; the remaining gap (eta_t->W+W-, excited toponia) is theory-explored-untested with only tens of signal events and unquantified backgrounds.
- Closest: arXiv:2503.22382 (CMS); arXiv:2608.19546 (CMS); arXiv:2601.11780 (ATLAS); arXiv:2512.17557 (CMS)
- HL-LHC: Excess established in Run 2; HL-LHC needed for eta_t->WW (tens of events) and P-wave states.
- Reason: Core signature well covered by CMS/ATLAS; residual sub-channels have marginal event counts. Not rejected: ground-state-only analyses leave eta_t->WW open and a dedicated HL-LHC study could be worthwhile.

- Gap: collab:CMS/ATLAS queries for 'energy correlator' returned only CMS inclusive-jet EEC (2402.13864) and heavy-ion/ALICE papers; no CMS/ATLAS EEC measurement on boosted top/W decays or EEC bump-hunt exists in INSPIRE (ATLAS TEEC alpha_s 2301.09351 is event-level).

## C061 - PASS (searched-limited-regime, conf low-medium)
- Observable: pp->HHH in final states other than 6b/4b2gamma (e.g. bbbb tautau, bbbb WW), and resonant X->SH->HHH; kappa_4 sensitivity.
- Gap: CMS/ATLAS HHH searches found (collab queries) cover only 6b and 4b2gamma; no experimental search or HL-LHC projection in bbbbtautau/bbbbWW or resonant HHH channels.
- Closest: arXiv:2607.05145 (CMS); INSPIRE:2945642 (CMS); arXiv:2411.02040 (ATLAS)
- HL-LHC: SM HHH is out of reach in 6b alone; adding channels with taus/photons/leptons and combination is the HL-LHC path; resonant TRSM cascades accessible earlier.
- Reason: Clear observable, limited-regime coverage, HL-LHC-relevant. Caveats: tiny SM rate (~3 fb, scout quote 2309.12514 style: not verified here), branching fractions suppress tau/WW channels; sensitivity to kappa_4 poor.
