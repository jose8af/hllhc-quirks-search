# Gate-2 triage batch 4 (C040-C052)

## C040: PASS (searched-limited-regime, conf medium)
- Observable: Direct stau pair production with Delta m(stau,LSP) <~ 60 GeV: soft hadronic tau(s) + ISR jet + MET (also VBF-tagged variants)
- Gap: No experimental analysis excludes direct stau pair production in the coannihilation corridor beyond m(stau)~115 GeV at Delta m=30 GeV; the combined ATLAS Run2+Run3 fit gives no exclusion, and no CMS/ATLAS HL-LHC projection for the compressed ISR-tau topology was found.
- Closest: arXiv:2609.37215 (ATLAS); arXiv:1910.01185 (CMS); arXiv:2207.02254 (CMS)
- HL: Direct compressed-stau cross section tiny; only 3 ab-1 statistics + soft-tau/ISR trigger and track-based tau ID improvements (Phase-2 tracker/L1 track trigger) could reach beyond ~115 GeV. Compressed-case HL projection not found.
- Reason: Signature measurable; closest ATLAS search (2609.37215) is explicitly weak in this corridor; CMS compressed-tau search uses 77 fb-1 only. Caveat: low cross section may leave HL reach marginal.

## C041: HOLD (searched-limited-regime, conf low)
- Observable: (a) higgsino->HH/ZH+gravitino MET (4b, bbgammagamma); (b) displaced stau/HSCP; (c) displaced non-pointing/delayed photon + MET from long-lived bino NLSP
- Gap: Candidate is three heterogeneous sub-directions; strongest sub-gap is delayed/non-pointing photon+MET at 13 TeV with only 77 fb-1 CMS and no found ATLAS 13 TeV result, with HL timing-detector projection missing. Coverage check for that gap incomplete (INSPIRE title queries returned little).
- Closest: arXiv:2201.04206 (CMS); arXiv:2511.21240 (ATLAS); arXiv:1909.06166 (CMS); arXiv:2601.17576 (CMS)
- HL: Photon timing (ECAL/MTD barrel, HGCAL) for delayed photons; HH+MET lumi reach. No experimental HL projection for delayed-photon found.
- Reason: Higgsino HH/ZH and displaced stau directions are searched (Run 2); delayed-photon gap plausible but unverified, so HOLD.

## C042: HOLD (theory-explored-untested, conf low)
- Observable: Sgluon (colour-octet scalar) pair -> 4t / 4 jets / tt+jets; single sgluon -> tt, gg; Dirac-gluino squark jets+MET
- Gap: No CMS/ATLAS dedicated sgluon-pair->top-rich search found at 13 TeV (only recasts); however the model-space is broad and the HL-added value over Run-3 is not demonstrated.
- Closest: recid:2053983 (CMS); arXiv:2107.13565 (theory); arXiv:2010.14293 (ATLAS)
- HL: Four-top / multi-b high-multiplicity statistics; HGTD/MTD irrelevant. Benefit mostly luminosity.
- Reason: Signature identifiable and no dedicated search found, but evidence of absence is weak (abstract-keyword queries) and HL-specific argument absent.

## C043: HOLD (searched-limited-regime, conf low)
- Observable: NMSSM singlino/singlet-Higgs cascades from stops/electroweakinos with boosted H1->bb/gammagamma
- Gap: Stop/electroweakino-initiated cascades with light singlet H1 not found in CMS/ATLAS searches (ATLAS NMSSM singlino query empty); candidate too diffuse to state a single gap.
- Closest: arXiv:2204.13532 (CMS)
- HL: Unclear: boosted-jet tagging with Phase-2 upgrades; no specific HL argument.
- Reason: Vague signature, partial coverage by 2204.13532, no HL projection.

## C044: PASS (one-observable-not-another, conf medium)
- Observable: Pair or single VLQ -> t + S with S decaying to modes beyond gammagamma/bb (WW, ZZ, gg, tautau, light quarks), and X5/3 -> t W S with same-sign dileptons + photon/b-jets
- Gap: Experiments cover T->tS only for S->gammagamma and S->bb; no found CMS/ATLAS search for S->WW/ZZ/tautau/gg or for X5/3->tWS, and no HL projection for exotic-scalar VLQ decays.
- Closest: arXiv:2607.28381 (ATLAS); arXiv:2510.25874 (CMS); arXiv:2604.10729 (CMS); arXiv:2610.03489 (CMS)
- HL: Pair-production mass reach and rare-S-decay statistics scale with 3 ab-1; boosted S tagging improves with Phase-2 tracking/timing.
- Reason: Active but narrow experimental coverage leaves distinct S-decay channels; HL value from luminosity. Field moving fast (2025-26 papers), so risk of near-term coverage.

## C045: PASS (proposed-not-searched, conf medium)
- Observable: uu->TT same-charge pair production (t-channel) of first-generation-coupled VLQs: same-sign dilepton/WW + jets, charge-asymmetric lepton rates
- Gap: No CMS/ATLAS search includes the same-charge uu->TT (VLQ coupled to first generation) topology; ATLAS 140 fb-1 search is lepton+jets QCD pair only; CMS only 8 TeV.
- Closest: arXiv:2405.19862 (ATLAS); arXiv:1708.02510 (CMS); arXiv:2504.05476 (theory)
- HL: Valence-quark-initiated high-mass production, same-sign signature low-background; 3 ab-1 extends mass reach where QCD pair falls steeply.
- Reason: Clean theory-defined signature, closest searches leave a describable gap.

## C046: HOLD (searched-limited-regime, conf medium)
- Observable: Heavy spin-1 resonance -> t T' / b B' (SSDL+jets, multi-top); VBF W'->WZ
- Gap: Resonance->t+VLQ cascades covered only with 35.9 fb-1; gap is mostly an un-updated Run 2/3 analysis rather than HL-specific. VBF Higgsless WZ sub-direction is covered (2004.14636, 2207.03925).
- Closest: arXiv:1812.06489 (CMS); arXiv:2609.24950 (ATLAS); arXiv:2004.14636 (ATLAS); arXiv:2207.03925 (ATLAS)
- HL: Higher mass reach via luminosity; marginal beyond updating Run 2 analysis.
- Reason: Partly covered; remaining gap is dataset-size, little HL-specific rationale.

## C047: PASS (proposed-not-searched, conf low)
- Observable: Fermionic portal-matter VLQ -> t/q + (boosted or displaced) dark photon A'->ll/jj; VLL cascades through dark sector (multimuon)
- Gap: No CMS/ATLAS search found for VLQ -> top + dark photon (prompt boosted or displaced A'->ll/jj); closest searches target scalar phi->bb/gammagamma.
- Closest: arXiv:2503.16699 (CMS); arXiv:2604.10729 (CMS); arXiv:2410.06925 (theory)
- HL: Collimated dilepton/jet-like A' tagging benefits from Phase-2 tracker granularity and timing; luminosity for rare VLQ pairs.
- Reason: Identifiable signature, no experimental search found with several queries; pheno shows 13/14 TeV sensitivity. Searches limited to INSPIRE keyword matching.

## C048: PASS (indirectly-covered, conf medium)
- Observable: Pair-produced colour-sextet composite top partners decaying via coloured pNGBs: top-rich high-multiplicity jets/b-jets, same-sign leptons, MET
- Gap: No dedicated CMS/ATLAS search for colour-sextet fermions exists per the source paper; only recasts of generic high-multiplicity searches give 2-2.5 TeV limits and ~3 TeV HL extrapolation; single-sextet and pNGB-specific topologies unstudied.
- Closest: arXiv:2605.04143 (theory); arXiv:2606.09567 (CMS)
- HL: Heavy (multi-TeV) pair production with high multiplicity; single sextet production 'expected to become most relevant in HL-LHC regime' (Sec. 3).
- Reason: Describable gap (dedicated top-rich search + single production), explicit HL statement, but modest gain beyond recasts.

## C049: HOLD (indirectly-covered, conf low)
- Observable: Non-minimal UED / KK-gluon: multilepton+MET and multijet+MET cascades, KK gluon resonances
- Gap: Non-minimal UED has no dedicated CMS/ATLAS search found, but it is mapped onto degenerate-SUSY searches (2110.00500); low novelty vs existing recasts and no HL-specific hook.
- Closest: arXiv:2110.00500 (theory); arXiv:2309.00586 (theory); arXiv:2603.23454 (CMS)
- HL: Generic luminosity extrapolation of SUSY-like degenerate-spectrum searches; compressed spectrum benefits from soft-lepton/track trigger.
- Reason: Indirectly covered; low priority; no positive evidence of full coverage so not rejected.

## C050: HOLD (well-covered, conf medium)
- Observable: High-mass heavy-flavour (b,s,c,tau) Drell-Yan tails, t-channel LQ/contact interactions, LFV e-mu/mu-tau/e-tau tails with b-tag
- Gap: Main directions (bbll, bsll, tautau+b, tau-b LQ) are covered and one has an ATLAS HL projection; residual niche is b-tag-categorised LFV (e-mu, e-tau, mu-tau) tails and c-tag, not found in any search.
- Closest: arXiv:2506.13565 (CMS); arXiv:2503.19836 (ATLAS); arXiv:2308.07826 (CMS); arXiv:2503.20023 (CMS); arXiv:2307.08567 (ATLAS)
- HL: Tail statistics scale with luminosity; ATLAS HL projection for b-jet final states already exists.
- Reason: Mostly covered; residual LFV+b-tag niche is thin so HOLD rather than PASS or REJECT.

## C051: PASS (one-observable-not-another, conf medium)
- Observable: Unfolded high-mass (m_ll>~150 GeV) and high-pT Drell-Yan angular coefficients A0-A7 (Lam-Tung violation, T-odd A5-A7) and A4/A_FB above Z peak, e and mu
- Gap: No CMS/ATLAS unfolded A0-A2 or T-odd A5-A7 measurement above the Z peak (m_ll>150 GeV) found; existing 13 TeV measurements are Z-peak only (2604.25678), A4 only coarse above 150 GeV (2408.07622).
- Closest: arXiv:2604.25678 (CMS); arXiv:1606.00689 (ATLAS); arXiv:1504.03512 (CMS); arXiv:2408.07622 (CMS)
- HL: High-mass tail statistics are the limiting factor; 3 ab-1 gives ~x10 events; Lam-Tung breaking operators contribute quadratically (no interference).
- Reason: Clear observable, clear gap, two pheno papers provide HL-LHC projections and motivation; needs proper experimental high-mass angular unfolding.

## C052: HOLD (one-observable-not-another, conf medium)
- Observable: Positivity-cone-aware reporting of dim-8 limits (DY moments, VBS aQGC, diphoton), EFT-truncation/clipping nuisances
- Gap: Experiments report aQGC with clipping and positivity overlays but not cone/extremal-ray limits; DY dim-8 moment positivity tests only phenomenological. This is an interpretation methodology rather than a new signature.
- Closest: arXiv:2603.18630 (ATLAS); arXiv:2510.00118 (CMS); arXiv:2410.04210 (CMS); arXiv:2604.21670 (ATLAS)
- HL: Reporting/interpretation framework; HL-LHC adds tail stats but no new detector-specific hook.
- Reason: Observable exists and partly measured; value is in reporting format; overlaps with C051 DY moments.
