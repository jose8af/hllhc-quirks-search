# Gate-2 triage batch 6 (C066-C074)

## C066 - PASS (searched-limited-regime, confidence medium)
- Observable: m(l+jet) resonance from lepton-quark fusion single-LQ production with flavour-tagged jets beyond u/b (c-tag; top/mono-top and LFV variants); Run 3 and HL-LHC extension
- Gap: Resonant single-LQ lepton+jet searches (ATLAS 2507.03650, CMS 2608.28377) cover e/mu with light and b jets only; no charm-tagged lepton+jet resonance, no top-containing/mono-top (l+b+MET) resonant LQ interpretation, no LFV (mu-tau, e-mu) lepton+jet resonance, and no CMS electron-quark result found. No experimental HL-LHC projection exists.
- Closest: arXiv:2507.03650 (ATLAS); arXiv:2608.28377 (CMS); arXiv:2308.06143 (CMS); arXiv:2308.07826 (CMS); arXiv:2606.02067 (ATLAS); arXiv:2402.16561 (ATLAS)
- Projections: arXiv:2012.11474 (theory, Haisch et al.): HL-LHC/Run III projections for b+tau, mono-top (lepton+b+MET), mono-jet resonant 3rd-gen LQ; Sec. 5.1-5.3; arXiv:2501.07543 (theory): 14 TeV 200/500 fb-1 projection for LQ->t tau pair production only (not resonant); No ATLAS/CMS HL-LHC projection for resonant lepton-PDF LQ found on INSPIRE
- HL-LHC: Resonant lepton-quark fusion is luminosity-limited at high mass/low coupling (lepton PDF tails); ATLAS conclusion points to HL-LHC; c-tag/tau triggers and high-pT lepton+jet tails benefit from 3 ab-1. No timing-detector-specific angle.
- Reason: Identifiable m(l j) observable; first-generation results exist so the 'we urge' gap of 2005.06475 has been followed up, but flavour/charge extensions (c-tag, top, LFV, e for CMS) remain open and HL-LHC projections are theory-only. Do not claim 'never studied': ATLAS/CMS core lines exist.

## C069 - PASS (proposed-not-searched, confidence medium)

- Gap: No ATLAS/CMS search found targeting LQ -> N_R + jet with SS dilepton + multijet (no LQ/RHN or LQ+SS keyword hit in INSPIRE for either collaboration); closest results are SUSY SS/3L (ATLAS 2307.01094, which excludes only part of the plane per 2603.01903) and W_R/N searches with different topology.
- Closest: arXiv:2307.01094 (ATLAS); arXiv:2006.05872 (ATLAS); arXiv:2112.03949 (CMS); arXiv:1603.02248 (CMS); arXiv:1703.03995 (CMS)
- Projections: arXiv:2603.01903 (theory): HL-LHC 14 TeV projected 2-sigma/5-sigma contours for R2~ -> N j SS dimuon + multijet (Fig. 4); not an ATLAS/CMS projection; arXiv:2412.19751 (theory): RHN production via 1st-gen LQ
- HL-LHC: Low-background LNV signature scales with luminosity; HL-LHC is the stated target of the only study; muon trigger/ID and merged-jet tagging (light N) relevant; possible displaced extension unchecked.
- Reason: Identifiable clean LNV signature, partial indirect constraints only, explicit HL-LHC relevance. Sibling seeds (mono-LQ DM: CMS 1811.10151 partly covers; LQ+VLL cascade: weak, ATLAS 2503.22581 covers VLL) are weak and not the basis for PASS. Evidence is from one theory group and absence from title/abstract-level queries, so confidence is moderate.
