# Scout note: compositeness and heavy new states

Counts: 112 papers, 16 candidate seeds, 170 logged queries. Full data in `scout_composite_heavy.json`. All IDs were returned by inspire.py; gap quotes are from arxiv_pdf.py text (columns sometimes garbled; quotes were trimmed to contiguous verbatim spans). No numbers (limits/xsecs) are quoted here.

## Method
Seminal/review searches (topcite, date<2012 and newest), cites/refs on ~10 seeds, gap-phrase `ab` queries, `arxiv_pdf.py --gaps` on ~25 papers, 20+ collaboration:CMS/ATLAS closest-search queries, forward-citation checks (roadmap 2203.13753 -> CMS 2604.10729, 2510.25874; ATLAS 2607.28381: gap partly followed up for S->bb/gamma gamma only).

## Candidate seeds (status taxonomy, confidence)
1. **Charge-5/3 and 2/3 top partners with ALP/scalar cascades: same-sign leptons plus hard photon or 6-top** (searched-limited-regime, experimental-analysis, medium). Top partners (X5/3, T2/3) can decay via a light composite pseudoscalar a into t a, with a to photons or W+W-, giving same-sign dilepton + photon or multi-top final states. The authors recast Run-2 SSL searches and say a dedicated SSL+photon search is needed.
2. **Vector-like quarks decaying to a new (pseudo)scalar S with S->WW/ZZ/gg/light-quark final states** (searched-limited-regime, experimental-analysis, medium). Roadmap paper states VLQ -> t/b + S (S sub-TeV singlet) has not been explored experimentally; since then ATLAS (diphoton) and CMS (bb, single production) appeared. Other S decay modes (gg, WW, ZZ, tautau, long-lived S) remain open.
3. **Fully hadronic exotic VLQ decays (4j+2b / 6b) at HL-LHC** (proposed-not-searched, experimental-analysis, low). Pair-produced B with exotic decays into jets and b-jets leads to a 4j+2b/6b topology absent from dedicated searches; authors estimate HL-LHC reach from a dedicated search in this channel.
4. **Same-charge (tt, uu->TT) single/pair VLQ production coupled to first generation** (proposed-not-searched, experimental-analysis, medium). If VLQs couple only to u/d quarks, PDF-enhanced same-charge pair production (uu->TT) yields same-sign VLQ pairs; the CMS 8 TeV and ATLAS lepton+jets light-quark searches target opposite-charge QCD pairs or single production. Exotic decays also allowed.
5. **Cascade decays of composite spin-1 rho resonances into heavy fermionic partners** (proposed-not-searched, phenomenology, medium). rho -> top partner + SM quark cascades give SSDL+jets final states. Authors say no dedicated searches exist and they recast; also composite resonance spin-1 (HVT/HVS) VBF production not studied.
6. **Colour-sextet (exotic-colour) composite top partners** (indirectly-covered, phenomenology, low). Hyperfermion models predict top partners in colour-sextet and octet representations with high jet multiplicity / b-rich decays. No dedicated search exists; recasts use SUSY searches. Single sextet production left for HL-LHC.
7. **Fermionic portal-matter VLQ decaying to a (long-lived) dark photon** (proposed-not-searched, phenomenology, low). VLQ -> t/q + dark photon A'; boosted A' tagged with ML or a long-lived A' decaying in the detector. Authors defer the long-lived case to future work.
8. **Type-II seesaw doubly charged scalars: compressed spectra, low-mass same-sign pairs and displaced decays** (constrained-but-region-open, experimental-analysis, medium). Compressed H++/H+ spectra (Delta m ~ O(10) GeV) produce soft same-sign leptons with low invariant mass; tiny vev leads to displaced-vertex doubly-charged scalar decays. Recent work revises exclusions and tests robustness of models.
9. **Pseudo-Dirac heavy neutral leptons: lepton-number-blind vs LNV channels and HNL oscillations at HL-LHC** (one-observable-not-another, phenomenology, medium). Symmetry-protected seesaw yields quasi-degenerate HNL pairs whose LNV amplitudes cancel; mass splitting and resolved N-Nbar oscillations in displaced vertices determine sensitivity. Searches typically assume Majorana or Dirac limits.
10. **Boosted heavy neutrinos/leptons (fat-jet with lepton sub-jet) from W_R / seesaw partners, incl. tau-flavoured N, at HL-LHC** (LHC-not-projected-to-HL, experimental-analysis, low). CMS now has WR -> tau N searches (13 TeV); type-III seesaw heavy leptons searched with Run 2 multilepton but tau-rich flavour structures and fat-jet signatures at HL-LHC remain less studied.
11. **Neutral naturalness: electroweakly charged quirks and glueball hidden-valley signatures** (theory-explored-untested, experimental-analysis, medium). Squirks/quirks with O(1) electroweak charge and masses of a few hundred GeV are unconstrained; glueball (hidden-valley) decays produce displaced or LLP signatures. Recent LLP searches target Higgs decays not quirk-specific topologies.
12. **Top-philic heavy resonances and VLQ-mediator cascades at HL-LHC (X -> t T)** (well-covered, phenomenology, low). Top-philic mediators X -> t tbar_p (VLQ partner) and four-top final states. ATLAS (2304.01678, 2609.24950) and CMS (2608.01251) top-philic searches exist; EFT/ML-optimised HL-LHC studies appear.
13. **Radion-QCD interference peak-dip in ttbar at HL-LHC (warped extra dimension)** (one-observable-not-another, phenomenology, low). Heavy scalar/KK resonances in gg -> ttbar suffer signal-background interference with peak-dip structure; finite-top-mass effects recomputed for HL-LHC. Experimental ttbar searches typically use narrow-width, no-interference templates.
14. **Higgs-compositeness and contact-interaction effects in high-mass dilepton tails at HL-LHC** (well-covered, experimental-analysis, low). CMS has a dedicated Higgs compositeness dilepton search (13+13.6 TeV) and ATLAS 13.6 TeV high-mass dilepton; an HL-LHC projection with Drell-Yan EFT tails and flavour-structured (b-tagged, tau) contact interactions is not seen.
15. **Vector-like leptons: long-lived and tau-rich decay modes and non-doublet representations** (searched-limited-regime, experimental-analysis, low). VLL searches now exist for tau/b final states, first/second generation, and LLP decays in muon system. Triplet, exotic-charge VLLs and decays to dark/ALP states not found in titles.
16. **Recasting atlas: exotic VLQ decay channels not covered by dedicated searches** (indirectly-covered, experimental-analysis, medium). Systematic recast of public ATLAS/CMS searches shows large parameter space in VLQ branching fractions (exotic decays) is only indirectly bounded. A dedicated search for the tt-rich channel is argued to reach beyond 1.5 TeV.

## Caveats
- Coverage judged from titles/abstracts of CMS/ATLAS papers only; per-seed notes list what remains UNVERIFIED.
- Several 2026 papers are very recent; forward citations are thin.
- Seeds 12-14 are weak leads (contact interactions, radion interference, top-philic) kept for breadth.
- 2608.03988 claims an inconsistency in the ATLAS H++ four-lepton cutflow: treat ATLAS DCH limits with care until checked.
