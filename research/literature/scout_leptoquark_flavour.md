# Scout note: leptoquarks / flavour structure at the LHC

JSON: scout_leptoquark_flavour.json (100 papers, 19 candidate seeds). All IDs verified through inspire.py; quotes come from arxiv_pdf.py text (two-column PDFs sometimes interleave; flagged in `location`).

## Intellectual development
- Seminal (1980s-2000s): LQ production in hadron colliders (Hewett, recid 248169), bounds (Leurer, hep-ph/9309266), pair production (hep-ph/9610408), single/pair at LHC (hep-ph/0502067). Tevatron/HERA searches were the experimental precedent.
- B-anomaly era (2013-2019): LQ for R_D(*), R_K, g-2 (1309.0301, 1511.01900, 1511.06024, 1608.08501, 1611.04930 composite, 1708.08450 U1 gauge LQ). Reviews: Dorsner et al. 1603.04993; single-LQ reviews 1808.08179, 2103.12504; footprints 2103.16558.
- Collider developments: Hunter's guide / toolbox (1706.05033, 1801.07641), high-pT vector LQ (1901.10480), Drell-Yan tails & HighPT (2207.10714, 2207.10756, 2412.14162), lepton-quark fusion (2005.06475 + 2005.06477), resonant third-gen (2012.11474), NLO pair/resonant (2108.11404, 2209.02599).
- Experimental: Run 2 pair searches (ATLAS 2006.05872, 2010.02098, 2101.11582, 2210.04517, 2401.11928 combination; CMS 2012.04178, 2402.08668), t-channel (CMS 2503.20023, 2308.07826; ATLAS 2606.02067), lepton-PDF resonant (CMS 2308.06143 tau-q; ATLAS 2507.03650; CMS 2608.28377 mu-q), LFV top (ATLAS 2403.06742, 2610.01308; CMS 2504.08532). The CMS review proceedings 2505.08738 has the coverage tables (Tables 1-4) and the sentence flagging e/mu + light/charm-jet final states as open.
- HL-LHC projections: only a 2018 CMS note (recid 1710497) and the Yellow Report (recid 1768569/1722300); no recent ATLAS/CMS LQ projections found (UNVERIFIED completeness).
