# Scout note: exotic Higgs / extended scalar sectors

Companion to `scout_higgs_scalars.json` (19 seeds, ~128 papers, 61 query groups). All claims are lead-level; IDs verified through inspire.py.

## Intellectual development
- 1980s-2000s: Higgs Hunter's Guide, 2HDM (review 1106.0034), then light CP-odd scalars in NMSSM/"ideal Higgs" (hep-ph/0005308, hep-ph/0502105), invisible Higgs (hep-ph/0009158), and displaced Higgs decays from hidden valleys (hep-ph/0605193).
- 2012-2014: after the 125 GeV discovery, the exotic-decay programme was organised (Curtin et al. 1312.4992), with LFV h->e/mu/tau (1209.1397) and light-quark/charm Yukawa ideas (1406.1722, 1503.00290, 1606.09253, 1606.09621).
- 2015-2019: naturalness-motivated (1506.06141) and EW-phase-transition-motivated (1911.10210, later 2203.08206) exotic decays; ALP EFT reinterpretation (1704.08207, 1708.00443); resonant di-Higgs/Higgs-portal (1310.6035).
- 2020-2026: experiments cover most standard channels (h->aa in 4b, 2mu2tau, 4tau, 4gamma semi-merged, Za->llgammagamma, ZH invisible, charm, LFV). Theory has moved to cascades (X->SH, H->hh'->4b), long-lived scalars/dark jets, triplet scalars with displaced decays, HHH and quartic coupling, and light-quark Yukawas via kinematics/off-shell/gamma H.

## Forward-citation checks (gap followed up?)
- 1704.08207 "no dedicated searches for h->Za->llgammagamma": followed up by ATLAS 2312.01942 and a CMS result (recid 2646011). Gap closed for gamma gamma; hadronic/tau/long-lived a with Z remains a lead.
- 2407.03015 (HHH whitepaper "hopefully ... first experimental search results"): followed by CMS 6b (2607.05145, recid 3068992) and ATLAS 6b (reinterpreted in 2606.01966). Open: other final states, resonant HHH, quartic.
- 1911.10210 (lower-mass scalars): citations include ATLAS H->4b/6b (2507.01165) but not sub-5 GeV; gap partially open.

## Dead ends / weak areas
- INSPIRE `abstract "..."` syntax returns nothing; `ab "..."` works. Gap-phrase abstract searches are sparse for this cluster; most leads came from PDF `--gaps` harvest.
- Standard exotic channels (h->aa->4b/4tau/mumutautau/4gamma, ZH invisible, doubly charged prompt multilepton, light H+ -> cs/tau nu) are well covered; included only as weak seeds.
- Rare SM few-body Higgs decays (2508.00466): not searched but SM rates (10^-11..10^-13) are far below reach; value only as recast of ALP searches.
- LFV h: Run-2 limits are mature; HL-LHC value is only via a 2025 model-specific projection (2511.19337, abstract-only read).
- Some seeds (95 GeV, CP-violating Hgg, off-shell interference, tt interference) have no verbatim gap sentence; kept for breadth.
- pdftotext column interleaving garbles some quotes (marked in the JSON); verify locations before citing.
