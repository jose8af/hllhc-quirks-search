# Scout note: LLP / unusual-signature cluster

- Pre-2012 seeds: hidden valleys (hep-ph/0604261), highly displaced Higgs vertices (hep-ph/0605193), split-SUSY long-lived gluinos (hep-ph/0408248), timing for neutral LLPs to photons (hep-ph/0407022; CDF 0804.1043).

Theory now targets HL-LHC timing (4D DV) and model-specific variants (scotogenic, neutrino-mass displaced showers, HCAL emerging photon jets, quirks/SUEP).
