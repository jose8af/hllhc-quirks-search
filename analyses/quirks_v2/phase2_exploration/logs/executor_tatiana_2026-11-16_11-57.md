## 4. What is recorded per pilot event (full hit records, STR §4.4 pt 5)
- `ev<i>` (n, 13): every measurement on the barrel counting surfaces and the BTL (`run_chain.COLS`: sid, x, y, z, s0, s1,

  every product), the parent's Geant4 crossings of the sensitive surfaces, the parent's origin vertex.
- Z(νν) events also: truth neutrino MET vector, generator tries, σ_gen, generator-level jets (SlowJet anti-kT 0.4,
  pT > 20 GeV), prompt isolated e/μ/γ candidates, and the v1 generator-level preselection weight of `gen_d123.presel`
  (as a labelled v1 quantity; the v2 MET model is applied in Phase 3 from the stored summary).
- Not recorded: any per-rung flag or count. The pilot is classified in Phase 3 (channel (i)); I compute no H.
