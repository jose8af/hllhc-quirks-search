**What fails is not the likelihood.** The T1 workspace and v1's model are the same function: asymptotic limit
828.7069 (T1, pyhf fits) against 828.7067 (v1's own code), and every toy's q̃ equals the exact one-dimensional
profile. What fails is that the frozen toy recipe does not return a stable number at this point:
1. In SR-G a b-only toy has n = B·e^{2.71θ}. When n < μS the fixed-μ fit cannot absorb the deficit and q̃ jumps to
   10³–10⁷. The fraction of such toys is Φ(ln(μS/B)/σ) = 0.4993 at μ_a: **the asymptotic limit puts the grid point
   "×1" where the b-only median sits on the jump, by construction** (the limit is where μS reaches the background
   median, 832.72). Measured: the b-only fraction on the jump at μ_a is 0.446–0.525 in the ten replicas.
2. At μ_a the CLs of a 1000-toy ensemble is therefore either exactly 0 (median on the jump: replicas 4, 5, 7) or
   0.26–0.73 (median just below it, where q_obs varies by a factor 5 for a shift of 0.03 in θ). At 1.35 μ_a it is
   exactly 0 in every replica (no s+b toy can reach a deficit of tens of σ).
3. The frozen interpolation of ln CLs then joins a finite value to the zero-count value across a step of 207 or 290
   in μ: the result is 704–717 or 930–963, neither of which is where CLs crosses 0.05.
My advance expectation (log §2.4) had this mechanism and the low branch right (≈ 700) and the high branch wrong: I
assumed CLs(μ_a) ≈ 0.05 there (→ 827–835) and so expected NOT RESOLVED. The toys gave FAIL.

## 4. The pyhf trap (for Phases 3 and 4) and the guard

Minimal reproduction [V: `p2-c-t1f-guard`, `p2f_t1.py hyporepro`]: v1-mode workspace, b-only Asimov data,
`pyhf.infer.hypotest(mu, data, model, test_stat='qtilde', return_expected=True)` with default start values returns
**CLs = 0.0 at μ = 780 and 820 without raising** (true 0.308, 0.123); at 828 it returns 0.0 with the default
tolerance and the right 0.057 with 10⁻¹⁰. The fixed-μ fit comes back **at its start point** (θ_G = 0) with
"success", 3.3×10⁵ above the minimum in twice-NLL, gradient 1.5×10⁶. Passing good `init_pars` to `hypotest` does not
cure it (0.308 right at 780, 0.0 at 820): one start vector serves all its internal fits. Cause: curvatures of 10⁶
beside 1; SLSQP's first line search gains less than its tolerance.
Guard the builder carries (`p2f_t1.py`): (a) never `hypotest` / `qmu_tilde` with default starts: `Fitter.two_fits`
with a start per fit and SLSQP tolerance 10⁻¹⁰; (b) after every fit `newton_decrement()` (jax gradient and Hessian
over free parameters not at a bound; ≈ gap to the minimum): 5×10⁻¹²–2×10⁻⁷ on converged fits, 1.8×10⁵ on the false
ones; accept below 10⁻⁴, else retry, else drop under the 2 % rule; (c) in v1 mode the exact profile
(`V1Truth.q_ref`) for every toy. (b) is tested only on this 3-parameter workspace.

## 7. Finding and proposal (for the user; nothing is applied)

**Phase 1 assumed** that the T1 toy procedure (eight μ values spaced by factors 1.33–1.39, ln-CLs interpolation,
1000 + 1000 toys) returns a limit with a toy error small against 10 %, and that v1's 789.7 is a number known to
better than 10 %. **Phase 2 found**: at v1's best point (B ≈ 6×10⁵ with a log width of 2.7) the profile statistic
has a jump exactly where the limit lies; the frozen recipe returns 704–963 (sd 114) from identical inputs and FAILS
the criterion although likelihood, fits and asymptotic limit agree with v1 to 2×10⁻⁴; v1's own number scatters by
38 between seeds. **Implications**: (1) T1-b as worded tests the grid and the interpolation at a pathological
point, not the likelihood. (2) The same recipe is what T1-i will run: wherever CLs falls to zero inside one grid
step the interpolation error is of the order of the step (25–35 %); the v2 workspaces (few events, counted rates)
have no such cliff, which T1-c (i) can show, but the recipe needs a rule for an empty tail and a refinement of the
bracket in any case.
Proposed wording, for decision with C1-b and C2-a: *"T1-b, v1 mode: (a) the asymptotic limit of the T1 workspace
agrees with v1's own asymptotic limit within 1 % [measured: 2×10⁻⁴]; (b) v1's statistic run on the builder's inputs
reproduces v1's hybrid-toy number with v1's seed, and its seed scatter is reported [measured: identical; sd 38];
(c) the limit of the T1 toy procedure is reported with its replica scatter, without a tolerance."* And for the toy
procedure of §4.11 pt 2: bracket refined by bisection until the two bracketing μ differ by less than 2 %, with a
stated rule for an empty tail. Why: (a) and (b) are the two statements about v1 that are well defined; a tolerance
on (c) compares two medians that sit on a discontinuity.
