Script: `src/s2/f200_fxfx.py E1500` (Sune; unchanged). It ran in the sim `ana` env on maxwell, nice 10.
- Inputs: the 6 npz files, taken from done markers with sha256 re-checked.

**Checks:**
- **Per-job spread.** The six per-job values have a sample standard deviation of 0.0068. The per-job error expected
  from the total is 0.00242 × √6 ≈ 0.0059. Σ(f_i − f̄)²/σ_i² ≈ 6.6 for 5 degrees of freedom, so the spread is
  consistent with statistics. Equal-weight mean 0.16355, the same as the pooled value [VERIFIED, python on the json].
- **Precision.** 1.48 % meets the target (≈ 2 %, as for E 500). The planning estimate was 1.4-1.6 % (ESTIMATE, plan).

**Readings:**
- At 1,500 GeV, FxFx (NLO 0-1j) lies 2.4 % below MLM 0-2j. This is 1.3σ in MC statistics, well inside the
  ±5.9 % band, whose largest piece is the tune (+5.4 %).
- At 500 GeV FxFx lies 1.0 % above MLM. Together the two masses show no significant FxFx-MLM difference.
- Under the frozen rule (enlarge only if the central value is outside) the band and κ are unchanged at both masses.
- For the reach: the T1 nuisance on f₂₀₀ does not grow, so this does not change the ≈ 243 GeV reach outlook.
