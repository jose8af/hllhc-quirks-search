### 6.2 E 500 shower batches: BLOCKED. The shower step crashes, so no f₂₀₀^FxFx

**Fix made** (`s2_job.py` l.670, committed): `n_lhe` counted lines that start with `<event`, but MG indents them
(`  <event npLO=…>`). n_lhe was therefore 0, and this value is passed to Pythia (`Main:numberOfEvents`) and to
`convert_hepmc --nlhe`. It now counts stripped lines.

**Smoke/timing job** `p2-b-s2-fxfx-gt` (`fxfx-e500-gt00`, 2,000 events, `--stage gen --shower --restore`, maxwell,
14:27; SLOTS line written first; node count before 2):
- Event generation from the saved grids works: 2,000 events in 45.5 s wall, 41.8 CPU-s (≈ 21 ms/event) [VERIFIED].
- `py8_fxfx` crashed: rc −11 (SIGSEGV), 4 s; the job status is `failed`.

**Reproduced by hand on maxwell** (same card, same LHE, gdb):
- The crash is in the MG5aMC_PY8_interface's DJR histograms: `MultiHist::fill` called from `main`.
- Source `HEPTools/MG5aMC_PY8_interface/MG5aMC_PY8_interface.cc`:
  - the histograms are booked for `j = 0 … nJets`, with `nJets = JetMatching:nJetMax` = 1 for FxFx (l.273, l.395);
  - they are filled at index `njetNow = matching->nMEpartons().first` (l.851-852, l.876).
  For the MC@NLO 0-1j sample njetNow can exceed nJetMax, so the fill writes out of bounds.
- The interface is the LO (MLM) driver; MG's own MC@NLO showering goes through the MCatNLO driver in the process dir.
- The HepMC write (l.797) comes before the fill, but the program dies on the first event.

**Reading:** f₂₀₀^FxFx at E 500 is **not computed**. The S2-c FxFx piece stays **NOT MET** at both masses.

**Ways forward** (each needs a build, so none is done here; decision for the orchestrator/user):
- (a) Bounds guard in the interface's DJR fill (`if (njetNow > nJets) njetNow = nJets;`, histograms only) and a
  rebuild of `MG5aMC_PY8_interface` against the installed HEPTools Pythia. This is the smallest change. It modifies
  installed software. Other FxFx problems in this LO driver are not excluded.
- (b) Shower through aMC@NLO's own `shower=PYTHIA8` path. MG compiles the MCatNLO Pythia8 driver in the process dir,
  and the FxFx/CP5/quirk settings go in `shower_card.dat`. This is MG's supported FxFx route; `s2_job.py fxfx` needs
  a new code path.
- (c) Pythia's own `main164` (the FxFx example) compiled against the HEPTools Pythia.

**Cost after the fix [ESTIMATE]:**
- Pythia time per event unknown (the job crashed at event 1). If it is like the MLM showers (≈ 21 ms/event,
  STR/FEAS_B), gen + shower is ≈ 45 ms/event.
- 17.5 % of the E 500 grids-job LHE events have negative weights (349/2000, VERIFIED with awk).
  Effective-N factor (1 − 2·0.175)² ≈ 0.42.

### 7.1 Driver (commit b8f42ed, before production)
**Code:**
- `src/s2/fxfx_shower.cc`: our program, modelled on Pythia 8.313 `examples/main164.cc`, jet-matching branch
  (`CombineMatchingInput` → JetMatchingMadgraph with `JetMatching:doFxFx = on`). No HepMC, no Rivet.
  Per matched event it writes:
  - the nominal signed weight (`weightValueByIndex`);
  - p_T, η and m of the final-state quirk pair (the `pt_pair` of `convert_hepmc.py`);
  - the hard pair p_T, n_ME, anti-kt 0.4 jets above 30 GeV, and npNLO.
- Built against the installed `HEPTools/pythia8` (`pythia8-config`; nothing installed, no installed file changed) by
  `src/s2/build_fxfx_shower.sh`: compiled in `/var/tmp/jdochoa` on maxwell (≈ 9 s), binary
  `$SW/fxfx_shower/fxfx_shower-8e6cb23ec0ccc9ec` (named by the source sha256).
- `s2_job.py fxfx --shower` refuses to run unless the binary matches the committed source. It converts the text
  output to npz with the sim `ana` env.

**Settings:** `fxfx_own_card` is exactly `fxfx_py8_card` (Edgar, `s2_job.py` l.552-571) minus three
interface-only keys. That card holds:
- the FxFx block of `main164fxfx.cmnd` (scheme 1, setMad off, coneRadius 1, etaJetMax 10, global recoil, ME
  corrections off, nJetMax 1);
- qCut 45 and qCutME = ptj 30 (`FXFX_PTJ, FXFX_QCUT`, l.552: Edgar's logged JUDGMENT, v1's MLM values reused);
- CP5, the quirk card and QED FSR (the S2-d nominal).

The frozen text fixes only "one NLO-merged (FxFx, 0–1 jet) sample at 500 GeV and one at 1,500 GeV" (STR l.2019-2020).

### 7.4 E 1500: can the grid step run as two resumable jobs? (read-only: MG 2.9.22 `amcatnlo_run_interface.py`)
**Not with MG's own driver.**
- The only restart point is after MINT step 1. `job_status.pkl` is written when step 1 ends (l.2186-2190), and
  `launch -o` skips steps 0 and 1 only (l.1926-1928, l.2015-2024).
- A fresh launch without `-o` deletes the G* channel directories (`clean_previous_results`, l.2998-3020). Step 0
  cannot be kept and step 1 restarted.

**The MG-supported split is (job A: steps 0+1 with nevents = 0, which stops after step 1, l.1934) + (job B:
`-o` event generation).**
- Job A ≈ 39 + 75 min ≈ 6,850 s (ESTIMATE): margin ≈ 350 s (5 %) against 7,200 s, no better than one job.
- Job B and the tarball take a few minutes.

**A real step-0 / step-1 split would mean running `madevent_mintMC` per channel outside MG's driver** (new code,
not done).
- With that, step 0 ≈ 2,350 s and step 1 ≈ 4,500 s (from §6.1), margins ≈ 4,850 s and ≈ 2,700 s against 7,200 s.
- The 56 step-1 channels are independent: 2-3 jobs of ≤ 30 min would also be possible.

**Recommendation:**
- If the user wants E 1500, the simplest choice is a single job at 7,200 s on an otherwise empty node.
- If the ≈ 5 % margin is not acceptable, record E 1500 as not done. E 500 shows FxFx/MLM = 1.01 ± 0.02, inside the
  band, but this is no evidence about 1,500 GeV, where the tune spread is the largest piece.
