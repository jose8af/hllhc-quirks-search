## 3. Why T1-c (iii)-(v) and (iv) were not run
The v1-count workspace (v1 factor chains at v1's best-point selection FB2&h>5&o>5 under the v2 treatment; reading R1)
has 51 parameters. Its leading zero-count term is CP_pvpair (1.03×10⁵ events per unit λ). Its counts are 3×10⁵–10⁹,
and v1's pile-up log widths act on one term (trend 5.23, density 1.15, stat 0.63–1.10). I made three remediations of
the fit guard:
1. a method ladder (SLSQP, then L-BFGS-B);
2. free fit first, then the fixed fit from the free minimum, then a μ continuation;
3. a KKT active set with projection onto the bound.
After all three, 14–19 of 80 toys are still dropped, and each 1000+1000 unit costs about 5,500 s (table in the
session log, 15:05). The b-only median q̃ is on the jump: 3.3×10⁵ at μ = 800 and 4.7×10⁴ at μ = 300. Different
starts reach minima that differ by 10²–10⁵ in twice-NLL. Test (iii) + (v) would need about 280 units, roughly
430 CPU-h, and would still be unresolved (> 2 % dropped). Brief §2: "a MUST item looks infeasible after a real
attempt" → stop and report; no redesign by me.

**Decision requested** (options, not chosen):
- (a) a different reading of "built from v1 counts" that is fit-able, for example v1's pile-up term under the v2
  treatment (s_tot widths, bounded by its no-credit value). This is a redesign after a result → advisor.
- (b) run (iii)-(v) and (iv) on the v2 placeholder workspace now, as an executable dry run of the convention's code
  on the v2 structure; well conditioned, B ≈ 350. Its unit cost is still to be measured (`p3-d-phcost` is written,
  not launched).

Recommendation: (b) plus (c).
