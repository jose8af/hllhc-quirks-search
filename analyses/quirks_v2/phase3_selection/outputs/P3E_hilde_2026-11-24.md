## 6. Open issues
- **Interaction-level seeds (not a job-seed overlap; frozen v1 scheme, for information).** Each pile-up interaction
  re-seeds Pythia with one of `rng.integers(1, 900_000_000)` (`acts/stage4/code/s4pu.py` l.142), about 200 per event.
  So about 1.2 × 10⁶ dev.pu1 interactions and 4.8 × 10⁶ prod.pu1 interactions are drawn from 9 × 10⁸ values. An
  expected ≈ 0.5 % of dev.pu1 interactions (≈ 6,000) reuse a Pythia seed that also occurs in prod.pu1. They would be the
  same minimum-bias interaction, at a different vertex in a different event (ESTIMATE, birthday count). The same holds
  for c1.pu1 vs prod.pu1. STR's "disjoint by construction" refers to the job seeds, which are disjoint. Whether this
  level matters for "must be independent of 14" is the orchestrator's call. It is not changed here: that would be a
  chain edit.
- The p3-b job scripts hard-code the c1.pu1 directory (§3). A dev.pu1 pass needs a SRC switch, in a new p3-b/p3-e job
  script or as an argument.
- Per-job Pythia MPI init costs ≈ 70 CPU-s per job (≈ 5.8 CPU-h over the campaign). A per-node shared init file, as
  c1.pu1 used, would save that. I kept the per-job choice, as in the test and in Casper's production definitions.
